# COMMON_PRAYERS_AND_BLESSINGS.md — Everyday Devotions of Elyndor

> **Canon Status:** ✅ CANONICAL — established February 2026.
> **Purpose:** A reference of prayers, blessings, and invocations that ordinary people of Elyndor genuinely use in daily life. Each reflects one or more Voices of the Divine Chorus (per D-04c) but is spoken as **ordinary speech, not ceremonial performance.**
> **Design principle:** these are things people *say*, not things they *recite*. The measure of a good entry here is whether it sounds like a real person under real conditions. Ceremony belongs in temples; these belong in kitchens, on roads, at bedsides, at graves.

---

## 1. At the Table — Meal Blessings

Spoken by whoever begins the meal — parent, host, elder, sometimes a child chosen for the honor.

- *"Warm hands to those who cooked. Warm bread to those who eat. Warm homes to us all."* — Elyra + Lyssara.
- *"For what was grown, for what was gathered, for what was shared — thanks."* — Veridan + Elyra.
- *"May this meal keep us strong enough to feed another."* — Kaelos + Lyssara. (Common in Stonefang halls.)
- *"For the fire that cooked, the hands that carried, the earth that grew — thanks."* — Kaelos + Lyssara + Veridan. (Dwarven forge-halls.)
- *"May none go hungry at this hour."* — Elyra. (Short form; used when time is short.)
- *"Bread broken is bread doubled."* — universal folk saying used as a blessing before shared meals.

---

## 2. Weddings

Marriages invoke **Elyra** (hearth) and **Lyssara** (gift), with a third Voice chosen by the couple.

- *"May your hearth be warm, your hands be full, and the world outside your door be a little kinder because you built this together."* — Elyra + Lyssara + Aurelion.
- *"Two flames make one fire. Tend it."* — Elyra + Kaelos. (Common Solari-influenced wording.)
- *"May you grow together and outward — deep roots, wide branches."* — Veridan + Elyra. (Old Ways / Mossroot.)
- *"Weigh your choices together and neither of you carries alone."* — Tyras + Elyra.
- *"May the memory of this day protect the harder days to come."* — Solenne + Aurelion.
- **Goblin wedding blessing:** *"Two hands, one contribution."* — Lyssara. Deliberately understated; goblin weddings emphasize the joining of *service*, not spectacle.

---

## 3. Funerals

All invoke **Noctis**; most pair with a second Voice reflecting the deceased's life.

- *"May your journey end in peace, and your memory endure."* — Noctis + Solenne. (The most common form.)
- *"You are not forgotten. You will not be forgotten."* — Solenne. (Whispered to the coffin.)
- *"Rest now. Your hands are open. Your work is done."* — Noctis + Lyssara. (For those who gave much.)
- *"The dawn comes for those who remain. The night is yours."* — Noctis + Aurelion.
- **Stonefang:** *"You are stone in the chamber. Your name endures."* — Noctis + Solenne.
- **Mossroot:** *"The tree that grows from you will remember what we cannot."* — Noctis + Veridan.
- **Ashfire:** *"Ashes to hearth. Life to the flame that warms the ones who come."* — Noctis + Elyra.
- **Dwarven:** *"May the mountain hold you. May the fire remember you."* — Noctis + Kaelos.
- **Veiled Order (Morvel tradition):** *"You have crossed the threshold. What follows is yours alone."* — Noctis via the Herald Morvel.

---

## 4. Before Battle — Soldier & Guard Blessings

Invoke **Kaelos** (strength to protect) and often **Tyras** (fight for what is right) or **Aurelion** (hope to return).

- *"May your arm be steady, your cause be true, your friends beside you."* — Kaelos + Tyras + Elyra.
- *"Strength that serves none serves nothing. Serve well."* — Kaelos. (Traditional Kaelos prayer; commanders often say this to units.)
- *"Come home."* — Elyra. (Whispered by families; the simplest blessing in the world.)
- *"The dawn is worth returning to."* — Aurelion. (A soldier's hope-prayer.)
- **Talos-style muttering (military tradition):** *"Do it well. Come back to do it again."* — Kaelos in the register of veterans.

---

## 5. Healers, Midwives, and Physicians

Invoke **Elyra** (compassion) and **Lyssara** (skilled service), sometimes **Veridan** (life) or **Aurelion** (hope for recovery).

- *"May my hands do what my heart intends."* — Elyra + Lyssara. (Spoken quietly before touching a patient.)
- *"What is broken can be mended. What is torn can be closed. What is sick can turn."* — Veridan + Aurelion.
- *"I do not know what will happen. I know I will not leave."* — Elyra. (For terminal cases.)
- *"May the child breathe. May the mother rest. May the house be quiet after."* — Elyra + Aurelion + Noctis. (Midwife blessing.)
- *"The pain is real. The care is also real. Both matter."* — Elyra. (Modern; common among community healers.)

---

## 6. Blacksmiths & Forge-Workers

Invoke **Kaelos** (the Unbroken Forge) primarily; often **Lyssara** (contribution through craft).

- *"Strike true. What I make will outlast me."* — Kaelos + Lyssara.
- *"The hammer is a promise. The anvil is a witness."* — Kaelos.
- *"May this tool serve better than its maker."* — Lyssara. (A humble craftsman's blessing on a finished piece.)
- *"By fire and iron, may it hold."* — Kaelos + Solaryn Herald tradition.
- **Dwarven forge-oath:** *"I mark this work. Let anyone who breaks it know my name."* — Kaelos + Lyssara + Solenne.

---

## 7. Merchants & Traders

Invoke **Tyras** (honest scale) and **Lyssara** (fair contribution); sometimes **Elyra** (hospitality to travelers).

- *"May my scale be true and my word truer."* — Tyras. (Common oath opening in market disputes.)
- *"An honest trade leaves both parties warmer."* — Elyra + Tyras.
- *"The coin returns to the hand that gave fair measure."* — Tyras + Lyssara. (Folk saying, spoken as blessing.)
- *"May the road be quiet and the customers many."* — Aurelion + Lyssara. (A caravan-master's morning blessing.)
- **Goblin merchant saying:** *"The debt remembered is the debt kept."* — Solenne + Tyras. (Stonefang traders in particular.)

---

## 8. Rangers, Hunters, Foresters

Invoke **Veridan** (life, adaptation) and sometimes **Noctis** (the death that feeds).

- *"May I take only what I need, and leave more than I take."* — Veridan + Lyssara.
- *"The forest hears. Speak accordingly."* — Veridan. (A Mossroot-style ranger's blessing.)
- *"For the life given, thanks. For the life kept, thanks."* — Veridan + Noctis. (Spoken over game or catch.)
- *"May my tracks fade before the wind finds them."* — Veridan + Zephyros Herald tradition.
- **Sylvara Herald tradition:** *"The Deep Root remembers every step. Walk worthy of that memory."* — Veridan via Sylvara.

---

## 9. Miners

Invoke **Kaelos** (the strength of stone) and **Solenne** (memory of what has been dug through). Dwarven traditions dominate here.

- *"May the stone give way. May the ceiling hold. May we come up together."* — Kaelos + Elyra.
- *"For every stone taken, a stone left standing."* — Kaelos + Tharok Herald tradition.
- *"The mountain remembers. Work as if it is watching."* — Solenne + Kaelos. (Dwarven traditional.)
- *"Down together. Up together."* — Elyra. (Short blessing at shift-start.)

---

## 10. Sailors & River-Folk

Invoke **Neressa** (Herald of the tide) and **Aurelion** (dawn returning to sea).

- *"May the tide bring us home."* — Neressa Herald tradition.
- *"Fair winds, sound hull, dry decks."* — Zephyros Herald + Kaelos + Elyra.
- *"The sea keeps what it wants. Give it what you can spare."* — Neressa. (Traditional captain's blessing before departure.)
- *"For those who did not return, we speak their names. For those returning, we set another place."* — Solenne + Elyra + Noctis. (Harbor prayer after storms.)
- **Coastal Solari prayer:** *"The tide obeys no master and forgives no fool. Sail as one who knows this."* — Neressa via Tyras.

---

## 11. Children — Bedtime Prayers

Invoke **Noctis** (rest) and **Aurelion** (dawn to come).

- *"May the night be quiet, and the morning kind."* — Noctis + Aurelion.
- *"Sleep well, small one. The Chorus watches with us."* — universal parent's blessing.
- *"The dark is not empty. The dark is where dreams live."* — Noctis. (A softer wording used to comfort scared children.)
- *"Tomorrow will come. It will be a good tomorrow, or we will make it one."* — Aurelion.
- **Goblin bedtime saying:** *"May you dream of what you will build."* — Lyssara + Aurelion.

---

## 12. Harvest & Planting

Invoke **Veridan** (growth), **Elyra** (feeding the community), **Solenne** (last year's seeds).

- *"From the earth we came, and to the earth we return the debt."* — Veridan + Lyssara.
- *"May this seed remember its parent, and its parent remember its grandparent, and so on until the memory reaches whatever first planted."* — Solenne + Veridan.
- *"For a good year, thanks. For a hard year, endurance."* — Kaelos + Veridan.
- *"The field feeds the village. The village tends the field. Neither forgets."* — Elyra + Veridan.

---

## 13. Births

Invoke **Aurelion** (new dawn), **Veridan** (life), **Elyra** (welcome).

- *"You are here now. You are ours now. Welcome."* — Elyra + Aurelion.
- *"May your first cry be answered, and every cry after."* — Elyra.
- *"You have already begun to leave the world better than you found it. Keep going."* — Lyssara + Veridan. (Goblin traditional.)
- *"A dawn to you, small one. Many dawns."* — Aurelion.

---

## 14. Seasonal Festivals

Short traditional prayers spoken at each major festival (see `DIVINE_CHORUS.md` §11).

- **First Light:** *"The night held. The dawn returned. So it will again."* — Aurelion.
- **The Forge Day:** *"Strength held this year. May it hold the next."* — Kaelos.
- **Hearth Week:** *"Every door is a threshold. Every stranger is a possibility."* — Elyra.
- **The Green Turning:** *"What sleeps returns. What returns changes. Both are life."* — Veridan.
- **The Reading:** *"We are not the first. We will not be the last. We are the ones speaking now."* — Solenne.
- **The Weighing:** *"Weigh honestly. What you refuse to name still weighs."* — Tyras.
- **The Giving:** *"An open hand is not empty. It is ready."* — Lyssara.
- **The Long Night:** *"You are remembered. You are missed. You are still ours."* — Noctis + Solenne.
- **The Silent Feast:** *"For the Voice we do not name — a place, a portion, and our unbroken attention."* — silent invocation of the Forgotten.

---

## 15. Traveler Blessings

Spoken by those who stay behind. Invoke **Aurelion** (safe return), **Kaelos** (endurance), **Elyra** (welcome home).

- *"Safe roads, kind weather, and someone waiting."* — universal.
- *"May you find good bread and better company."* — Elyra.
- *"Every path returns to a hearth. Find yours."* — Elyra + Aurelion.
- *"Come back with stories. Come back."* — universal parent's blessing.

---

## 16. Everyday Blessings (Occasion-Free)

Small blessings for the smallest moments.

- *"A dawn to you."* — Aurelion. (Common morning greeting throughout human lands.)
- *"Warm hands leave here."* — Lyssara. (Common closing when leaving a shop, home, or workplace.)
- *"May your rest be quiet."* — Noctis. (Common goodnight.)
- *"May your choices weigh true."* — Tyras. (Said before or after difficult conversations.)
- *"May you leave the world better."* — Lyssara. (Universal blessing at parting.)
- *"The Chorus is not far."* — universal. (Said to someone in pain or crisis; deliberately open — the speaker offers presence, not answers.)
