## 6. Risks

**Where future work could accidentally contradict canon:**

1. **Divine Chorus / Herald confusion.** When authoring religions, priests, or temples, do not treat Heralds *as* the Voices they served. Always cite `DIVINE_CHORUS.md`.
2. **Vampire "monsters" trope.** Do not author vampire NPCs as generic villains. Every vampire has a House Doctrine, a Measure, and (usually) a sire whose lifelong responsibility they carry.
3. **Goblin "raider" trope.** Every goblin is a contribution-oriented civilization member. Corrupted Goblins are individual tragedies, not category enemies.
4. **The Forgotten Eighth reveal temptation.** Every questline that touches theology will feel a pull to *identify* her. Never do this. Her identity is permanently open.
5. **The Chosen One trope.** No Chorus member selects heroes. No prophecy names individuals. The player is one voice among many.
6. **Age-framework backslide.** Do not use "Age of Creation / Kingdoms / Wars / Shadows" language ever. Only D-01's four Ages.
7. **Invent-first temptation.** Before creating a new named character, check `LEGACY_CHARACTER_RESERVE.md`.
8. **Overwrite-first temptation.** Before rewriting existing canon docs, check if the D-04c-style *reframing pattern* solves the problem instead.
