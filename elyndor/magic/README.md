# Magic

> A record of the arcane, divine, and natural forces that shape the world of Elyndor.

---

## Current Status

⚠️ **NOT YET DEFINED** — The magic system of Elyndor has not been formally established in canon.

What is known from the playable cast:
- Elemental magic exists — Eleanor (a human mage) demonstrates an unusually strong bond with multiple elements simultaneously ([CONFIRMED via character documentation])
- A mages guild exists and has a ranking system ([CONFIRMED via character documentation])
- Magic appears to be learnable, organized, and institutionalized to some degree

These are **game-specific observations** — the deeper nature of magic in Elyndor (its source, its limits, its history) remains ⚠️ NOT YET DEFINED.
