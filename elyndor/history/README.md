## Filing Rules

- Historical events get their own files: `<event_or_period>.md`
- Write from an in-world historian's perspective
- Clearly distinguish what is known from what is debated or mythologized
- Do not invent history beyond what has been explicitly established

---

## Notes

The historical record of Elyndor is fragmentary. The further back one looks, the less reliable the sources become. The Age of Legends is the farthest confirmed period — beyond that, the world has no reliable written history.
