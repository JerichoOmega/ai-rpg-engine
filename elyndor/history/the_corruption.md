# The Corruption

> **Canon Status:** [CONFIRMED — established July 2026]  
> **Perspective:** Written from the view of a scholar with access to ancient records. Much of what is documented here is contested, mythologized, or unknown to ordinary people of the world.

---

## The Ancient God

An ancient god was imprisoned long ago.

The details of its imprisonment — who performed the sealing, what forces were required, why it was necessary, and what the god itself is — are not known to modern scholars. The records that might have told this history belong to an era older than the Age of Legends, or they were destroyed, or they were buried with the people who wrote them.

What is known:

- The entity is not dead. It is sealed.
- The seal was not designed to last forever.
- The seal is weakening.

---

## The Weakening Prison

At the time of any campaign set in Elyndor, the god's prison has already begun to deteriorate.

The god has not broken free. It cannot act directly in the world. But its presence — its awareness, its intent, its nature — is beginning to leak through the fractures in its seal.

This leakage is the origin of what the world calls **The Corruption**.

---

## The Corruption

The Corruption is the presence of the imprisoned god bleeding into the world.

It is **not mind control**. It does not seize people and force them to commit evil acts. It does not manufacture darkness where none existed.

Instead, it **amplifies what is already there**.

### What The Corruption Does

| Domain | Effect |
|---|---|
| People | Amplifies existing flaws — fear becomes paranoia, ambition becomes ruthlessness, grief becomes despair, hatred becomes violence |
| Rulers | Corrupt tendencies accelerate into tyranny |
| Communities | Fearful villages become hostile, closed, dangerous |
| Religious | Cults receive whispers; fractured orders fracture further |
| Natural world | Forests become twisted; ecosystems collapse; wildlife grows unnaturally aggressive |
| Creatures | Ancient monsters that have slept for centuries begin to wake |
| Magic | Spells become unstable; ancient relics activate; magical disasters multiply |
| Ancient places | Ruins stir; sealed vaults crack; buried things return |

### What The Corruption Does Not Do

- It does not create evil where none existed
- It does not control individuals against their will
- It does not manufacture suffering from nothing

> **Individuals remain responsible for their choices.**  
> The Corruption does not excuse what people do under its influence. It accelerates the darkness that was already present. The cruelty was always in them. The prison just made it louder.

---

## How The World Experiences The Corruption

The vast majority of people in Elyndor do not know what is causing the world's deterioration.

They see the symptoms:
- A king who was merely ambitious has become murderous
- A forest that was once safe is now something else
- Magic is misbehaving in ways it never did before
- Old enemies are emboldened; old alliances are fraying

What they do not see is the cause.

Each faction has developed its own explanation for the world's decline. These explanations conflict with each other, fuel political disputes, and generate misinformation that the player must navigate.

The truth — that a god's prison is failing — remains buried in records too old, too damaged, or too scattered to reconstruct easily.

## The Mystery

The identity of the imprisoned god, the nature of its original crime or threat, the history of its sealing, and the full mechanics of The Corruption's spread are not yet defined.

These are intentional gaps — the story's mystery depends on them remaining unknown to the player at the campaign's start.

| Question | Status |
|---|---|
| The god's name | ⚠️ NOT YET DEFINED |
| Why it was sealed | ⚠️ NOT YET DEFINED |
| Who sealed it | ⚠️ NOT YET DEFINED |
| Whether the seal can be repaired | ⚠️ NOT YET DEFINED |
| What happens if the god fully awakens | ⚠️ NOT YET DEFINED |

These questions are the campaign's spine. Do not answer them prematurely.
