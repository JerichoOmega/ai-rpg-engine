## Filing Rules

- Each organization gets its own file: `<organization_name>.md`
- Write from an in-world perspective — how members, outsiders, or historians would describe them
- Include: purpose, structure, membership, history, reputation, and current status
- Leave unknown fields blank

---

## Notes

Organizations that exist within a single game's story and have no significance to the broader world of Elyndor belong in `docs/` (game documentation), not here. Organizations belong in the Universe Bible only when they are likely to appear across multiple games, stories, or projects — or when their history predates the current game's events.
