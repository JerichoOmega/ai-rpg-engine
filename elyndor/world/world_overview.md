# World Overview — Elyndor

> *Written as scholars of Elyndor understand their world.*

---

## Overview

Elyndor is an ancient fantasy world whose oldest civilizations have long since vanished into myth. Across the land lie forgotten ruins, mysterious relics, creatures of unknown origin, and stories whose truth has been blurred by the passage of centuries.

The precise boundaries of the world — how many continents exist, how many kingdoms have risen and fallen, what lies beyond the explored lands — are not fully known even to the world's most learned historians. What is known is preserved here. What is unknown is left open, as the world itself intends.

---

## The Nature of History in Elyndor

Much of what the people of Elyndor believe about their own past is pieced together from fragments:

- Carvings in ruins that predate written language
- Songs that have been passed from generation to generation, their original meaning contested
- Relics whose purpose scholars debate without conclusion
- Statues of figures whose names were lost when the civilizations that built them fell

The further back one looks, the less reliable the record becomes. The age before the current one is referred to in many traditions simply as the **Age of Legends** — a time when extraordinary figures walked the world and their deeds became the foundation of myth.

## Setting Philosophy

Elyndor is intentionally open-ended so it can support multiple games, novels, or other creative projects. No single story or game defines the full scope of the world. Each project contributes its own layer to the world without overwriting what came before.

> *"The ruins tell more truth than the scholars. The songs remember what the books forgot."*  
> — Common Elyndoran saying
