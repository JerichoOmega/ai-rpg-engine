# Ancient Legends

> This section documents the legendary historical figures of Elyndor's distant past.

---

## What Are the Ancient Legends?

The Ancient Legends were extraordinary individuals — heroes, warriors, scholars, rulers — whose deeds shaped the world long before the present age. Their stories survive through:

- Ancient temples built in their honor
- Ruined cities that bear their names or emblems
- Songs and folklore passed through generations
- Statues and monuments in lands they once walked
- Lost journals, relics, and recorded accounts

Whether their deeds happened exactly as told is a matter of scholarly debate. What is not debated is their impact on the world that followed.

---

## Rules for This Section

- Ancient Legends are **historical and mythological figures**, not playable game characters.
- They lived in a forgotten age — the **Age of Legends**.
- Their stories should be written as historians and scholars of Elyndor would record them.
- Do not invent details beyond what has been established. Leave sections blank for future development.
- Ancient Legends become playable characters **only if a future story explicitly establishes them as such**.
