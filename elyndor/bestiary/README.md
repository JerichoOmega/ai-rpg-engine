# Bestiary

> A record of the creatures, monsters, and beasts of Elyndor — as documented by naturalists, hunters, and scholars who have encountered or studied them.

---

## Filing Rules

- Each creature has its own file: `<creature_name>.md`
- Use `_creature_template.md` for all new entries
- Write from the perspective of an in-world naturalist or scholar
- Avoid gameplay mechanics language (use "dangerous" not "deals 40 damage")
- Leave unknown sections blank — do not invent details

## Notes
