# Companion Reactivity Standard

> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED — established July 2026.
> **Authority:** Defines the minimum reactivity every companion must exhibit. Companion identity, arcs, and relationships are canon in [`../design/companions/COMPANION_BIBLE.md`](../design/companions/COMPANION_BIBLE.md); this standard governs how companions *react to the changing world*. Dialogue should continually reinforce that the world is changing around the player.

## The Standard

Companions must visibly react to all eight of the following. "Visibly react" means at least a line of dialogue, a camp conversation, or a changed behavior — in character, through *their* values, not generic commentary.

| Trigger | What reactivity looks like |
|---|---|
| **Major story events** | Every companion has a reaction beat after each campaign-arc milestone; silence from a present companion is a bug |
| **Regional changes** | Entering a region that has escalated or recovered ([`../design/world_state/REGIONAL_ESCALATION.md`](../design/world_state/REGIONAL_ESCALATION.md)) draws comment — especially from companions with history there |
| **World State changes** | When a World State category crosses a threshold the companion would care about, they notice before the player does |
| **Player reputation** | Companions register how factions treat the party — pride, discomfort, or warning, per their own allegiances |
| **Faction relationships** | A companion whose faction the player helped or harmed responds personally, not just numerically |
| **Corruption spreading** | Each companion has an authored register for encountering the Corruption's growth — fear, anger, study, denial — consistent across the campaign |
| **Settlement recovery** | When a place the party saved visibly recovers ([`../design/VISIBLE_CONSEQUENCES.md`](../design/VISIBLE_CONSEQUENCES.md)), companions mark it; earned hope is a scarce resource — spend it on screen |
| **Companion disagreements** | Companions react to *each other's* positions, not just the player's choices ([`../design/companions/COMPANION_RELATIONSHIPS.md`](../design/companions/COMPANION_RELATIONSHIPS.md)); the player should sometimes be an audience, not an arbiter |

## Reactivity in combat (Reactive Combat)

Companion reactivity extends onto the battlefield. Under the **Reactive Combat** pillar
([`../design/REACTIVE_COMBAT.md`](../design/REACTIVE_COMBAT.md)), companions **recognize
openings and voice them in character** ("Corwin has a clear shot"; Ronan's *"Now!"*) so the
player-commander can authorize a reaction or Partner Technique for **Resolve**. These barks
follow the same craft rules below — through each companion's values, sparingly, at high
value — and, like all reactivity, they **surface options without removing player agency.**
As relationships deepen, combat coordination, animations, and available Partner Techniques
evolve to show trust.

## Rules of Craft

1. **Through their values.** The same event gets different reactions from each companion; if two reactions are interchangeable, one is wrong.
2. **Sparingly at high value.** One sharp line beats three filler barks. Do not react to everything — react *reliably to the eight triggers above*.
3. **Memory.** Reactions accumulate: a companion who objected to a choice references it when its consequences land.
4. **Reactivity is communication.** Companion dialogue is a primary channel for telling the player the world changed ([`../design/PLAYER_COMMUNICATION.md`](../design/PLAYER_COMMUNICATION.md)).

## Minimum Authored Kit (per companion)

- One reaction beat per campaign-arc milestone
- A Corruption register (their consistent stance)
- At least one region they have personal history with, and lines for its escalation and recovery
- Positions on the major factions sufficient to improvise reputation reactions in-character
- Disagreement dynamics with at least two other companions
