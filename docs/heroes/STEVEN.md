# Hero Bible — Steven

> ⚠️ **RETIRED — July 2026**  
> Steven is no longer a canonical companion in Project Dungeon Keeper.  
> He has been replaced by **Torren**, a complete redesign of the fifth companion role.  
> This is not a rename. Torren is a new character with different personality, background, philosophy, combat identity, and story arc.  
>
> **Canonical companion:** [`docs/heroes/TORREN.md`](TORREN.md)  
> **Character sheet:** [`docs/characters/torren.md`](../characters/torren.md)

---

## Why This Change Was Made

When Steven was originally designed, Project Dungeon Keeper had a much broader narrative focus. As the world, companions, religion, magic system, and Corruption evolved around the central theme — *"The greatest battle is not against darkness, but against forgetting who you are"* — Steven's identity as a silent, mysterious miner no longer contributed meaningfully to that theme.

Rather than forcing the character to fit, the role was redesigned from the ground up. The crafting and battlefield support niche is preserved and expressed through Torren.

---

## What Was Preserved

The following gameplay concepts from Steven's design carried forward into Torren:

- Crafting and equipment maintenance
- Weapon and armor improvement
- Battlefield support and construction
- The fifth companion slot as a non-standard, utility-focused role
