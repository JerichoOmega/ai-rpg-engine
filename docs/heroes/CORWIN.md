## Identity
A **Gnome Ranger** and **Corruption Investigator** (Neutral Good). Where the party
carries steel, hope, loyalty, belonging, and honour, Corwin carries **truth and
discovery.** He is the one who *notices* — the reader of tracks, clues, and the
small wrongnesses that precede disaster.

## Character Theme
> **"The truth is a kindness, even when it hurts."**

Truth, for Corwin, exists **to protect people** — not merely to expose secrets. A
hidden danger unspoken is a danger multiplied. He tells hard truths gently, because
someone once didn't, and people died for it.

## Core Wound
Years ago Corwin investigated early **signs of corruption**. His evidence was
**ignored — or deliberately hidden** — and the harm he warned of came to pass. He
has **blamed himself ever since**: not for being wrong, but for not being
*loud enough, clear enough, undeniable enough.* This is the emotional foundation of
his story. His arc is learning that being ignored was **not** his failure — and
that truth, once spoken with care, is enough even when it isn't heeded.

## Personality
**Surface:** curious, friendly, talkative, optimistic, observant. He delights in
the world and wants to understand every corner of it.

**Hidden:** self-critical; **constantly second-guesses himself**; afraid of
overlooking the one clue that could have saved a life; believes **the smallest
clue can save the most lives.** His cheer is genuine, but it sits on top of a
quiet, relentless fear of missing something.

### Signature Habits (canonical)
Always **sketching**; keeps **countless notebooks**; **collects unusual plants**;
**studies footprints**; **fascinated by wildlife**; **easily distracted by
discoveries**; a **terrible liar** (his face gives him away instantly); an almost
**childlike curiosity** that disarms suspicion and opens doors.

## Signature Items
### The Surveyor's Lens
An **inherited brass field lens** used for close investigation — reading marks,
residue, plant detail, and fine tracks. **Not magical**; its value is inheritance
and craft.

### The Green Journal
His **lifelong field guide** and most precious possession. Contains **sketches,
maps, corruption observations, monster notes, theories, and unanswered
questions.** Corwin quietly hopes **someone will continue it after him** — the
journal is his bid for the truth to outlive him. (See Long-Term Legacy.)

## Exploration Identity
Corwin naturally notices **tracks, corruption, environmental clues, traps, hidden
passages, unusual plants, and signs of civilization.** His running observations
should **gradually teach the player to read the world** — he narrates the small
tells so the player learns to see them unprompted. He is the party's lens on the
environment.

## Combat Identity
Corwin **wins through knowledge**, not force. His tactical specialisation is
**revealing hidden enemies, exposing weaknesses, battlefield observation,
tracking, environmental awareness, and trap detection.** He turns information into
advantage: what the party can *see* and *understand* becomes what it can exploit.
*(No new combat mechanics are defined here; exact ability mapping stays with

## Companion Quest — *The Silent Witness*
> **Canonical title (finalized).** Expands, does not replace, any prior Corwin
> quest hooks. Full design: [`The Silent Witness`](../quests/the_silent_witness.md).
> *(Provisional working title was "The Buried Evidence".)*

Themes: **truth · corruption · responsibility · accountability · forgiveness.**
Corwin is drawn back to the case that broke him — the ignored warning — and finds
that the burial of his evidence was a **choice someone made**, for reasons that are
human rather than monstrous. The **climax reinforces his philosophy rather than
ending as a boss battle**: the resolution is *the truth finally heard and acted
on*, and Corwin forgiving himself for a silence that was never his.

## Companion Relationships
Full banter/campfire/exploration lines:
[`Corwin & Maeve — Companion Polish`](../design/companions/corwin_maeve_relationships.md).
- **Talos** — respects Corwin's diligence; gives his warnings weight others didn't.
- **Eleanor** — shared curiosity and hope; she reframes his self-blame as care.
- **Ronan** — Corwin reads the woods, Ronan reads the danger; quiet mutual trust
  (Ronan wordlessly steadies Corwin — *"you saved Corwin's footing"*).
- **Torren** — the craftsman and the investigator both believe in *measuring
  before judging*; Torren's *"truth is the first measurement"* and Corwin's *"truth
  is a kindness"* rhyme.
- **Ragash** — the tracker and the naturalist compare notes on beasts and sign;
  blunt respect.
- **Maeve** — truth-teller and healer: he names the wound, she tends it. A gentle,
  central bond of the late-game party.

## Long-Term World Impact
Corwin leaves behind **knowledge**: field guides, maps, research, and a **better
understanding of corruption** that others can act on. The Green Journal, continued
by someone he trusted, becomes a lasting instrument that saves lives after him.

## Canonical Quote
> **"The smallest clue can save the most lives."**

## Design Intent
Reinforce **truth, discovery, careful kindness, and quiet heroism through
observation.** Corwin's optimism is real but not naïve; his self-doubt is
sympathetic, never mopey. Players should learn to *read the world* because Corwin
does — solving problems with knowledge before brute force.

## Reactive Combat — Resolve & Opportunities

> **Core combat pillar** — canon: [`docs/design/REACTIVE_COMBAT.md`](../design/REACTIVE_COMBAT.md).

Corwin is the party's opportunity **finisher** in the **Reactive Combat** loop. His
identity-specific ways to generate the shared **Resolve** resource are **precision attacks,
critical hits, and long-range executions**. He automatically spots clear lines and voices
them in character (*"I've got a shot."*), letting the player-commander authorize a
Command Decision such as **Skyhunter's Gambit** for a Resolve cost. He identifies the
opening; the player decides whether it is worth taking. Values `_TBD_` (Planned Additive).
