## Identity
**Maeve Ashwood** — the party's **Community Healer** and **battlefield physician**;
the **emotional anchor** of the group (Neutral Good). Her philosophy is
**Compassion & Hope Through Service.** She is devoted to the Voice **Elyra, The
Living Hearth** — *"every act of unforced hospitality is an act of Elyra"* — which
makes her healing a form of quiet worship.

## Character Theme
> **"Hope is something you practice."**

Hope is not a speech or a miracle. It is **built through small daily acts** — a
cup of tea, a mended sleeve, a wound checked before it's mentioned. Maeve *does*
hope, deliberately, every day.

## Core Philosophy
> **"People rarely remember the medicine. They remember the person who stayed."**

## Core Wound
During the war Maeve had **enough medicine to save only one of two critically
wounded patients.** One survived. One died. **She remembers both names** — she
keeps them **written inside her journal.** The wound is not the choice itself but
the belief that she must *always* be enough, alone, or someone dies. Her arc is
learning to **let others carry her sometimes.**

## Personality
**Surface:** gentle, patient, motherly, calm, warm — the person a frightened
stranger instinctively trusts.

**Hidden:** carries **tremendous grief**; **rarely forgives herself**; **pushes
herself too hard**; **finds it difficult to accept help.** Her calm is a discipline
she practises, not an absence of pain.

### Signature Habits (canonical)
Makes **tea every evening**; quietly **repairs clothing**; **replaces empty
bandages**; **checks everyone's health without being asked**; **leaves supplies
anonymously**; **watches over companions while they sleep**; **notices unmet needs
before anyone speaks.** The party **gradually realises she quietly holds everyone
together.**

## Signature Items
### The Lantern of Ashes
A **simple brass lantern** — **not magical**. She **lights it wherever hope must
be rebuilt**: a ruined village, a grieving camp, a long dark watch. Over the
journey it becomes **her defining symbol** and a recognisable **nighttime sign of
safety** — where the lantern burns, someone stayed.

### The Field Journal
Contains **patient notes, herbal remedies, pressed flowers, children's drawings,
letters, and memories of those she lost** (including the two names from her Core
Wound). Crucially, she records **how people lived — not how they died.** The
journal is an act of remembrance as care.

## Exploration Identity
Entering settlements, Maeve naturally **checks on the sick, helps children, visits
local healers, gathers herbs, assists elders, and leaves medicine anonymously.**
**NPCs should recognise her reputation** — doors open for her, and people she once
helped remember her before she remembers them.

## Combat Identity
Maeve specialises in **battlefield medicine, healing, protection, morale, civilian
rescue, and preventing deaths.** She is **not simply "the healer"** — she is the
**emotional anchor**: the party fights differently, and better, because she is
there and because keeping people alive is the point. *(No new combat mechanics are
defined here; mapping stays with [`docs/COMBAT_SYSTEM.md`](../COMBAT_SYSTEM.md);
specific kit `_TBD_`.)*

## Companion Quest — *A Light in the Ashes*
> Preserves the **existing recruitment structure**: Maeve is recruited **through**
> this quest and **refuses to leave the refugee settlement until it can stand on
> its own.** Expanded, not replaced. Full design:
> [`A Light in the Ashes`](../quests/a_light_in_the_ashes.md).

Themes: **grief · compassion · healing · rebuilding · remembrance · hope.** The
quest grows from a recruitment beat into a story about **rebuilding hope, identity,
and community** in a place the war hollowed out. The **climax emphasises
restoration rather than vengeance** — the win is a community that can care for
itself, and Maeve finally believing she is allowed to leave because she is no
longer the only thing holding it up.

## Companion Relationships
Full banter/campfire/settlement lines:
[`Corwin & Maeve — Companion Polish`](../design/companions/corwin_maeve_relationships.md).
- **Talos** — two people who carry the cost so others don't; she tends the wounds
  his duty leaves on him.
- **Eleanor** — hope in two registers: Eleanor's radiant optimism and Maeve's
  practised, hands-on kind; they steady each other.
- **Ronan** — she is one of the few who names his real fear (*not* becoming a
  monster, but **losing people**) and never flinches from him.
- **Torren** — the smith mends things, the healer mends people; warm, easy respect
  and terrible shared jokes over evening tea.
- **Corwin** — he names the wound, she tends it; a gentle central bond.
- **Ragash** — blunt loyalty meets quiet care; Maeve tends the hounds without being
  asked, and Ragash never forgets it.

## Long-Term World Impact
Maeve leaves behind **healthier communities, clinics, traditions, trained healers,
and people who learned to care for one another.** Her legacy is not a monument but
a *practice* that continues in the places she passed through.

## Canonical Quote
> **"People rarely remember the medicine. They remember the person who stayed."**

## Design Intent
Reinforce **compassion, hope through service, remembrance, and quiet strength.**
Maeve's grief must never tip into martyrdom-as-spectacle; her power is that she
keeps choosing to stay and to hope. Players should succeed in her scenes by
**preserving life**, not maximising damage.
