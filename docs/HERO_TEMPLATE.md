# Hero Bible Template

> **Usage:** Copy this file to `docs/heroes/<HERONAME>.md` and fill in every section.  
> Do not leave sections blank or marked "TBD" — if something is not yet known, mark it ⚠️ **NOT YET DEFINED** and note what creative direction is needed.  
> Every section must connect back to the Core Wound. If a section feels arbitrary, the Core Wound may need refinement.

---

## Hero Summary

*One paragraph. Who is this person? What is the essential truth about them that a player will eventually understand? Write it as if you already know the full arc.*

---

## Core Identity

*Complete this sentence: "[Hero] is not a [class label] because [obvious reason]. They became [class label] because [the real, specific, human reason]."*

*Everything about their gameplay should reinforce this.*

---

## Core Wound

*What happened to them — or what did they come to believe — that shaped who they became?*

*Be specific. Not "she was betrayed" but "she trusted someone completely and was used because of it, and now she cannot tell kindness from manipulation."*

*The Core Wound is the lens through which they see the world. It is not the character. It is the scar.*

---

## Core Belief

*What truth do they currently hold about the world — a truth formed by their wound?*

*Write it as a statement they would make if asked directly. It may be partially wrong. It may be partially right. It should be something another character could challenge.*

---

## Greatest Fear

*Not a generic fear. The specific thing they cannot bear to lose or fail at.*

*It should connect directly to the Core Wound.*

---

## Driving Goal

*Why do they join the adventure? What are they moving toward — or running from?*

*This should be concrete and personal, not abstract.*

---

## Personality

### Public Personality

*How they present to people who have not earned trust. List 3–5 traits with brief descriptions.*

### Private Personality

*How they are with people they trust. List 3–5 traits. Should contrast meaningfully with the public face.*

### The Contrast

*What is the gap between their public and private faces, and why does it exist? The contrast is often the most interesting thing about them.*

---

## Moral Compass

*What do they value, and in what order? Whose wellbeing do they prioritize?*

*Where would they draw a line? What would they refuse to do regardless of orders?*

---

## Visual Identity

*Height, build, notable physical features, scars, age impression.*

### Equipment & Clothing

### Recognizable Personal Detail
*One meaningful item, marking, or habit that appears on the model and tells a story. (Example: Talos's bead bracelet.)*

### Silhouette
*Describe the silhouette in one sentence. A player should recognize them from across the battlefield.*

## Combat Identity

*How does their personality translate into how they fight?*

*Describe their role in terms of what they do for the party — not in terms of stat bonuses.*

*The quest is not about defeating a villain. What emotional wound does it address, and how might it heal — or fail to heal?*
