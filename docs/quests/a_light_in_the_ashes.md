## Core Theme — *Compassion & Hope Through Service*
Themes: **grief · compassion · healing · rebuilding · remembrance · hope.**
Maeve's philosophy: *"Hope is something you practice."*

## Premise
A **war-hollowed refugee settlement** is barely holding on. Maeve Ashwood — its
**Community Healer** and battlefield physician — is the reason it survives at all.
She will not abandon it. The party's task is not to *extract* her but to help the
settlement reach the point where **it no longer needs her to survive.**

## Structure
### Act I — The Person Who Stayed
The party arrives to find Maeve holding a community together through **small daily
acts** (her signature habits): tea, bandages, checking on the sick, the **Lantern
of Ashes** lit against the dark. Recruitment is gated: *she refuses to leave until
the settlement can stand on its own.*

### Act II — Rebuilding
The work is **restoration**: shoring up food, safety, medicine, and morale;
training locals to care for one another; reviving traditions the war interrupted.
Maeve's **Core Wound** surfaces — the two patients, the two names in her journal —
and her difficulty **accepting help.** The party learns that hope here is *built,
not declared.*

### Act III — Standing on Its Own
The settlement passes the threshold: it can care for itself. Maeve can finally
leave — not because the need ended, but because **she is no longer the only thing
holding it up.** She learns to let others carry her sometimes.

## Climax — restoration, not vengeance
The climax **emphasises restoration rather than vengeance.** Whatever threatened
the settlement, the *win is a community that endures and cares for itself* — a
light kept burning — not a punished enemy. Any combat protects and rescues rather
than destroys. Objective/mechanics implementation: `_TBD_`.

## Combat Philosophy (encounter design)
Maeve's encounters **teach protection, civilian rescue, battlefield medicine,
triage, morale, and keeping hope alive under pressure.** Players should **succeed
by preserving life rather than maximising damage** — every civilian saved matters.

settlement should persist as a place her influence lingers (clinics, trained
healers, traditions) — see [Long-Term World Impact](../heroes/MAEVE.md#long-term-world-impact).
