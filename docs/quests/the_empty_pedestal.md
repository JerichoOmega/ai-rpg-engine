## Theme
**Truth versus oath — what matters more: keeping an oath, or saving lives?**

The quest is **NOT about recovering a relic.** It is about **uncovering historical
truth.** The central question runs through every beat: *when duty and life demand
opposite things, which is the higher obligation — and who gets to decide?*

## Premise
Torren believes that **recovering the lost relic will restore Clan Ironhall's
honour.** He carries the **Founder's Compass** (its final space still blank) and
the **Ironhall Master's Seal** (wrapped in cloth, unused — he does not believe he
has earned the right). As the quest progresses he discovers the relic was never
the true issue: **the clan's history was incomplete.**

## The Ancestor — Durgan Ironhall
**Durgan Ironhall**, Torren's ancestor and former **Lord Warden of the Vault**,
swore an unbreakable oath to guard the relic. During a catastrophic disaster he
faced an impossible choice:

- **Protect the relic**, or
- **Save the kingdom's people.**

**He chose the people.** History remembered only that he *broke his oath*, and
his name — and the clan's — was marked with disgrace in the **Hall of Names**.

## Major Twist (Canonical)
Near the end of the quest, Torren discovers a **sealed letter** bearing the
**High King's seal** — never opened. The High King's final order states:

> *If the choice ever becomes impossible… save the people.*

The King **explicitly released Durgan from his oath.** But:

- The **High King died before publicly revealing the order.**
- **Durgan accepted disgrace** rather than create political instability by
  producing a dead king's private command.

**Therefore:** Durgan **obeyed** his King. The council judged on **incomplete
information.** **No major historical figure becomes a villain.** The tragedy comes
from **incomplete history, not malice.**

> **Non-negotiable narrative rule:** preserve the "no villain" reading. Durgan,
> the council, and the King are all acting with honour under impossible
> constraints. Sympathy belongs to everyone.

## Structure — arc shape
1. **The Blank Space** — Torren resolves to restore the clan's honour by
   recovering the lost relic; the party sets out for the Iron Peaks.
2. **Return to Clan Ironhall** — arrival at the deep-hall; the weight of the
   disgrace carved in the Hall of Names; Borik's skepticism; Odrin's gruff pride.
3. **The Historian's Doubt** — Elder Hilda reveals that the records don't add up;
   the search shifts from *relic* to *truth*.
4. **Following Durgan** — retracing the Lord Warden of the Vault, the disaster,
   and the impossible choice.
5. **The Empty Pedestal** — the party reaches the pedestal itself and learns the
   relic was never the point.
6. **The Sealed Letter** — the twist: the High King's unopened final order that
   released Durgan from his oath.
7. **Bearing the Truth** — Torren must decide how to reveal a truth that
   overturns generations of belief; Garrik grounds him; Brynja comes around.
8. **Honour Restored** — **High Thane Borik personally removes the mark of
   disgrace** carved across Clan Ironhall's name in the Hall of Names.
9. **The Silent Forge** — Torren returns to the long-cold forge and completes an
   **ordinary piece of craftsmanship**; for the first time in generations he
   **stamps it with the Ironhall Master's Seal.**
10. **The Compass** — later, Torren **engraves his own name inside the Founder's
    Compass**, filling the blank space: Clan Ironhall's lineage restored.

## Canonical ending (preserve)
- The **mark of disgrace is removed** from Clan Ironhall's name by High Thane
  Borik, in person.
- Torren **relights the Silent Forge** with an ordinary, honest piece of work and
  **finally uses the Ironhall Master's Seal** — the right he believed he had not
  yet earned.
- Torren **engraves his own name inside the Founder's Compass**, symbolising that
  **Clan Ironhall's lineage has been restored.**

This ending is canonical and must be preserved in future writing.

## Canon reconciliation
- This quest **authors Torren's companion arc** around **clan honour and
  historical truth**. It is **complementary to — not a replacement for** — his
  intimate personal Core Wound (the **Cold Clasp**, "something I couldn't save",
  [`TORREN.md`](../heroes/TORREN.md)). Writers may let the two resonate (both are
  about refusing to give up on something and the cost of judgment made on
  incomplete truth) without collapsing one into the other.
