## Naming philosophy
Companion-quest titles are **symbolic and multivalent** — a short phrase that names
the character's wound, their object of meaning, and their arc at once (e.g. *The
Empty Pedestal*, *A Light in the Ashes*, *The Silent Witness*). Maintain this style
for future companion quests; provisional/working titles are allowed but should be
finalized before they harden into canon.

## Quest capsule summaries
- **[The Broken Oath](the_broken_oath.md)** — Ragash, excommunicated from the
  Bloodhorn Clan for warning against a wrong, confronts loyalty vs. conscience.
- **[The Lost Howl](the_lost_howl.md)** — Ronan finds the Hidden Pack; the climax
  saves the Lost Wolf (Bram) instead of slaying it. *What makes a family?*
- **[The Empty Pedestal](the_empty_pedestal.md)** — Torren and the legacy of Clan
  Ironhall; *"truth is the first measurement."*
- **[The Silent Witness](the_silent_witness.md)** — Corwin reopens the corruption
  case that broke him; the climax is truth finally heard, not a boss fight.
- **[A Light in the Ashes](a_light_in_the_ashes.md)** — Maeve Ashwood's recruitment
  and the rebuilding of a refugee settlement until it can stand on its own.
- **Eleanor — *The Weight of What We Build*** — justice, power, and responsibility
  through the Mages Guild ([`ELEANOR.md`](../heroes/ELEANOR.md)).
- **Talos — Personal Quest** — duty, refusal, and accountability (Vellmar's Ford);
  canonical title `_TBD_` ([`TALOS.md`](../heroes/TALOS.md)).
