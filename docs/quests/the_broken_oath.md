## Theme
**Conviction — the conflict between duty and conscience.**
*What do you do when duty and conscience demand different things?*

## Premise
Ragash was **excommunicated from the Bloodhorn Clan** after she **warned an
innocent frontier settlement** of an impending Bloodhorn **punitive raid**. She
**knowingly violated a ruling of the Circle of Elders** because she believed
innocent lives deserved protection. She **accepts responsibility** for breaking
her oath — and she **does not regret** saving the innocent.

## Core themes (guide dialogue, writing, animation, cinematics)
Loyalty · conviction · belonging · chosen family · responsibility · **strength
through compassion.**

## ⚠️ Important narrative rule (non-negotiable)
- **The Bloodhorn are NOT villains.**
- **Ragash is NOT portrayed as perfect.**
- **Both sides have understandable motivations; the player should sympathise with
  both.**
The clan defends its law, its territory, and the weight of oaths; Ragash defends
innocent life over obedience. The tragedy is that both are honourable.

## Structure — major acts
1. **Return to Bloodhorn territory** — Ragash resolves to go back; the party
   crosses into the mountains.
2. **Border ambush** — first contact; tension, not clean villainy.
3. **Arrival at Bloodhorn Hold** — reunion, judgement, the weight of the exile.
4. **Night raid** — a crisis strikes the Hold / the region.
5. **Rescue operations** — save endangered innocents / clanfolk / hounds.
6. **Hold the mountain pass** — a defensive stand at a chokepoint.
7. **Siege of Bloodhorn Hold** — the central large-scale confrontation.
8. **Champion's Duel** — an honour-bound single combat that can decide the outcome.
9. **Resolution and reconciliation** — consequences settle; honour is weighed.

(Encounter design for the combat beats should follow the gold-standard principles

## Canonical ending (preserve)
- The **High Chieftain offers Ragash her Bloodhorn insignia back.**
- **Ragash respectfully declines to return permanently** — she has found a **new
  family with Talos and her companions.**
- She **remains Bloodhorn in spirit**; she simply **walks a different path.**

This ending is canonical and must be preserved in future writing.

## Notes
- Ties directly to Ragash's core belief, *"You earn belonging. It is not given"*
  (`docs/heroes/RAGASH.md`) and her chosen-family arc.
- Bloodhorn military culture (horn calls, Houndmasters, Hold defences) informs
  the siege/pass beats — see [`Bloodhorn Clan`](../world/factions/bloodhorn_clan.md).
