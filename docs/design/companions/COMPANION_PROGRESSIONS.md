# Companion Progressions

> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED — 2026-07-31.  
> **Authority:** This document defines the canonical progression philosophy and the two confirmed specialization paths for each of the six confirmed companions. These paths are fixed. Companions never change class. Players customize how a companion performs their role, not who that companion is.  
> **Cross-references:** [`COMPANION_BIBLE.md`](COMPANION_BIBLE.md) · [`docs/heroes/`](../../heroes/)

---

## Progression Philosophy

Each companion possesses one fixed Combat Identity.

**Players customize how that companion performs their role — not who they are.**

Companions never change classes. A Construct Engineer does not become a Healer. A Frontline Defender does not become a Rogue. The class is a fundamental expression of the companion's identity, not a container players can swap.

Instead, every companion chooses one of **two permanent specialization paths**.

These paths represent differing philosophies of the same Combat Identity — two ways of being the thing they already are. They reflect how the companion has grown, not what they have become.

The choice is permanent. It reflects a character moment, not a menu decision. The player's relationship with the companion, and the companion's own arc, should make one path feel more narratively true by the time the choice arrives.

## Canonical Companion Progressions

---

### Eleanor

**Combat Identity:** Elemental Mage  
**Expedition Identity:** Magical scholar; arcane anomaly identification; magical reagent preservation  
**Hero Bible:** [`docs/heroes/ELEANOR.md`](../../heroes/ELEANOR.md)

---

#### Path One — Elemental Savant

*The power is the point. Eleanor pursues the upper limits of what elemental combination can do.*

Focus:
- Raw magical power
- Larger and more complex elemental combinations
- High single-target and area damage
- Area-of-effect destruction

This path represents Eleanor leaning into the sheer force of what her Harmonic Soul can do — treating her gift as something to be expanded and explored to its furthest edges.

---

#### Path Two — Spellweaver

*Control over chaos. Eleanor turns the battlefield itself into an instrument.*

Focus:
- Battlefield control
- Magical terrain manipulation
- Wards and protective fields
- Positioning and sight-line manipulation
- Tactical support

This path represents Eleanor developing precision over power — learning to use her gift surgically rather than powerfully, shaping the conditions of a fight rather than ending it by force.

---

### Torren

**Combat Identity:** Construct Engineer  
**Expedition Identity:** Master blacksmith; salvage expert; engineering and structural knowledge; equipment maintenance  
**Hero Bible:** [`docs/heroes/TORREN.md`](../../heroes/TORREN.md)

---

#### Path One — Master Smith

*The forge is an extension of himself. Torren fights with tools he built, and nobody builds better.*

Focus:
- Enhanced personal combat capability
- Forged equipment and weapon craftsmanship
- Heavy hammer mastery
- Self-sufficiency in battle

This path represents Torren as a craftsman-warrior — someone who wins fights through the quality of what he has made and his personal command of it.

---

#### Path Two — Siege Engineer

*The battlefield is a problem to be solved. Torren solves it at scale.*

Focus:
- Advanced constructs and deployable mechanical support
- Battlefield control through engineered positions and obstacles
- Larger deployable creations
- Sustained attrition and area denial

This path represents Torren as an architect of battle — someone who wins fights by designing the conditions under which winning is inevitable before the first blow is struck.

---

### Talos

**Combat Identity:** Frontline Defender  
**Expedition Identity:** Structural and fortification knowledge; defensive planning; hazard recognition; safe route identification  
**Hero Bible:** [`docs/heroes/TALOS.md`](../../heroes/TALOS.md)

---

#### Path One — Guardian

*Equipment: Sword and Shield. The line does not break.*

Focus:
- Ally protection
- Defensive techniques and reactive combat
- Battlefield control through positioning and draw
- Absorbing punishment so others don't have to

This path represents the soldier Talos was trained to be — someone whose instinct is to stand between the party and what is coming.

---

#### Path Two — Champion

*Equipment: Greatsword. The threat ends before it reaches the party.*

Focus:
- Aggressive offense
- High damage output
- Enemy disruption and destabilization
- Ending fights quickly

This path represents a different expression of the same core value — protection through overwhelming force rather than resilient defense. Talos keeps the party safe by ensuring threats cannot sustain their assault.

Both paths are philosophically consistent with who Talos is. They represent different answers to the same question: *how do you protect the people beside you?*

---

### The Ranger *(name TBD)*

**Combat Identity:** Ranger  
**Expedition Identity:** Hunter; scout; forager; tracker; pathfinder; danger warning

---

#### Path One — Archer

*Distance is an advantage. The Ranger fights from positions the enemy cannot reach.*

Focus:
- Precision and accuracy
- Long-range combat
- Critical strikes and targeted shots
- Mobility and positioning

---

#### Path Two — Crossbow Specialist

*Impact over elegance. The Crossbow Specialist treats every shot as a tactical decision.*

Focus:
- Armor penetration
- Tactical shot selection (legs, shields, weapon hands)
- Heavy impact and stagger
- Battlefield control through targeted disruption

Outside combat, both paths retain the full Expedition Identity:
- Finds food and hunts wildlife
- Locates herbs and foraging opportunities
- Detects hidden paths and alternative routes
- Tracks creatures for the party
- Warns of danger ahead during travel

---

### Ragash

**Combat Identity:** Orc Houndmaster  
**Expedition Identity:** Her hounds extend her scouting range and tracking capability beyond any single companion  
**Hero Bible:** [`docs/heroes/RAGASH.md`](../../heroes/RAGASH.md)

---

#### Path One — Beastmaster

*The pack is everything. Ragash builds something greater than herself.*

Focus:
- Stronger, more capable companion hounds
- Pack coordination and combined action
- Hound utility (scouting, item retrieval, area denial)
- Defensive support and hound resilience

This path represents Ragash investing her identity in the pack — her strength is the sum of theirs.

---

#### Path Two — Huntmaster

*Ragash and her hounds hunt together as one coordinated strike.*

Focus:
- Ragash's personal combat capability
- Coordinated strikes between Ragash and her hounds
- Bleeding attacks and sustained damage
- High mobility and pursuit

This path represents Ragash as the apex of the pack — her hounds extend her, but she is the point of the spear.

---

### Ronan

**Combat Identity:** Werewolf Warrior  
**Expedition Identity:** His curse gives him perceptions and capabilities unavailable to any other companion  
**Hero Bible:** [`docs/heroes/RONAN.md`](../../heroes/RONAN.md)

---

#### Path One — Guardian

*Controlled transformation. Ronan treats his curse as a responsibility.*

Focus:
- Controlled and deliberate transformation
- Defensive combat
- Ally protection
- Reliable frontline presence

This path represents Ronan choosing discipline over power — learning to hold the transformation at the edge of useful without losing himself in it.

---

#### Path Two — Predator

*Aggressive transformation. Ronan accepts what he is capable of.*

Focus:
- Aggressive and extended transformations
- High mobility and pursuit
- Bleeding attacks and burst damage
- Attrition through relentless pressure

This path represents Ronan making peace with the predator — not celebrating it, but accepting that the power is real and that using it is not the same as becoming it.

**Both paths represent different methods of living with his curse rather than opposing moral alignments.** The Guardian and the Predator are equally valid responses to what Ronan is. Neither is the "good" path. Neither is the "dark side." They are two honest answers to the same impossible question.
