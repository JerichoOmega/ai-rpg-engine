# Journey Conversations

> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED — 2026-07-31.  
> **Authority:** This document defines how companion relationships develop during travel. There is no traditional camp-based relationship system. Journey Conversations are the canonical mechanism for companion growth, trust-building, and personal quest unlocks. All companion dialogue design must align with this framework.  
> **Cross-references:** [`COMPANION_BIBLE.md`](COMPANION_BIBLE.md) · [`COMPANION_RELATIONSHIPS.md`](COMPANION_RELATIONSHIPS.md) · [`docs/systems/journey_system.md`](../../systems/journey_system.md)

## The Core Principle

**There is no traditional camp system.**

Companion development occurs while traveling.

The game does not ask players to return to a hub between missions to talk to companions. Relationships are not built at a static location between adventures. Companions converse while the player continues moving — **travel itself is the primary location for character growth**.

Players should gradually feel that weeks and months are passing as the companions travel together. By the end of the game, they should be able to look back at how the relationships began and recognize the distance they have traveled — not just across the map, but as people.

---

## When Journey Conversations Trigger

Journey Conversations trigger naturally based on:

| Trigger Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Region** | Entering the Great Forest for the first time; crossing into the Capital Province |
| **Story progress** | Completing a major quest; uncovering a significant piece of lore; a faction event resolving |
| **Companion combinations** | Two companions with a defined relationship dynamic are both active |
| **Previous conversations** | A prior conversation raised a question that now has an answer |
| **Trust level** | A companion's trust with the player has crossed a threshold |
| **Major events** | A significant choice was made; a companion was nearly killed; something changed |

Conversations do not interrupt gameplay. They layer over travel — companions speak while the party moves. The world continues; the characters talk.

---

## Scheduling and Pacing

An invisible interaction scheduler governs Journey Conversation frequency. Its purpose is to prevent:

- **Conversation stacking** — two emotionally significant conversations in rapid succession
- **Tonal whiplash** — a humorous banter exchange immediately after a devastating story moment
- **Character saturation** — hearing from the same companion too frequently in a short span

The scheduler maintains emotional pacing across the journey. A great conversation should feel like something that emerged naturally from where the party has been, not like content being dispensed at intervals.

### Conversation Priority Tiers

When multiple conversations are eligible to trigger, the scheduler uses priority tiers:

1. **Main Story** — conversations directly connected to a just-completed or just-discovered story beat
2. **Companion Quest** — conversations that unlock, advance, or resolve a personal quest
3. **Significant Emotional** — responses to events that affected a companion personally
4. **Character Development** — reveals, confessions, relationship milestones
5. **Casual and Humor** — banter, observations, companions being themselves
6. **Ambient** — background color; what the companions notice about the world around them

Higher-priority conversations do not always trigger immediately. The scheduler may delay them to let lower-priority ambient moments breathe first, so that significant conversations feel earned by their context.

## What Journey Conversations Accomplish

Journey Conversations exist to:

- **Build companion relationships** — both player↔companion and companion↔companion
- **Reveal character history** — not through exposition, but through the things a companion chooses to say on a specific road at a specific moment
- **Unlock personal quests** — the threshold for a personal quest is always trust, not a menu selection
- **Create companion banter** — the texture of people who have spent real time together
- **Develop romances** (where applicable) — through accumulated proximity and specific moments, not through a dialogue approval threshold

---

## Relationship Memory

Journey Conversations carry **Dynamic Dialogue Memory**. Past interactions are referenced in future dialogue.

A companion who once told the player something personal will expect, at some later point, that the player remembers it. A companion who received an answer they found inadequate will return to the question later — differently, from a different angle. A companion who was trusted with something will treat that trust as real.

The conversations are not isolated. They are a continuous record of the relationship.

---

## Trust as the Primary Currency

Trust is the currency that Journey Conversations earn and spend.

Trust accumulates through:
- Shared decisions that align with a companion's values
- Journey Conversations that the companion found meaningful
- Personal quest advancement
- The player demonstrating they have paid attention to who the companion is

Trust unlocks:
- Higher-tier Journey Conversations
- Personal quest access
- Companion interventions during Corruption moments
- Romance content (where applicable)
- The companion's most guarded revelations

**Approval and trust are not the same thing.** A companion can approve of a decision and still not trust the player. A companion can occasionally disapprove of a decision and still trust deeply. Trust is the longer arc.

---

## Companion Interventions

At sufficient trust depth, a companion can intervene during **critical Corruption moments** — moments where the player is at risk of making a choice shaped by Corruption rather than genuine intention.

An intervention is not a lecture. It is a companion noticing something specific about the player's behavior and saying something specific about it. The intervention is available because the relationship has been built; a companion who doesn't know the player cannot intervene meaningfully.

Interventions are one of the most significant mechanical rewards for investing in companion relationships. They can change the outcome of major decisions.

---

## Relationship Evolution Through Three Phases

Companion relationships move through three broad phases over the course of the game:

**Strangers** *(early game)*  
Companions are professionals sharing a mission. They are not unfriendly — but they are not yet friends. Conversations are observational, practical, occasionally revealing. The relationships are just beginning.

**Trust** *(mid game)*  
The companions have been through things together. Friendships are forming, rivalries are clarifying, histories are being revealed. Conversations become more personal. The party is becoming something.

**Family** *(late game)*  
The companions know each other. Conversations reference the full history of the relationship. The party has developed the particular shorthand of people who have spent a long time together. Banter is specific; emotional conversations have depth because they are built on years of smaller ones.

The transition between phases is not declared. It is felt.
