## Core Rule — Read This First

> **Companions are people, not combat units.**

Every design decision about a companion — their combat role, their dialogue, their approval triggers, their specialization paths — flows from who they are as a person. Mechanics grow from character. Character is never invented to serve a mechanical slot.

If a proposed companion can be summarized entirely by their combat role, they have not been designed yet.
