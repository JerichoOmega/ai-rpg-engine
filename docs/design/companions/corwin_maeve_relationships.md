## Corwin

### Camp behaviours (recurring)
Sketches wildlife; updates and reorganises his journals; catalogues the day's
findings; asks the party endless questions; studies strange discoveries by
firelight until someone gently tells him to sleep.

### Exploration behaviours
Narrates the small tells — *"see how the moss only died on this side?"* — so the
player learns to read tracks, corruption, traps, hidden passages, unusual plants,
and signs of civilization. His curiosity opens doors his caution would never knock
on.

### Relationship banter
- **Talos:** "You actually read my report." / *Talos:* "You were right. I don't
  ignore people who are right."
- **Eleanor:** *Eleanor:* "You blame yourself for what other people didn't hear."
  / **Corwin:** "…I hadn't thought of it that way."
- **Ronan:** (wordless — Ronan steadies Corwin at a ledge; Corwin just nods and
  writes it down: *someone who watches the edges.*)
- **Torren:** *Torren:* "Measure the stone before you shape it." / **Corwin:** "The
  truth is a kindness, even when it hurts." / *Torren:* "…same church, different
  pew, lad."
- **Ragash:** *Ragash:* "You draw the beast that nearly ate you." / **Corwin:**
  "So the next person knows it's coming."
- **Maeve:** **Corwin:** "I found the wound. I never know what to do after." /
  *Maeve:* "That part's mine."

### Campfire scene (Corwin)
He shows the party the **Green Journal's** last blank pages and admits he hopes
someone finishes it after him. The moment quietly reframes his fear of being
forgotten into a wish for the truth to outlive him.

## Maeve Ashwood

### Camp behaviours (recurring)
Prepares **tea every evening**; tends injuries no one reported; repairs clothing;
prepares tomorrow's supplies; quietly **checks on companions during the night.**
The **Lantern of Ashes** becomes the camp's recognisable **nighttime symbol of
safety** — where it burns, someone stayed.

### Exploration behaviours (settlements)
Checks on the sick, helps children, visits local healers, gathers herbs, assists
elders, leaves medicine anonymously. NPCs recognise her — people she once helped
greet her first.

### Relationship banter
- **Talos:** *Talos:* "You should rest." / **Maeve:** "So should you. I'll make it
  an order if you will." (two people who carry the cost for others.)
- **Eleanor:** *Eleanor:* "You make hope look like chores." / **Maeve:** "That's
  because it is. Hope is something you practice."
- **Ronan:** **Maeve:** "You're not afraid of the wolf. You're afraid of the empty
  chair." / *Ronan:* (long silence) "…yes."
- **Torren:** *Torren:* "I fix things. You fix people." / **Maeve:** "You fix the
  roof so I have somewhere to fix them. Same work."
- **Corwin:** *Corwin:* "I found the wound." / **Maeve:** "Then leave the rest to
  me."
- **Ragash:** (Maeve tends the hounds' paws without being asked; *Ragash:* "…they
  like you." **Maeve:** "Everyone's easier to help than they think.")

### Quiet emotional moment (Maeve)
Late at night she opens the **Field Journal** to two names and reads them once —
the patients she couldn't both save. A companion offers to sit with her; her arc
is learning to let them. *"People rarely remember the medicine. They remember the
person who stayed."*

## Corwin ⟷ Maeve (the central late-party bond)
He names the wound; she tends it. Truth and compassion, working the same problem
from two ends. A recurring travel beat: Corwin's relentless *"but what if I missed
something?"* met by Maeve's *"then we'll find it together — after you sleep."*
