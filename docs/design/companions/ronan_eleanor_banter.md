# Ronan & Eleanor — Companion Banter & Campfire Lines

> **Status:** Canonical writing (2026-06). Ambient banter and campfire lines that
> build the Ronan–Eleanor bond **across the whole journey**, not only inside
> [`The Lost Howl`](../quests/the_lost_howl.md). Characters:
> [`Ronan`](../canon/Characters.md#ronan) · [`Eleanor`](../canon/Characters.md#eleanor).
> Writing/design only — no gameplay systems, triggers, or VFX are implemented
> here. Trigger conditions and VO are `_TBD_`.

## Voice & rules (preserve)
- Eleanor **never** separates Ronan from the wolf — she says **"you,"** never
  *"the wolf."* This holds in every line below.
- Ronan is quiet, guarded, self-deprecating, dryly funny; warmth underneath. He
  deflects care with humour, then occasionally lets the guard drop.
- Eleanor answers fear with **hope**, not naïveté — she knows the cost and chooses
  kindness anyway.
- Keep it small and human. No grand speeches during travel; the weight lands in
  short lines and pauses.

---

## Travel barks (short, ambient)
**Early journey — guarded**
- **Eleanor:** "You walk like you're expecting the ground to give out."
  **Ronan:** "It usually does."
- **Ronan:** "You should keep to the middle of the group."
  **Eleanor:** "So should you. You never do."
- **Eleanor:** "You went quiet again."
  **Ronan:** "Quiet's safer. For everyone."

**Mid journey — thawing**
- **Ronan:** "You're not afraid of me. It's unsettling."
  **Eleanor:** "I'll try to be more alarming."
- **Eleanor:** "You saved Corwin's footing back there and pretended you didn't."
  **Ronan:** "...he's clumsy on scree. That's all."
- **Ronan:** "Full moon's in three nights."
  **Eleanor:** "Then we'll make sure you're among friends for it."

**Late journey — trust**
- **Eleanor:** "You slept through the night."
  **Ronan:** "First time in a while. Don't make it weird."
- **Ronan:** "If it ever — if *I* ever —"
  **Eleanor:** "Then we'll handle it. *We.* Stop rehearsing the worst of yourself."
- **Eleanor:** "You're smiling."
  **Ronan:** "Don't tell Talos. He'll want one too."

## Campfire conversations (longer, chosen beats)
### The defining exchange *(canonical — preserve verbatim)*
> **Ronan:** "Have you ever wished you weren't born different?"
> **Eleanor:** "No."
> *(a pause)*
> **Eleanor:** "I've wished the world was kinder."

### On the seed pouch
> **Eleanor:** "What's in the little pouch? You touch it when you think no one's looking."
> **Ronan:** "Seeds. From home. Stupid, really — carrying dead weight across half the world."
> **Eleanor:** "Seeds aren't dead weight. They're just waiting for the right ground."

### On being alone
> **Ronan:** "Most of my life I figured I'd end it the way I started the worst of it. Alone."
> **Eleanor:** "And now?"
> **Ronan:** "Now there's too many of you to lose track of. It's annoying."
> **Eleanor:** "That's the nicest thing you've ever said to me."

### After a hard fight
> **Eleanor:** "You put yourself between the arrows and Corwin again."
> **Ronan:** "I heal faster. Math."
> **Eleanor:** "It isn't math. It's you deciding you're the one who's allowed to get hurt. Stop doing that."

## Post-*Lost Howl* campfire *(after the quest — canonical lines)*
> **Ronan:** "How did you know he wouldn't hurt you?"
> **Eleanor** *(smiling):* "I didn't."
> **Eleanor:** "Sometimes compassion is a choice, not a certainty."

> **Ronan:** "The Alpha offered me a place. A whole pack of people like me."
> **Eleanor:** "I heard what you told him."
> **Ronan:** "'I already found my pack.' ...I meant it."
> **Eleanor:** "I know. That's why the token travels next to the seeds now — hope and home, together."
