# Player Onboarding Roadmap

> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED — established July 2026.
> **Authority:** Defines when major systems are introduced to the player. The AI DM and all quest/encounter design must respect this pacing: **complex mechanics unfold naturally rather than all at once.** A system is "introduced" when the player first *needs* it and it is explained in-fiction — not when it silently starts running.
> **Cross-references:** [`CORE_DESIGN_PILLARS.md`](CORE_DESIGN_PILLARS.md) (Pillar: Player Choice, Quality Over Quantity) · [`PLAYER_COMMUNICATION.md`](PLAYER_COMMUNICATION.md) · [`docs/systems/ai_director.md`](../systems/ai_director.md) · [`docs/CAMPAIGN_DESIGN.md`](../CAMPAIGN_DESIGN.md)

## Principles

1. **Systems may run before they are surfaced.** World State simulates from minute one; the player doesn't need to know until its effects touch them.
2. **Introduce through fiction, not tutorials.** A companion disagreeing teaches approval; a merchant's empty stall teaches regional economy.
3. **One new system per beat.** Never introduce two unfamiliar mechanics in the same scene.
4. **The player's curiosity sets the ceiling, not the floor.** A player who digs early may find deeper systems; the roadmap governs what is *pushed* at them, not what is reachable.

## The Roadmap

### Hour 1 — The Immediate World
- **Basic combat** — one readable, winnable fight; core action economy only
- **Dialogue** — a conversation with stakes, showing that phrasing and approach matter
- **Exploration** — movement, discovery, and one optional finding that rewards curiosity

*Deliberately absent:* approval numbers, crafting, faction names beyond ambient mention, world-state framing.

### Hours 2–3 — The Party
- **Companion approval** — first visible companion reaction to a player choice
- **Camp conversations** — first camp scene; companions as people, not stat blocks
- **Crafting basics** — simple recipe tied to an immediate need ([`docs/systems/crafting.md`](../systems/crafting.md))
- Journal established as the player's memory ([`docs/systems/journal_system.md`](../systems/journal_system.md))

### Hours 4–8 — The Region
- **Reputation** — first faction consequence the player can trace to their own action ([`docs/systems/reputation.md`](../systems/reputation.md))
- **Regional consequences** — first visible change caused by a completed quest ([`VISIBLE_CONSEQUENCES.md`](VISIBLE_CONSEQUENCES.md))
- **Religion & shrines** — pilgrim and shrine encounters as texture before politics

### Later — The Simulation and the Deep World
Introduced only when the player's actions or travels make them relevant:

- **World State** — the player begins to see the world as a system: regional escalation and recovery ([`world_state/README.md`](world_state/README.md))
- **Regional consequences at scale** — cross-region ripples of earlier choices
- **Faction politics** — councils, rivalries, playing factions against each other ([`docs/world/factions/FACTION_BIBLE.md`](../world/factions/FACTION_BIBLE.md))
- **Vampire Houses** — hidden-world content; gated behind discovery, never front-loaded ([`docs/world/factions/vampire_houses/README.md`](../world/factions/vampire_houses/README.md))
- **Advanced crafting** — specialization paths and rare materials
- **Hidden systems** — the campaign's gated revelation layers and secret factions surface strictly by the discovery rules in their own canon docs; onboarding never schedules them by hour

## Enforcement

- The AI DM should check party progression before offering content that presumes an unintroduced system, and prefer encounters that teach the *next* system on the roadmap.
- Quest designers note in each quest which systems it presumes; a quest that presumes a "Later" system is late-campaign content by definition.
