## How to read a pairing
Each of the 21 companion pairings lists: **Working Technique Name · Combat Fantasy · Required
Positioning · Resolve Cost · Battlefield Purpose · Opening Created · Potential Follow-Up ·
Animation Theme.** Technique names are working titles. Cost is a **band** (Minor / Major /
Legendary — see [`RESOLVE_ECONOMY.md`](RESOLVE_ECONOMY.md)); most Partner Techniques are Major.
Companions: Talos, Corwin, Ragash, Eleanor, Ronan, Torren, Maeve.

## Talos pairings

### Talos + Corwin — *"Shield & Shot"*
- **Fantasy:** Talos breaks the guard; Corwin's arrow threads the gap the instant it opens.
- **Positioning:** Talos adjacent to target; Corwin with a clear line behind him.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Convert a defensive lock into a guaranteed precision kill.
- **Opening created:** *Exposed* on the struck enemy (Corwin capitalizes). **Follow-up:** Corwin
  *Marked* → focus fire. **Animation:** shield-slam → slow-mo arrow through the crack.

### Talos + Ragash — *"Anvil & Pack"*
- **Fantasy:** Talos pins the line; Ragash's hounds hit the flanks that can no longer retreat.
- **Positioning:** Talos front/center; Ragash + hounds on a flank.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Lock enemies between wall and teeth. **Opening created:**
  *Cornered* / *Ally Flanking*. **Follow-up:** Ragash *Grappled* → execute. **Animation:** braced
  shield wall while hounds pour around the edges.

### Talos + Eleanor — *"Bulwark Storm"*
- **Fantasy:** Talos holds a ward-line while Eleanor unloads area magic safely behind it.
- **Positioning:** Talos front; Eleanor within his protected zone.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Safe burst under cover. **Opening created:** *Ally Protected*
  (Eleanor overextends safely). **Follow-up:** elemental chain (Burning/Frozen). **Animation:**
  shield dome flaring as a storm erupts past it.

### Talos + Ronan — *"Hold and Hunt"*
- **Fantasy:** Talos taunts and roots the strongest threat; Ronan tears into the exposed rest.
- **Positioning:** Talos engaged with the anchor enemy; Ronan free to roam.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Split enemy attention; enable predator gameplay. **Opening
  created:** *Distracted* on non-anchor enemies. **Follow-up:** Ronan *Terrified* pounce.
  **Animation:** roar of defiance vs. a low, fast lunge.

### Talos + Torren — *"Fortify"*
- **Fantasy:** Torren throws up an emplacement; Talos makes it unbreakable.
- **Positioning:** Both on a chosen hold point / choke.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Create a fortress zone; deny ground. **Opening created:**
  *Cornered* for anyone who tries the choke. **Follow-up:** ranged allies fire from safety.
  **Animation:** hammer-driven barricade + planted shield.

### Talos + Maeve — *"Last Line"*
- **Fantasy:** Talos shields a downed ally while Maeve revives under fire.
- **Positioning:** Both adjacent to a downed/surrounded ally.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Turn a near-loss into a held line. **Opening created:** *Ally
  Protected* + *Inspired*. **Follow-up:** revived ally acts immediately. **Animation:** shield
  planted over a kneeling healer.

## Corwin pairings

### Corwin + Ragash — *"Flush & Fell"*
- **Fantasy:** Ragash's hounds drive prey out of cover into Corwin's sightline.
- **Positioning:** Ragash pushing from one side; Corwin holding the open lane.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Deny cover; punish flight. **Opening created:** *Exposed* /
  *Marked*. **Follow-up:** long-range execution. **Animation:** hounds burst cover → clean shot.

### Corwin + Eleanor — *"Storm Arrow"*
- **Fantasy:** Eleanor charges the field (wet/electrified); Corwin's shot detonates the chain.
- **Positioning:** Corwin with a line into Eleanor's charged zone.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Turn a precise shot into area burst. **Opening created:**
  *Electrified* chain. **Follow-up:** shattered/scorched cluster. **Animation:** arrow crackling
  through a storm-soaked lane.

### Corwin + Ronan — *"Pin the Prey"*
- **Fantasy:** Corwin pins a runner in place; Ronan closes the distance and finishes it.
- **Positioning:** Corwin at range; Ronan with a charge lane.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Stop escapes; enable pounce. **Opening created:** *Pinned* →
  *Terrified*. **Follow-up:** Ronan execute. **Animation:** pinning shot → predatory leap.

### Corwin + Torren — *"Called Shot: Demolition"*
- **Fantasy:** Corwin's shot triggers Torren's rigged object at the perfect moment.
- **Positioning:** Corwin with a line to a rigged explosive/structure.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** On-demand area detonation. **Opening created:** *Staggered* /
  *Knocked Down* cluster. **Follow-up:** melee cleanup. **Animation:** spark-arrow → controlled blast.

### Corwin + Maeve — *"Steady Hands"*
- **Fantasy:** Maeve steadies Corwin's aim/nerves for an impossible executing shot.
- **Positioning:** Maeve adjacent to Corwin; Corwin with any line.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Guaranteed high-value execution. **Opening created:** *Exposed*
  guaranteed. **Follow-up:** morale/Inspired ripple. **Animation:** a breath held, then loosed.

## Ragash pairings

### Ragash + Eleanor — *"Frostpack"*
- **Fantasy:** Eleanor chills the ground; the hounds herd enemies onto it to freeze and shatter.
- **Positioning:** Eleanor sets terrain; Ragash drives enemies onto it.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** CC + shatter combo. **Opening created:** *Chilled* → *Frozen*.
  **Follow-up:** shatter strike. **Animation:** hounds circling a freezing floor.

### Ragash + Ronan — *"Wolf and Wolves"*
- **Fantasy:** Ragash's pack and Ronan hunt as one — total encirclement of isolated prey.
- **Positioning:** Ragash + hounds and Ronan surrounding a target.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Overwhelm an isolated/strong enemy. **Opening created:**
  *Cornered* + *Terrified*. **Follow-up:** simultaneous maul. **Animation:** a closing ring of teeth.
- *(Also a natural Legendary candidate vs a lone elite/boss-add — see Tiers.)*

### Ragash + Torren — *"Trapline"*
- **Fantasy:** Torren rigs restraints; Ragash's hounds herd enemies into them.
- **Positioning:** Torren places traps on likely flee routes; Ragash drives.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Area denial + capture. **Opening created:** *Pinned* / *Rooted*.
  **Follow-up:** focus fire. **Animation:** snapping snares as the pack drives forward.

### Ragash + Maeve — *"Wounded Beast"*
- **Fantasy:** Maeve sustains the hounds through a prolonged harass; the pack never breaks.
- **Positioning:** Maeve near the pack's operating area.
- **Cost:** Minor–Major. **Purpose:** Sustained attrition/flanking pressure. **Opening created:**
  persistent *Distracted*/flank windows. **Follow-up:** Ragash Huntmaster strike. **Animation:**
  hounds shrugging off wounds and diving back in.

## Eleanor pairings

### Eleanor + Ronan — *"Terror and Tempest"*
- **Fantasy:** Ronan's fear scatters a cluster; Eleanor catches the panicked crowd in an AoE.
- **Positioning:** Ronan among enemies; Eleanor with an area line on them.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Punish clustered/fleeing enemies. **Opening created:**
  *Terrified* herd → area effect. **Follow-up:** Burning/Confused cluster. **Animation:** a
  howl scattering figures into a gathering storm.

### Eleanor + Torren — *"Thermal Lance"*
- **Fantasy:** Torren superheats a construct/weapon; Eleanor channels through it for a lance.
- **Positioning:** Eleanor adjacent to Torren's construct/line.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Armor-melting focused beam. **Opening created:** *Scorched*.
  **Follow-up:** guard-break/precision follow. **Animation:** molten metal + arcane channel.

### Eleanor + Maeve — *"Sanctuary"*
- **Fantasy:** A warded, healing zone the party can fight boldly inside.
- **Positioning:** Both centered on the party's hold point.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Sustain + safe aggression. **Opening created:** *Ally Protected*
  + *Inspired*. **Follow-up:** whole-party Partner Technique window. **Animation:** a bright,
  humming dome.

## Torren + Maeve — *"Field Works"*
- **Fantasy:** Torren builds a fortified aid station; Maeve turns it into an unbreakable rally point.
- **Positioning:** Both on the party's hold/rally point.
- **Cost:** Major. **Purpose:** Persistent safe zone + sustain + area denial. **Opening created:**
  *Cornered* for attackers, *Ally Protected* for the party. **Follow-up:** the party projects
  force outward from safety. **Animation:** hammered barricades wrapped in a healer's light.

## Legendary Partner Techniques (multi-character)
The Tiers model reserves **Legendary** windows (often boss enrage/exposed phases) for
**multi-character** techniques that spend banked Resolve for a cinematic payoff — e.g. a
full-party focus on an *Exposed* boss, or *Wolf and Wolves* + *Storm Arrow* chained during a
Legendary opening. Specific Legendary set-pieces are authored per encounter (`_TBD_`), starting
with the Corruption Avatar's exposed phase
([`encounters/the_corruption_avatar.md`](encounters/the_corruption_avatar.md)).

## Design guarantees
- Every pairing has **at least one** technique so any two-companion combination feels intentional.
- Each technique **creates a follow-up opening** (Design Rule 1) — Partner Techniques are not
  terminal burst; they keep the loop going.
- Techniques obey positioning, targeting, and the Resolve economy — **spectacle never overrides
  tactics.**
