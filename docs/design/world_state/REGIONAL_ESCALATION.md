# Regional Escalation — How Ignored Threats Grow

> **Canon Status:** Confirmed — established July 2026.
> **Authority:** Canonical escalation patterns for the Living World. Regional canon defers to [`docs/world/WORLD_BIBLE.md`](../../world/WORLD_BIBLE.md); vampire canon to [`docs/world/factions/vampire_houses/`](../../world/factions/vampire_houses/README.md); encounter mechanics to [`docs/systems/frontier_encounters.md`](../../systems/frontier_encounters.md).
> **Cross-references:** [`LIVING_WORLD_SYSTEM.md`](LIVING_WORLD_SYSTEM.md) · [`WORLD_REACTIONS.md`](WORLD_REACTIONS.md) · [`CONSEQUENCES.md`](CONSEQUENCES.md)

Threats the player ignores do not wait, and do not fail the game. They **escalate believably** — each stage with visible signals, faction responses, and in-world logic. The player is never punished directly; the world simply reacts.

---

## The Escalation Pattern (Canonical Template)

Every escalating threat defines:

1. **Stages** — a believable progression from nuisance to crisis.
2. **Signals** — what the player sees, hears, and reads at each stage ([`WORLD_REACTIONS.md`](WORLD_REACTIONS.md) channels).
3. **World State pressure** — which categories each stage moves.
4. **Reversibility** — what intervention at each stage can achieve; later intervention costs more and restores less.

## Example: The Bandit Crisis

Ignoring organized bandits allows them to grow:

> **Small camps → Organized patrols → Road blockades → Fortified encampments → Regional control**

Consequences as stages advance:

- merchants avoid roads
- prices increase
- supplies become scarce
- random encounters become more dangerous

Faction texture: Rangers report the camps first; the Merchant Guild reroutes; Thornvale-style border lords demand Army action; the Council debates. By the fortified stage, clearing the roads is a campaign, not a quest.

## Example: The Vampire Crisis

Ignoring the Vampire Houses does not immediately fail the game. Instead **the hidden vampire society begins to collapse, and feral vampires escape into the wider world**:

- stronger night encounters
- refugee movements
- closed trade routes
- missing caravans
- abandoned villages
- increased Frontier Ranger patrols
- military casualties
- reduced merchant availability
- greater corruption pressure

Canon constraints: the Veil holds as *public* knowledge even in collapse — mortals experience the crisis as monsters, plague-fear, and rumor, not as "the vampire houses are failing" ([`THE_VEIL.md`](../../world/factions/vampire_houses/THE_VEIL.md)). The Journal records rumor-grade entries ("The Crimson Conclave has reportedly withdrawn from several regions") only where the player has touched that hidden world.

## Example: The Corrupted Forest

If left untreated:

- wildlife mutates
- food becomes scarce
- alchemy ingredients become corrupted
- Ranger expedition bonuses become less effective
- travel becomes increasingly dangerous

This is the environmental template: Corruption escalation degrades **Environmental Health → Prosperity → Security** in sequence, and quietly damages the systems the player relies on (crafting inputs, expedition support) rather than just spawning harder fights.

## Regional Character

Escalation wears each region's face ([`docs/world/WORLD_BIBLE.md`](../../world/WORLD_BIBLE.md)): the Frontier falls to bandits and corruption first (weakest institutions fail first — established canon); the Capital Province escalates politically; the Great Forest environmentally; trade regions economically. Write escalation in the region's native failure mode.

## Design Rules

1. **No stage is skipped silently.** Each escalation stage must emit at least one player-visible signal before the next stage begins.
2. **Escalation is content.** Each stage should open quests appropriate to it — scouting at the camp stage, convoy defense at the blockade stage, siege at the fortress stage.
3. **Intervention always matters.** Even late intervention improves outcomes — it just cannot fully restore what earlier action would have saved ([`CONSEQUENCES.md`](CONSEQUENCES.md)).
4. **In-world explanation is mandatory.** If a designer cannot say *why* the danger grew, the escalation is not yet valid.
