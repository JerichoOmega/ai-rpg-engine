# World Reactions — Factions, Companions, and the Journal

> **Canon Status:** Confirmed — established July 2026.
> **Authority:** Canonical for how the world visibly responds to World State changes. Faction detail defers to [`docs/world/factions/FACTION_BIBLE.md`](../../world/factions/FACTION_BIBLE.md); companion canon to [`docs/design/companions/`](../companions/README.md); journal mechanics to [`docs/systems/journal_system.md`](../../systems/journal_system.md).
> **Cross-references:** [`WORLD_STATE_SYSTEM.md`](WORLD_STATE_SYSTEM.md) · [`REGIONAL_ESCALATION.md`](REGIONAL_ESCALATION.md)

The Living World only works if the player can *see* the world reacting. Three channels carry the signal: institutions, companions, and the Journal.

---

## Faction Integration

**Every major faction responds dynamically to World State changes.** Canonical response patterns:

| Faction | Responses |
|---|---|
| **Imperial Council** | Emergency legislation · military deployments · political debates ([`GOVERNMENT.md`](../../world/factions/GOVERNMENT.md) — slowly, publicly, and behind schedule is in character) |
| **Great Library** | Sends archivists · preserves endangered knowledge · prioritizes threatened regions |
| **Covenant** | Relief efforts · pilgrimages · shrine restoration |
| **Merchant Guild** | Reroutes caravans · changes prices · adjusts trade |
| **Frontier Rangers** | Increase patrols · investigate threats · escort civilians |
| **Builders' Guild** | Repairs roads · restores bridges · rebuilds settlements |

Faction responses are *content*, not bookkeeping: the player should encounter the archivist convoy, meet the rerouted caravan, see the Rangers' doubled patrol. Each response is a quest surface and a readable signal of which way the world is moving.

## Companion Integration

**Companions notice changes.** Canonical examples:

- **The Ranger** (planned sixth companion — [`docs/world/factions/MILITARY_ORDERS.md`](../../world/factions/MILITARY_ORDERS.md)) comments on disappearing wildlife.
- **Torren** ([`docs/heroes/TORREN.md`](../../heroes/TORREN.md)) notices deteriorating infrastructure.
- **Eleanor** ([`docs/heroes/ELEANOR.md`](../../heroes/ELEANOR.md)) comments on magical instability.

Companions react naturally to a changing world — through Journey Conversations ([`docs/systems/journey_system.md`](../../systems/journey_system.md)) and ambient comments, in their own voice and expertise. Every companion should have World State observation lines keyed to the categories they would plausibly notice.

## Journal Integration

**The Journal updates automatically as the player encounters a changing world, and becomes a living historical record.** Automatic does not mean omniscient: per [`docs/systems/journal_system.md`](../../systems/journal_system.md), the Journal only expands through discovery. A World State change enters the Journal when the player **witnesses it directly, receives a credible in-world report (NPC, notice, faction contact), or hears sourced rumor** — never as a global broadcast of developments in regions the player has no connection to. Canonical entry style — specific, located, and consequential:

> "The roads north of Ashwyn have become unsafe."
> "The Shrine of Whispering Grove has fallen into neglect."
> "Merchant caravans have ceased travel through Frost Pass."
> "The Crimson Conclave has reportedly withdrawn from several regions."

Rules:

1. Entries record *world changes*, not just player deeds — the Journal is the continent's memory of the playthrough, as the player comes to know it.
1. **Discovery gate (mandatory):** no entry without an in-world source — direct observation, credible report, or attributed rumor. Unvisited, unreported changes stay out of the Journal until the player learns of them.
2. Entries name places and institutions; "things got worse" is never an entry.
3. Rumor-grade knowledge is allowed (note the Conclave example: the Journal can record what people *say*, preserving the Veil and other secrets — [`docs/world/factions/vampire_houses/THE_VEIL.md`](../../world/factions/vampire_houses/THE_VEIL.md)).
4. Journal mechanics and revelation handling defer to [`docs/systems/journal_system.md`](../../systems/journal_system.md).

## Design Rule

Every significant World State change must surface through **at least one** of the three channels — ideally all three at different scales: a faction acts on it, a companion remarks on it, and the Journal records it once the player has encountered it through one of those surfaces.
