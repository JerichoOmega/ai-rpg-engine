## The Imperial Army

- **Purpose:** Protect civilization · defend trade · respond to monster threats · assist frontier settlements.
- **Command:** The **Military High Command**, headed by **Marshal Edric Voss** ([`docs/lore/IMPERIAL_CAPITAL.md`](../../lore/IMPERIAL_CAPITAL.md)). Under the federal structure ([`GOVERNMENT.md`](GOVERNMENT.md)), the Army is the continental defense instrument coordinated through the Imperial Council; realms retain their own militias in peacetime.
- **Character:** A Restoration institution — built to hold roads and settlements against decline, not to conquer. Its veterans (Talos among them — [`docs/heroes/TALOS.md`](../../heroes/TALOS.md)) know the gap between its purpose and its politics.

### The Five Branches

| Branch | Role |
|---|---|
| **Infantry** | Garrisons, road security, settlement defense — the Army's face in ordinary life |
| **Engineers** | Fortification, siegecraft, disaster response; works closely with the Builders' Guild |
| **Scouts** | Reconnaissance and threat tracking ahead of the garrisons |
| **Medical Corps** | Field medicine, plague response (with the Alchemists' Consortium), veterans' care |
| **Frontier Rangers** | See below — a branch on paper, an order in practice |

## The Frontier Rangers

- **Purpose:** Protect roads · scout dangerous regions · track monsters · guide expeditions. **They know the wilderness better than anyone else.**
- **Status:** Formally the Army's fifth branch; operationally independent — Ranger patrols answer to their own wardens-of-district, requisition from the Army, and follow doctrine written by people who sleep outdoors. The arrangement is canonical: **operational independence, military support.**
- **Character:** Small patrols, long circuits, deep local knowledge; the first institution to notice a new Corruption zone, a missing caravan, or a settlement about to fail. In Frontier content they are the connective tissue of encounters ([`docs/systems/frontier_encounters.md`](../../systems/frontier_encounters.md)).
- **Companion hook (canon):** The planned **Ranger companion** (sixth companion, Phase 2) should have connections to this organization — history in a Ranger district, or standing with its wardens. Author their Hero Bible with this link.
- **World State:** Increase patrols, investigate new corruption zones, warn settlements of emerging dangers — Ranger activity level is a frontline World State signal.
- **Player interaction:** Wilderness guidance, tracking quests, early-warning arcs, safe-passage relationships.

## The Library Expedition Corps

- **Purpose:** Field researchers working for the Great Library ([`docs/lore/GREAT_LIBRARY.md`](../../lore/GREAT_LIBRARY.md)): recover ruins · escort scholars · retrieve artifacts · document discoveries.
- **Character:** Scholar-soldiers and hired specialists; the Library's motto made ambulatory. Where the Explorers' Guild charts, the Corps *retrieves* — the two share members, funding, and a professional rivalry conducted mostly through footnotes.
- **Status:** A civil order under Library authority, not a military body; it hires guards (often Adventurers Guild) rather than fielding troops.
- **Player interaction:** **Major quest-giving organization** — ruin expeditions, artifact recovery, escort contracts, historical investigations feeding the journal and revelation arcs.
- **World State:** Redirects to endangered regions to save threatened knowledge first — Corps priorities are a map of what the Library fears losing.

## Design Rules

1. The Army protects; it does not rule. Military-government tension (Voss, the Crown, the Council, Thornvale's marches) is political story, not doctrine.
2. Rangers are never generic soldiers; write them as the people who know the land's name for every danger on it.
3. Corps quests must serve the memory-against-forgetting theme — what is retrieved matters less than what it lets the world remember.
