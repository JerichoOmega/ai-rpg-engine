## The Covenant (Continental Institution)

"The Covenant" in faction contexts is the **Solari Covenant** in its continental institutional role — the largest and most institutional of the four religious traditions, embedded in nobility and law in the Capital Province and Sol Kareth ([`docs/world/religions.md`](../religions.md)).

- **Type:** Continental religious organization.
- **Purpose:** Maintain temples · protect shrines · teach the traditions of the Divine Chorus · preserve sacred history.
- **View of the Divine Chorus:** Cosmic caretakers rather than miracle workers. The Chorus loves creation and intervenes only when reality itself is threatened; the Covenant's work is therefore mortal work.
- **View of the Long Decline:** An existential *and spiritual* crisis — a test of whether civilization deserves what the Chorus preserved.

### Structure

| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **High Council** | Senior clergy; doctrine, appointments, and the Covenant's voice toward the Imperial Council |
| **Regional Clergy** | Diocesan administration; temples, festivals, records |
| **Shrine Keepers** | Covenant-sworn caretakers of Covenant-maintained shrines |
| **Temple Wardens** | Protection of temples, pilgrims, and sacred property |
| **Pilgrim Orders** | Lay and sworn orders organizing pilgrimage routes and hospitality |
| **The Inquisition** | Defined wholly in [`docs/world/covenant_inquisitors.md`](../covenant_inquisitors.md) — doctrine enforcement and the policing of dangerous knowledge; its internal tension with Reformists is canon |

### Player Interaction

Shrines, pilgrimages, religious festivals, historical records, community support — and, through the Inquisition and Reformists, the harder questions the institution would rather not be asked.

## The Shrine Wardens (Independent Order)

**Independent caretakers of shrines — not a Covenant body.** The distinction is canon and politically alive: Wardens maintain sacred sites of *all* traditions, while the Covenant's Shrine Keepers serve Covenant sites specifically.

- **Purpose:** Maintain sacred sites · protect traditions · assist pilgrims · document local history.
- **Character:** Local, sworn to place rather than hierarchy; a Warden may tend a roadside shrine their family has kept for five generations.
- **Relationship with the Covenant:** Independent but cooperative. The Covenant funds and honors Wardens where interests align, and quietly resents that it cannot command them.
- **World State:** Strongly influenced by it — shrine conditions (Maintained / Neglected / Abandoned / Corrupted / Revitalized, per [`docs/world/shrine_locations.md`](../shrine_locations.md)) are the most visible barometer of a region's health, and Wardens are the first to notice a shrine turning.
- **Player interaction:** Shrine restoration, local history, early warnings of Corruption; the humble questline surface that connects geography, religion, and World State.

## Design Rules

1. This file never overrides religions.md: the four traditions (Solari Covenant, Old Ways, Ancestors' Path, Veiled Order) and their reputation tracks stand as written ([`docs/systems/reputation.md`](../../systems/reputation.md)).
2. The Covenant is not the state church of the continent — it is the largest tradition among four, dominant in specific regions, and its institutional claim to primacy is *contested theology*, not fact.
3. Religious factions answer the Long Decline like everyone else: preservation through faith, through order, through memory, or through restraint — and no tradition's answer is authorially endorsed.
