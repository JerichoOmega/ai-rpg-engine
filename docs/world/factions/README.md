# Canonical Faction Bible — Folder Index

> **Canon Status:** Confirmed — established July 2026. Core reference document.
> **Authority:** This folder is the canonical reference for the continent's factions. It supplements — and defers to — the Lore Bible (`docs/lore/`), [`docs/world/WORLD_BIBLE.md`](../WORLD_BIBLE.md), and the existing authoritative faction documents cross-referenced throughout (religions, cults, goblin tribes, vampire houses, reputation system). Where a detailed document exists for a faction, that document remains authoritative; this folder provides the continental frame.

---

## Core Philosophy

Every faction exists to answer one question:

> **How does this group believe civilization should survive the Long Decline?**

- No faction exists simply to be "good" or "evil."
- Every organization has understandable goals, strengths, flaws, and internal disagreements.
- Every faction believes it is helping preserve civilization, even when its methods conflict with everyone else's.
- The world feels alive because its institutions pursue their own goals whether or not the player is present. The player influences history, but the continent is sustained by countless ordinary people and organizations working to preserve civilization.

## Canonical Design Rules

Every new faction added to the project must define:

1. Purpose
2. History
3. Leadership
4. Core Values
5. Allies
6. Rivals
7. View of the Divine Chorus
8. View of the Long Decline
9. Gameplay Function
10. Relationship to the World State

No faction should exist solely as exposition or decoration. Every organization must contribute meaningfully to the player's understanding of the world and reinforce the project's central themes.

## Standing Reconciliation Rules

- **Vampire houses and mortal noble houses are distinct institutions** with separate histories, identities, and naming conventions. Direct name collisions are prohibited unless intentionally created later as approved story revelations.
- **"The Covenant" in this folder is the Solari Covenant** of [`docs/world/religions.md`](../religions.md) in its continental institutional role; religions.md remains authoritative for theology and internal factions.
- Existing detailed canon (goblin tribes, vampire houses, covenant inquisitors, cults, Great Library, Imperial Capital) always takes precedence over the summaries here.
