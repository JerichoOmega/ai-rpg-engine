## Canonical Relationship Pairs

| Relationship | Character |
|---|---|
| **Imperial Council ↔ Great Library** | Mutual dependence, political tension. The Council needs the Library's knowledge for policy; the Library needs Council funding and protection. Each believes it serves the longer view. |
| **Imperial Council ↔ the Crown (Aldenmoor)** | Federal partners and quiet rivals. The Queen is the Council's most influential member and the realm most capable of ignoring it ([`GOVERNMENT.md`](GOVERNMENT.md)). |
| **Great Library ↔ Covenant** | Mutual respect, different interpretations of history — the Library preserves what happened; the Covenant preserves what it means. Their disagreements over the Sundering's record are conducted in exquisitely polite scholarship. |
| **Merchant Guild ↔ Builders' Guild** | Close cooperation: trade pays for roads, roads carry trade. The continent's most functional institutional friendship. |
| **Explorers' Guild ↔ Great Library** | Constant partnership — funding, discoveries, shared members with the Expedition Corps, and rivalry conducted through footnotes. |
| **Frontier Rangers ↔ Imperial Army** | Operational independence, military support. A branch on paper, an order in practice ([`MILITARY_ORDERS.md`](MILITARY_ORDERS.md)). |
| **Shrine Wardens ↔ Covenant** | Independent but cooperative; the Covenant funds what it cannot command ([`RELIGIOUS_ORDERS.md`](RELIGIOUS_ORDERS.md)). |
| **Veiled Order ↔ Great Library** | Officially denied; unofficially acknowledged by a select few ([`SECRET_ORGANIZATIONS.md`](SECRET_ORGANIZATIONS.md)). |
| **Alchemists' Consortium ↔ Imperial Council** | Valued in crisis, watched in peace; a standing oversight committee the Consortium keeps outmaneuvering. |
| **Smiths' Guild ↔ Imperial Army** | Supplier and standard-setter; arms contracts bind them, licensing disputes divide them. |
| **Covenant ↔ Old Ways** | Historical tension with baseline reputation effects — established in [`docs/world/religions.md`](../religions.md) and [`docs/systems/reputation.md`](../../systems/reputation.md). |
| **Noble house rivalries** | Aldenmoor/Varric (precedence), Thornvale/Ledric (budgets), Wrenmoor/Caelum (grain and credit), Corvath/Ashwyn (law and secrets) — [`NOBLE_HOUSES.md`](NOBLE_HOUSES.md). |
| **Vampire inter-house web** | Entirely separate and hidden — [`vampire_houses/VAMPIRE_POLITICS.md`](vampire_houses/VAMPIRE_POLITICS.md). Mortal factions do not knowingly have vampire relationships. |
| **Goblin cross-tribal dynamics** | Established in [`docs/systems/reputation.md`](../../systems/reputation.md) and [`docs/world/goblin_tribes.md`](../goblin_tribes.md). |

## Rules for Writers

1. **Relationships are levers, not lore trivia.** Every quest touching two factions should let their canonical relationship shape outcomes.
2. **Helping one side moves the other.** Cross-faction reputation effects follow [`docs/systems/reputation.md`](../../systems/reputation.md); never award standing in a vacuum.
3. **New relationships must be added here** when new factions are authored; contradicting an existing pair requires updating this file, not ignoring it.
4. **No total alliances, no total wars.** Every pair keeps friction inside friendship and courtesy inside rivalry — that is where the stories are.
