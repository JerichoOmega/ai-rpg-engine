## Orc Society — a mosaic of clans (canonical framing)
Elyndor's orcs are **not** a single unified culture. They are **many
independent clans**, bound by shared heritage but governing themselves (clan
councils; coalitions form for a purpose and dissolve when it ends — see
`NON_HUMAN_FACTIONS.md`). Broad cultural traditions include:

- **War Clans** — martial, expansionist, aggressive; the source of the "raider"
  stereotype the wider orc peoples resent.
- **Honor Clans** — disciplined, principled, self-reliant; keep their word, trade
  peacefully, defend fiercely. **The Bloodhorn are an Honor Clan.**
- **Wild Clans** — nomadic, close to the land and its beasts; live apart from
  settled powers.

No clan speaks for all orcs; clan-vs-clan disagreement is canonical and expected.

## The Bloodhorn Clan
- **Type:** Honor Clan.
- **Motto:** *"Strength is earned. Honor is remembered."*
- **Values:** honour, strength, discipline, self-reliance, responsibility, loyalty.
- **Conduct:** they **do not attack innocents without cause**; they **trade
  peacefully** when appropriate, **defend their territory fiercely**, and **keep
  their word.** Leadership is **earned and revocable**; oaths are witnessed and
  remembered.
- **Reputation:** a deliberate contrast to the more aggressive War Clans —
  respected, principled, unshowy.
- **Governance:** the **Circle of Elders** rules on matters of clan law and
  honour; a **High Chieftain** leads. (Roles/names beyond this: `_TBD_`.)

### Bloodhorn Hold (stronghold)
A **mountain fortress built for defence, not conquest**:
- Stone construction with **massive timber defences**.
- **Integrated kennels** (the hounds are central to Bloodhorn life).
- **Watchtowers**, **training grounds**, and **hunter lodges**.
- Defensible mountain approaches (passes, chokepoints) — see the siege beats in
  [`The Broken Oath`](../../quests/the_broken_oath.md).

### Battlefield culture — Horn Calls
Bloodhorn coordinate in the field through **distinct horn signals**, each a
different order. Canonical signal categories:
- **Scouts** — advance/observe.
- **Archers** — ready/volley.
- **Defenders** — hold the line.
- **Houndmasters** — release/recall the hounds.
- **Full mobilization** — all to arms.

### Houndmasters
Only a **small number** of Bloodhorn become **Houndmasters** — respected
specialists who raise, train, and fight alongside war-hounds. It is a mark of
rare skill and temperament. **Ragash is one of the finest Houndmasters the
Bloodhorn have ever produced** — which is part of why her exile cost the clan so
much, and why it cut her so deep.

## Canon reconciliation (for writers)
Ragash's established wound — *never quite fitting, earning belonging from her
hounds rather than her people* (`docs/heroes/RAGASH.md`) — is her life **within**
the Bloodhorn. **The Broken Oath** is the specific, defining rupture that
formalised her exile. Both are true: she was always slightly outside, and the
oath-breaking was the final line. Her present "Independent / keeps to herself"
status (`docs/characters/ragash.md`) is a **consequence** of that exile, not a
contradiction of her Bloodhorn origin.
