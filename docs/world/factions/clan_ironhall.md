## Overview
**Clan Ironhall** is a dwarven **craft-clan of the Iron Peaks** — master
blacksmiths, engineers, and keepers of a craft-lineage measured not in gold but
in *work that endures*. Ironhall does not define itself by conquest or wealth;
it defines itself by **the integrity of what its smiths make and the oaths its
Masters keep.** To carry the Ironhall mark on a finished work once meant the
work was *true* — sound to the core, honestly made, built to outlast its maker.

- **Homeland:** the Iron Peaks (dwarven heartland — [`RACES.md`](../RACES.md)).
- **Identity:** a **craft-honour clan** — closer in temperament to the orcish
  Honor Clans' principle of *"honour as accountability"* than to any War Clan,
  though the two peoples are unrelated. Ironhall keeps its word and signs its
  work.
- **Values:** craft, integrity, permanence, humility, restoration over ruin,
  and **truth** — including hard truths about its own history.
- **Governance:** dwarven clan-hall leadership under a **High Thane**; a clan
  **historian/loremaster** keeps the Hall of Names. (Wider dwarven federal
  standing: [`NON_HUMAN_FACTIONS.md`](NON_HUMAN_FACTIONS.md).)

## The Master's Lineage and the Founder's Compass
Ironhall's leadership of craft passes from one **Clan Master** to the next, and
with it passes **The Founder's Compass** — an ancient **master craftsman's
measuring compass** (not a navigation instrument) forged by the clan's founder.
Every Master has used it, repaired it, and **engraved their name inside one arm**
before passing it on. The compass is the clan's true crown: a working tool that
records the lineage in the metal itself.

**Engraved lineage (canonical, per the reference sheet), oldest → most recent:**
Durin Ironhall · Barag Ironhall · Thorek Ironhall · Borin Ironhall ·
Hadrin Ironhall · Orin Ironhall · **Durgan Ironhall** · … · *(final blank space
reserved for)* **Torren Ironhall**.

### The Ironhall Master's Seal *(secondary heirloom)*
The **Ironhall Master's Seal** conferred the right to stamp Clan Ironhall's mark
on completed works of true craftsmanship. **Following the Broken Oath the seal
was retired** — no official Ironhall work has carried its mark for generations.
The current bearer, **Torren**, carries the seal **wrapped in cloth and has never
used it**, believing he has not yet earned that right. (See the quest's ending.)

## Historical Truth — Durgan and the Broken Oath
> **Surface history (what the clan believes at the story's start):** Durgan
> Ironhall broke an unbreakable oath and disgraced the clan. His name — and the
> clan's — was marked with disgrace in the Hall of Names.
>
> **Full, corrected history is uncovered in
> [`The Empty Pedestal`](../../quests/the_empty_pedestal.md).** Summary below;
> the quest is authoritative for the twist.

**Durgan Ironhall** was a former **Lord Warden of the Vault** who swore an
unbreakable oath to guard a relic. During a catastrophic disaster he faced an
impossible choice: **protect the relic, or save the kingdom's people.** He chose
the people. History remembered only that he *broke his oath* — it did not
remember that the **High King himself, in a sealed final order Durgan never
revealed, had explicitly released him from that oath and commanded him to save
the people.** The King died before the order could be made public; Durgan
accepted disgrace rather than destabilise the realm by producing a dead king's
private command.

> **Non-negotiable narrative rule:** *No major historical figure is a villain.*
> Durgan obeyed his King. The council judged on incomplete information. The
> tragedy is **incomplete history, not malice.**

## Important Locations (Clan Ironhall Deep-Hall, Iron Peaks)

### The Hall of Names
The clan's hall of lineage and record, kept by the loremaster. Each Ironhall
Master's name is carved here. **A mark of disgrace was carved across Clan
Ironhall's name** after the Broken Oath. At the quest's end, **High Thane Borik
personally removes that mark.**

### The Hall of Echoes
A resonant chamber of the deep-hall where the clan's history is recited and
remembered; a place of testimony and truth. Its acoustics make spoken oaths and
confessions carry — fitting for a clan that signs its work. Exact function/rites:
`_TBD_`.

### The Silent Forge
An **ancestral forge that has stood cold for generations** — dark since the
disgrace, out of grief and shame. At the quest's end **Torren returns to the
Silent Forge and completes an ordinary piece of craftsmanship**, then stamps it
with the **Ironhall Master's Seal** for the first time in generations, relighting
what the shame extinguished.

### The Empty Pedestal
The pedestal from which the guarded relic was lost during Durgan's disaster — the
literal *empty pedestal* the clan has fixated on for generations. The companion
quest's central image and title: the quest reveals that **the empty pedestal was
never the true issue** — the missing thing was **the truth of what happened**,
not the relic.

### The Ancestral Forge (Clan Ironhall)
The living heart of Ironhall's craft in the Iron Peaks — workshops, the Master's
bench, and the deep-halls where the clan's smiths work. (The **Silent Forge** is
its long-cold, historically-significant chamber.)

## Canon reconciliation (for writers)
- **Dwarf culture:** Ironhall is **one** dwarven craft-clan, not the whole
  dwarven people. It fills part of the "detailed dwarven culture NOT YET FULLY
  DEFINED" gap in [`RACES.md`](../RACES.md) additively; other dwarven clans/
  traditions remain open.
- **Torren's personal Core Wound (the Cold Clasp)** — the intimate failure kept
  as a "pending job" ([`TORREN.md`](../../heroes/TORREN.md)) — is **distinct
  from** this ancestral clan-honour arc. Both are true and complementary: the
  Cold Clasp is his private grief; *The Empty Pedestal* is his heritage. Neither
  overwrites the other.
