# The Eight Founding Noble Houses of the Capital Province

> **Canon Status:** Confirmed — established July 2026.
> **Authority:** Canonical roster of the Capital Province's founding mortal noble houses. House Aldenmoor's history, Queen Merveth Aldenmoor, and Capital governance are defined in [`docs/lore/IMPERIAL_CAPITAL.md`](../../lore/IMPERIAL_CAPITAL.md), which takes precedence.
> **Naming rule:** Mortal noble houses and Vampire Houses ([`vampire_houses/`](vampire_houses/README.md)) are completely distinct institutions with separate histories and naming conventions. Direct name collisions are prohibited unless approved later as deliberate story revelations.
> **Cross-references:** [`GOVERNMENT.md`](GOVERNMENT.md) · [`FACTION_RELATIONSHIPS.md`](FACTION_RELATIONSHIPS.md)

No House is entirely heroic or villainous. Each entry defines crest, motto, primary influence, political priorities, historic achievements, allies, and rivals.

---

## House Aldenmoor — The Royal House

- **Crest:** A golden sunburst over a grey keystone, on royal blue.
- **Motto:** *"We held. We hold."*
- **Primary influence:** The Crown of the Capital Province. Queen Merveth Aldenmoor is the third-generation Aldenmoor sovereign ([`docs/lore/IMPERIAL_CAPITAL.md`](../../lore/IMPERIAL_CAPITAL.md) — including the honest account of the house's founding in military opportunism at the Age of Sundering's collapse).
- **Political priorities:** Continuity of the dynasty; the Capital's primacy within the Imperial Council; order above experiment.
- **Historic achievements:** Held the Capital through the great crisis; managed the transition into the Age of Restoration; built the administrative state the other houses staff.
- **Allies:** House Ledric (the administrators), House Caelum (the treasury's bankers).
- **Rivals:** House Varric (old money that remembers when Aldenmoor was new money); House Thornvale (border lords who resent Capital centralism).

## House Corvath — The Judges

- **Crest:** A black raven grasping a silver key, on dark green.
- **Motto:** *"The law remembers."*
- **Primary influence:** The courts and the judiciary; Corvath jurists have drafted or interpreted most of the Province's standing law.
- **Political priorities:** Legal supremacy over royal decree; predictability; the guilds kept firmly inside the law's fence.
- **Historic achievements:** The Corvath Codes — the consolidation of post-Sundering law that made commerce enforceable again.
- **Allies:** House Ledric; the Merchant Guild (which needs enforceable contracts).
- **Rivals:** House Ashwyn (whose intelligence work keeps slipping the law's fence); the Crown, politely, whenever decree outruns statute.

## House Wrenmoor — The Harvest Lords

- **Crest:** A golden wheat sheaf bound with a green serpent, on cream.
- **Motto:** *"All tables are set by ours."*
- **Primary influence:** Agriculture and the food supply; the great estates and granaries that feed the Capital.
- **Political priorities:** Grain prices, land law, and roads that move harvests; food security framed as patriotism and priced as leverage.
- **Historic achievements:** The granary network that carried the Capital through the famine years of the early Restoration.
- **Allies:** House Thornvale (whose borders shield Wrenmoor land); the Builders' Guild.
- **Rivals:** House Caelum (credit against collateral, forever); the Merchant Guild when speculation moves grain prices.

## House Ledric — The Ministers

- **Crest:** Three silver quills on a field of slate.
- **Motto:** *"Someone must keep the accounts."*
- **Primary influence:** The ministries and the civil service; Ledric cousins staff the administrative state at every level.
- **Political priorities:** Competent administration; institutional continuity across reigns; budgets that balance.
- **Historic achievements:** Built the professional bureaucracy the Age of Restoration runs on.
- **Allies:** House Aldenmoor (the Crown needs its clerks); House Corvath.
- **Rivals:** House Ashwyn (two houses that both believe they run the state from behind it).

## House Caelum — The Bankers

- **Crest:** A silver star above an open hand, on deep purple.
- **Motto:** *"Trust is the only currency."*
- **Primary influence:** Banking, credit, and the financing of trade; the Caelum letter of credit is honored across the continent.
- **Political priorities:** Sound currency; enforceable debt; peace, which is cheaper than war.
- **Historic achievements:** Financed the Restoration's reconstruction; created the letter-of-credit system that lets caravans travel without chests of coin.
- **Allies:** House Aldenmoor; the Merchant Guild.
- **Rivals:** House Wrenmoor; House Varric (which regards finance as a fashion and land as forever).

## House Varric — The Old Blood

- **Crest:** A red stag on a field of aged gold.
- **Motto:** *"Before the crown, the land."*
- **Primary influence:** The oldest landholdings and the deepest lineage; Varric's claim to precedence predates the Aldenmoor crown, and the house never finishes mentioning it.
- **Political priorities:** Precedence, tradition, and the prerogatives of old land; a quiet conviction that the crown is held in trust by newcomers.
- **Historic achievements:** The oldest continuously held estates in the Province; patrons of the Capital's founding monuments and oldest shrines.
- **Allies:** House Wrenmoor (land understands land); traditionalist clergy of the Covenant.
- **Rivals:** House Aldenmoor (the wound of precedence); House Caelum.

## House Thornvale — The Border Lords

- **Crest:** A thorned vine encircling a watchtower, on forest green.
- **Motto:** *"The wall is where we stand."*
- **Primary influence:** The Province's frontier marches; Thornvale raises and quarters more soldiers than any other house.
- **Political priorities:** Frontier defense funded by the center; autonomy for the marches; respect for the people who actually hold the line.
- **Historic achievements:** Held the border marches through every crisis of the Restoration; their watchtower line is the Province's outer skin.
- **Allies:** House Wrenmoor; the Imperial Army's frontier commands and the Frontier Rangers ([`MILITARY_ORDERS.md`](MILITARY_ORDERS.md)).
- **Rivals:** House Aldenmoor (centralism vs. the marches); House Ledric (whose budgets never reach the border intact).

## House Ashwyn — The Quiet House

- **Crest:** A grey moth on a black field.
- **Motto:** *"We hear what the wind hears."*
- **Primary influence:** Information — diplomacy's back channels, correspondence, and the Province's informal intelligence.
- **Political priorities:** Being indispensable to whoever rules; knowing first; never being the story.
- **Historic achievements:** Unwritten, by design. The Restoration's histories contain a suspicious number of crises that resolved themselves.
- **Allies:** Whoever currently rules — presently the Crown; the Covenant's diplomatic offices.
- **Rivals:** House Corvath (the law dislikes what it cannot subpoena); House Ledric.

---

## Design Notes

- The eight houses are **Capital Province** institutions; other realms have their own nobilities, authored separately as regions develop.
- House-vs-house tension is the engine of Capital political quests: precedence (Varric/Aldenmoor), budget (Thornvale/Ledric), grain and credit (Wrenmoor/Caelum), law and secrets (Corvath/Ashwyn).
- The Corruption amplifies what is present ([`docs/lore/IMPERIAL_CAPITAL.md`](../../lore/IMPERIAL_CAPITAL.md)): each house's virtue has a failure mode, and Corruption-era stories should push on exactly that seam.
