## Structure of Vampire Society

Ten houses survive into the current age, all seated at the Crimson Conclave ([`THE_CRIMSON_CONCLAVE.md`](THE_CRIMSON_CONCLAVE.md)):

- **The Seven Founding Houses** — Noctis, Valenor, Draven, Seraphe, Morcant, Kaelor, Vesper. Established the Conclave and codified the Veil. Older in lineage, narrower in current reach.
- **The Three Great Houses** — Vetharis, Drakmor, Soleth. Recognized later; rose during the wars of the Third Age to become the dominant powers of the current age.

"Founding" measures precedence; "Great" measures current power. The distinction is a live political wound — the Founding Houses have had centuries to resent the reversal and have not finished.

No house rules the others. See [`VAMPIRE_POLITICS.md`](VAMPIRE_POLITICS.md) for the full relationship web.

## The Three Great Houses

*Summaries only. Full canonical detail: [`docs/world/vampire_houses.md`](../../vampire_houses.md).*

| House | Philosophy | Lord | Sphere | Internal fault line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vetharis** — The Hand Beside the Throne | Political manipulation: influence, placement, information — never open force | Cassiel | Capital Province and major trade cities; agents in guilds, councils, courts | Loyalist Core vs. the Impatient Faction (Sera Vaine) |
| **Drakmor** — The Credible Threat | Military force: all political arrangements rest on who can hurt whom | Morreth | The Frontier and border regions; fortified positions at the continent's edges | Old Guard vs. the Expansionists |
| **Soleth** — The Memory That Outlasts | Ancient knowledge and vampire society's survival: what is remembered can be survived | The Archivist | Hidden places — archives beneath ruins, Iron Peaks deep passes, Sol Kareth; the Great Library arrangement ([`docs/lore/GREAT_LIBRARY.md`](../../../lore/GREAT_LIBRARY.md)) | Preservationists vs. those who would spend the archive |

> **Naming note for writers:** House Soleth (vampire) is distinct from any similarly named mortal noble family. Never conflate the two; a mortal-facing Soleth identity is a cover identity and must be flagged as such.

## The Seven Founding Houses

*Summaries only. Full profiles (history, leadership, philosophy, views, allies, rivals, traditions, architecture, heraldry, gameplay purpose, Conclave relationship): [`FOUNDING_HOUSES.md`](FOUNDING_HOUSES.md).*

| House | Identity | Motto | Story use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Noctis** — The First Shadow | The oldest surviving House; origins lost even to other vampires; members rarely appear at all | *"Before the light, we were."* | Deepest witnesses to the earliest ages; once-per-campaign encounters |
| **Valenor** — The Open Hand | Scholars and diplomats; hidden archives rivaling the Great Library; collectors of forgotten history | *"What is spoken need not be spilled."* | Diplomacy, mediation, the second archive; friendliest door into vampire society |
| **Draven** — The Old Standard | Old military lineage; renowned strategists; order through strength, strength in service of order | *"Order outlives the ordered."* | Honor-bound allies/antagonists; the counterweight to Drakmor |
| **Seraphe** — The Listening Order | Mystics and researchers of ancient magic; quietly investigate the Forgotten Eighth | *"The song is not finished."* | The vampire thread of the revelation arc; mystical quests |
| **Morcant** — The Web Between | Political manipulators; influence mortal noble houses from the shadows; brokers of secrets | *"Every secret has a price. We hold the ledger."* | Espionage and information brokering; the second political door |
| **Kaelor** — The High Silence | Hunters in remote wilderness estates; strict honor; respect for nature; avoid politics | *"We owe the world nothing but our absence."* | Isolationist encounters; honor dilemmas; sanctuary at a price |
| **Vesper** — The Quiet Ledger | Merchants; secret trade routes; rare artifacts; continent-spanning information networks | *"All things move. We decide the route."* | Relic recovery, secret routes, patrons; the economic door |

## Design Rules for All Houses

1. Every House possesses internal disagreement — no monolithic houses.
2. No House is purely good or purely evil, and no House exists solely as an antagonist or ally. Each represents a distinct philosophy on immortality, secrecy, and civilization's future.
3. Reputation is tracked per house ([`docs/systems/reputation.md`](../../../systems/reputation.md)). The three Great Houses have full reputation tier tables; the seven Founding Houses use narrative standing bands until individual tables are authored.
4. **Ten is the canonical number of surviving seated houses.** New houses may not be added without a Conclave-recognition storyline and updates to this roster, [`FOUNDING_HOUSES.md`](FOUNDING_HOUSES.md), [`THE_CRIMSON_CONCLAVE.md`](THE_CRIMSON_CONCLAVE.md), and [`VAMPIRE_POLITICS.md`](VAMPIRE_POLITICS.md). Houses may be *discovered to have fallen*, but new survivors require strong justification.
5. Every House defines: crest, colors, motto, architecture, traditions, ceremonies, etiquette, hierarchy, notable families, and famous historical members ([`VAMPIRE_CULTURE.md`](VAMPIRE_CULTURE.md)). Where not yet authored, that content is an open TODO, not an invitation to improvise silently.
