# The Seven Founding Houses

> **Canon Status:** Confirmed — established July 2026.
> **Authority:** Canonical full profiles of the seven Founding Houses of the Crimson Conclave. The three Great Houses (Vetharis, Drakmor, Soleth) are profiled in [`docs/world/vampire_houses.md`](../../vampire_houses.md); the full ten-house roster overview is in [`HOUSE_PROFILES.md`](HOUSE_PROFILES.md).
> **Cross-references:** [`THE_CRIMSON_CONCLAVE.md`](THE_CRIMSON_CONCLAVE.md) · [`THE_VEIL.md`](THE_VEIL.md) · [`VAMPIRE_CULTURE.md`](VAMPIRE_CULTURE.md) · [`VAMPIRE_HISTORY.md`](VAMPIRE_HISTORY.md)

Every House entry follows the canonical design rules: history, leadership, philosophy, view of humanity, view of the Divine Chorus, view of the Long Decline, allies, rivals, traditions, architecture, heraldry, gameplay purpose, and relationship to the Crimson Conclave. **No House exists solely as an antagonist or ally.** Each represents a distinct philosophy on immortality, secrecy, and civilization's future.

---

## House Noctis — The First Shadow

- **History:** The oldest surviving House; its history predates most modern kingdoms, and its true origins are unknown even to the other Houses. Perhaps a dozen members remain. Members rarely appear in public — or anywhere.
- **Leadership:** Unknown. No confirmed head of house exists in any other House's records.
- **Philosophy:** *Endure. Observe. Say nothing that is not necessary.*
- **View of humanity:** Weather. Watched, never argued with.
- **View of the Divine Chorus:** Noctis has never been recorded expressing one — which Seraphe finds more interesting than any position would be.
- **View of the Long Decline:** They have watched declines before. They do not say which ones.
- **Allies / Rivals:** None and none. Indifference from a great height; the Archivist of Soleth has received exactly two replies in a century of correspondence — both corrections to his archive.
- **Traditions:** Unknown beyond fragments. What is confirmed: they answer violations of the oldest taboos, and their memory of debts is perfect.
- **Architecture / Heraldry:** The few confirmed sites are ruins predating current record. Heraldry: a black field, unmarked — the absence of device *is* the device. **Motto:** *"Before the light, we were."*
- **Gameplay purpose:** The deepest witnesses to the earliest ages and the origin mystery — and the least willing to talk. A Noctis encounter is a once-per-campaign event.
- **Conclave:** Holds the first Founding Seat. It has stood empty longer than most mortal dynasties have existed; the seat remains theirs without dispute.

## House Valenor — The Open Hand

- **History:** Founded in the era of the Conclave's establishment as the Houses' diplomats and record-keepers; collectors of forgotten history whose hidden archives rival the Great Library — a claim Soleth disputes with the irritation of a rival who suspects it is true.
- **Leadership:** A head of house advised by a council of archivist-elders. *(Named leadership to be authored — see Task #141.)*
- **Philosophy:** *"What is spoken need not be spilled."* Scholarship and diplomacy over force, always.
- **View of humanity:** Colleagues who die too soon. Valenor genuinely likes mortals, which other Houses consider its charm and its weakness.
- **View of the Divine Chorus:** Respectful scholarly interest; Valenor archives Chorus theology as carefully as any other history.
- **View of the Long Decline:** Preservationist — civilization can and should be helped to endure.
- **Allies:** Draven (the old bloc: Valenor negotiates, Draven guarantees); cordial with Vesper and Seraphe; the only House Kaelor answers.
- **Rivals:** Soleth — archive rivalry wrapped in courtesy; they correspond constantly and cite each other never.
- **Traditions:** Inviolate couriers; consent traditions around the turning; guest wings that see actual use.
- **Architecture / Heraldry:** Open manor houses with famous libraries. A silver quill crossed with an olive branch on deep blue.
- **Gameplay purpose:** Diplomacy and mediation quests; the second archive when the Soleth path is closed; the friendliest door into vampire society.
- **Conclave:** The Conclave's institutional heart — Valenor Elders have hosted more sessions than any other House and usually hold the pen when consensus is drafted.

## House Draven — The Old Standard

- **History:** Old military lineage; produced renowned strategists and generals, several of whom served mortal armies under mortal names in wars whose causes no longer exist.
- **Leadership:** A Marshal of the House chosen by its officer families. *(Named leadership to be authored — see Task #141.)*
- **Philosophy:** *"Order outlives the ordered."* Order is maintained through strength — but strength exists to serve order, not replace it.
- **View of humanity:** Worth defending; mortal soldiers especially are regarded with professional respect.
- **View of the Divine Chorus:** A command structure they can respect; Draven keeps the Chorus's feast days out of old discipline.
- **View of the Long Decline:** Order can hold the line. Whether anything is behind the line is a question for other Houses.
- **Allies:** Valenor (the old bloc); quiet mutual respect with Kaelor, expressed by not bothering each other.
- **Rivals:** **Drakmor** — the bitterest feud in vampire society, predating the Great Houses' rise: a disagreement between soldiers about what soldiering is for. Morreth: "a museum with a sword." Draven: "an army in search of a war worth fighting."
- **Traditions:** Oaths kept past the death of everyone who witnessed them; formal military dress of armies that no longer exist.
- **Architecture / Heraldry:** Old towers and keeps in perfect repair long after their wars ended. A grey tower beneath a broken crown on dark red.
- **Gameplay purpose:** Honor-bound allies or antagonists; military history quests; the counterweight when Drakmor moves.
- **Conclave:** The seat that enforces — when the Conclave's rare collective judgments need teeth, Draven has historically provided them.

## House Seraphe — The Listening Order

- **History:** Founded by mystics and researchers of ancient magic; keepers of questions other Houses will not ask. Some members quietly investigate evidence connected to the Forgotten Eighth and study the Final Verse.
- **Leadership:** A circle of senior mystics rather than a single head. *(Named leadership to be authored — see Task #141.)*
- **Philosophy:** *"The song is not finished."* Understanding is the only inheritance worth centuries.
- **View of humanity:** Fellow listeners with shorter attention spans.
- **View of the Divine Chorus:** The House's central subject — approached with reverence, rigor, and extreme care, aware of the Chorus's single historical intervention.
- **View of the Long Decline:** A symptom of something older and less understood; treat the cause, not the symptom.
- **Allies:** Soleth — the closest active inter-house relationship; the Archivist exchanges materials with Seraphe more readily than with anyone. Buys fragments from Vesper and Morcant, knowing copies are kept.
- **Rivals:** None formal; Vetharis monitors them nervously — a House that studies the Forgotten Eighth could bring the wrong attention on everyone.
- **Traditions:** Layered robes in midnight colors; the veiled star worn as a small pin; a mortal lifetime of devotion required before the turning.
- **Architecture / Heraldry:** Estates with observatories, meditation halls, and libraries of forbidden theology. A seven-pointed star, one point veiled, on midnight violet.
- **Gameplay purpose:** The vampire thread of the Great Forgetting / Forgotten Eighth revelation arc; mystical quests; the origin-of-vampirism mystery ([`BLOODLINES.md`](BLOODLINES.md)).
- **Conclave:** Attends faithfully, speaks rarely, and is listened to when it does — the seat most likely to raise the question no one wants asked.

## House Morcant — The Web Between

- **History:** Political manipulators since their founding; masters of diplomacy who influence mortal noble houses from the shadows through intermediaries who rarely know whom they serve.
- **Leadership:** A head of house whose identity is itself treated as tradable information. *(Named leadership to be authored — see Task #141.)*
- **Philosophy:** *"Every secret has a price. We hold the ledger."*
- **View of humanity:** Customers, assets, and occasionally artists worth patronizing.
- **View of the Divine Chorus:** Uninterested — gods keep no ledgers Morcant can read.
- **View of the Long Decline:** Pragmatist — decline is a market condition; position accordingly.
- **Allies:** Transactional ties with Vesper (goods and information move on the same routes, by wary arrangement); sells to Seraphe.
- **Rivals:** **Vetharis** — rivals in the same trade by opposite methods: Vetharis embeds inside institutions, Morcant brokers from outside them. Each is the other's most attentive customer through cutouts both pretend not to recognize.
- **Traditions:** Ledgers of favors kept the way Soleth keeps archives; famously good at dressing as someone else's servant.
- **Architecture / Heraldry:** Nothing under their own name — rented rooms, borrowed houses, third-party holdings. A single silver diagonal thread on black-green.
- **Gameplay purpose:** Espionage and information-brokering quests; the second political door when Vetharis standing is burned.
- **Conclave:** Treats sessions as the continent's best marketplace; Morcant's Elder is suspected of representing more interests than one.

## House Kaelor — The High Silence

- **History:** Hunters who withdrew to remote wilderness estates — high valleys of the Iron Peaks and the Frozen Highlands — generations before the Great Houses rose, and have avoided politics whenever possible since.
- **Leadership:** The eldest of the house's hunting lodges speaks for it, on the rare occasions it speaks. *(Named leadership to be authored — see Task #141.)*
- **Philosophy:** *"We owe the world nothing but our absence."* Strict honor; respect for nature; the hunt as discipline rather than indulgence.
- **View of humanity:** Neighbors best served by distance — with one absolute exception: a traveler who reaches a Kaelor gate in winter will be fed, sheltered, and never fed upon. Guest-right is absolute.
- **View of the Divine Chorus:** The mountains were here before the songs; Kaelor keeps older courtesies.
- **View of the Long Decline:** The wild endures; civilizations are weather. Withdrawal is not despair — it is patience.
- **Allies:** Answers Valenor's letters out of courtesy and no one else's at all; wordless respect with Draven.
- **Rivals:** Drakmor probes their valleys occasionally and is repelled with precise, minimal force.
- **Traditions:** The formal hunt; duels under witnessed rules; debts honored across centuries; heavy, plain, old dress whose quality is visible only up close.
- **Architecture / Heraldry:** High mountain estates behind closed gates — austere, weatherworn, immaculate. A white peak over a closed gate on slate grey.
- **Gameplay purpose:** Isolationist encounters; honor-dilemma quests; sanctuary in hostile terrain at the price of Kaelor's exacting code.
- **Conclave:** Attends rarely; when a Kaelor Elder does appear, the matter is Veil enforcement, and their vote is never in doubt.

## House Vesper — The Quiet Ledger

- **History:** Merchants since their founding — builders of secret trade routes that outlasted the kingdoms they crossed. Acquirers of rare artifacts; maintainers of information networks spanning the continent.
- **Leadership:** A Factor-General elected by the house's senior trading families. *(Named leadership to be authored — see Task #141.)*
- **Philosophy:** *"All things move. We decide the route."* Wealth is the only power that survives every regime.
- **View of humanity:** Partners — the best trade requires living markets, which makes Vesper quietly pro-civilization out of pure self-interest.
- **View of the Divine Chorus:** Insurance is respected in all its forms.
- **View of the Long Decline:** Bad for business; therefore to be slowed. Vesper funds universities and sponsors archaeological expeditions anonymously — partly patronage, partly first refusal on whatever is dug up.
- **Allies:** Working arrangements with nearly everyone — Valenor (provenance and authentication), Seraphe (artifact fragments), Morcant (a wary goods-for-information exchange). The House that trades with Houses that will not meet.
- **Rivals:** Vetharis and Morcant both regard Vesper's networks as encroachment on their trades; Drakmor taxes routes it can reach, which Vesper prices in rather than contests.
- **Traditions:** Contracts honored to the letter across centuries — Vesper's word is its collateral; heraldry never shown on a route, only at the table.
- **Architecture / Heraldry:** Counting-houses and warehouses under mortal-facing names; a single true seat whose location is a house secret. A gold scale-beam over a crescent on deep umber.
- **Gameplay purpose:** Rare-artifact and relic-recovery quests; access to secret routes; the economic door into vampire society; patron NPCs.
- **Conclave:** The indispensable seat — Vesper finances the sessions, carries the correspondence, and abstains from most judgments; neutrality is the product it sells.
