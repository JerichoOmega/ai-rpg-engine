## Core Philosophy

Every faction answers one question: **How does this group believe civilization should survive the Long Decline?** No faction is simply good or evil; every one has understandable goals, strengths, flaws, and internal disagreements — and believes it is preserving civilization even when its methods conflict.

## Tier I — Continental Powers

### The Imperial Council
- **Type:** Continental government — the federal body of the Age of Restoration, consciously modeled on the First Council of the First Empire ([`docs/lore/civilization/FIRST_COUNCIL.md`](../../lore/civilization/FIRST_COUNCIL.md)).
- **Structure:** Representative council: delegates from every recognized kingdom, province, race, and major territory. No emperor. No continental king. Member realms — including Queen Merveth Aldenmoor's Capital Province — retain full internal sovereignty. Full structure: [`GOVERNMENT.md`](GOVERNMENT.md).
- **Responsibilities:** Maintain peace · coordinate diplomacy · build infrastructure · maintain trade · protect civilization (continental law, defense coordination).
- **Strengths:** Stable government, professional administration, infrastructure, diplomacy.
- **Weaknesses:** Political deadlock, regional disagreements, bureaucracy, corruption.
- **View of the Divine Chorus:** Respected protectors of cosmic order; not expected to solve mortal politics.
- **View of the Long Decline:** The greatest threat facing civilization — though many members underestimate its true cause.
- **Player interaction:** Political quests, regional disputes, council decisions, diplomatic missions.

### The Great Library
- **Type:** Academic institution. **Authoritative document:** [`docs/lore/GREAT_LIBRARY.md`](../../lore/GREAT_LIBRARY.md).
- **Purpose:** Preserve all knowledge. **Canonical motto:** *"Knowledge belongs to tomorrow. What is forgotten belongs to oblivion."*
- **Responsibilities:** History, maps, languages, ancient civilizations, magical research, preservation.
- **View of the Long Decline:** Knowledge lost can never truly be recovered.
- **Player interaction:** Historical investigations, lore, journal integration, artifact recovery, scholar quests. Field operations: the Library Expedition Corps ([`MILITARY_ORDERS.md`](MILITARY_ORDERS.md)).

### The Covenant
- **Type:** Continental religious organization — the Solari Covenant in its institutional role. **Authoritative documents:** [`docs/world/religions.md`](../religions.md) · [`docs/world/covenant_inquisitors.md`](../covenant_inquisitors.md). Structure and detail: [`RELIGIOUS_ORDERS.md`](RELIGIOUS_ORDERS.md).
- **Purpose:** Maintain temples, protect shrines, teach the traditions of the Divine Chorus, preserve sacred history.
- **View of the Divine Chorus:** Cosmic caretakers rather than miracle workers.
- **View of the Long Decline:** An existential and spiritual crisis.

### The Imperial Army
- **Type:** Continental military, under the Military High Command (Marshal Edric Voss — [`docs/lore/IMPERIAL_CAPITAL.md`](../../lore/IMPERIAL_CAPITAL.md)). Detail: [`MILITARY_ORDERS.md`](MILITARY_ORDERS.md).
- **Purpose:** Protect civilization, defend trade, respond to monster threats, assist frontier settlements.
- **Branches:** Infantry · Engineers · Scouts · Medical Corps · Frontier Rangers.

## World State Integration (Required)

Every faction must respond dynamically to changes in the World State (canonical system: [`docs/design/world_state/`](../../design/world_state/README.md)). Canonical examples:

| Faction | Response to World State shifts |
|---|---|
| **Imperial Council** | Redirects aid; strengthens defenses; debates emergency measures |
| **Great Library** | Sends archivists to endangered regions; prioritizes preserving threatened knowledge |
| **Covenant** | Organizes relief efforts; restores shrines; supports local communities |
| **Merchant Guild** | Changes caravan routes; responds to economic instability |
| **Builders' Guild** | Repairs roads; restores bridges; rebuilds damaged settlements |
| **Frontier Rangers** | Increase patrols; investigate new corruption zones; warn settlements |

NPC dialogue, quests, settlements, and environmental storytelling should visibly reflect these changes.

## Reputation (Required)

Every major faction maintains its own reputation with the player — trust, political influence, access to services, discounts, special quests, unique dialogue, story branching. **Never a single universal meter.** Authoritative system: [`docs/systems/reputation.md`](../../systems/reputation.md); principles: [`FACTION_REPUTATION.md`](FACTION_REPUTATION.md).

## Canonical Design Rules

Every new faction must define: Purpose · History · Leadership · Core Values · Allies · Rivals · View of the Divine Chorus · View of the Long Decline · Gameplay Function · Relationship to the World State. No faction exists solely as exposition or decoration.
