# The Great Guilds

> **Canon Status:** Confirmed — established July 2026.
> **Authority:** Canonical profiles of the five great trade guilds. The **Mages Guild** and **Adventurers Guild** are established canon with their own reputation tracks ([`docs/systems/reputation.md`](../../systems/reputation.md)); they are summarized here for the continental picture and are not redefined. Economic detail defers to [`docs/systems/factions_economy.md`](../../systems/factions_economy.md).
> **Cross-references:** [`FACTION_BIBLE.md`](FACTION_BIBLE.md) · [`FACTION_RELATIONSHIPS.md`](FACTION_RELATIONSHIPS.md) · [`GOVERNMENT.md`](GOVERNMENT.md)

Each guild defines purpose, history, leadership, responsibilities, relationships with the Imperial Council and the Great Library, and gameplay interactions.

---

## Merchant Guild

- **Purpose:** Keep goods moving — caravans, contracts, market law, and the trust that makes a stranger's coin good.
- **History:** Grew from post-Sundering caravan compacts: merchants who pooled guards and honored each other's debts because no one else would.
- **Leadership:** An elected Guildmaster and a council of route-masters; wealth votes, but so does mileage.
- **Responsibilities:** Caravan scheduling and protection contracts, market arbitration, standard weights and measures, credit letters (with House Caelum).
- **Imperial Council:** Its most persistent lobby — road law, tariff harmony, and relief for trade disruptions. The Council needs the Guild's logistics in every crisis and resents needing them.
- **Great Library:** Buys maps and route intelligence; donates trade records that have become one of the Library's best historical sources.
- **Gameplay:** Escort and logistics quests, market events, trade-war intrigue, caravan travel infrastructure. **World State:** reroutes caravans and reprices risk visibly — closed routes and empty stalls are how players read economic decline.

## Smiths' Guild

- **Purpose:** Uphold the standards of metalwork — from horseshoes to siege steel — and the licensing of forges.
- **History:** The oldest charter among the five; its mark-and-standard system predates the Restoration and survived the Sundering in the Iron Peaks trade towns.
- **Leadership:** A Forgemaster-General elected by master smiths; regional forge-councils below.
- **Responsibilities:** Quality marks, apprenticeship law, arms contracts (with the Army), price floors that keep village smiths alive.
- **Imperial Council:** Supplies the Army and the Builders' programs; in return defends its licensing monopoly fiercely whenever the Council flirts with open forging.
- **Great Library:** Quietly deposits master techniques against loss — the Guild learned from the Sundering that skills die faster than books.
- **Gameplay:** Crafting and upgrade services, materials quests, arms-supply intrigue between houses and realms. **World State:** forge output shifts from tools to arms as regions destabilize — a readable signal.

## Alchemists' Consortium

- **Purpose:** Regulate the making and sale of alchemical goods — medicine, reagents, and the things that should not be sold at all.
- **History:** Founded as a mutual-protection pact after a poisoning scandal nearly got every alchemist in the Capital hanged; self-policing was the price of survival.
- **Leadership:** A rotating Board of Assessors; secretive by charter, which everyone finds suspicious and no one has fixed.
- **Responsibilities:** Licensing, formula registries, poison law compliance, plague response stockpiles.
- **Imperial Council:** Valued in crisis (plague, blight, Corruption symptoms), watched in peace; the Council keeps a standing committee on Consortium oversight that the Consortium keeps outmaneuvering.
- **Great Library:** Tense partnership — the Library wants formulas preserved; the Consortium wants them controlled. They compromise: sealed deposits, opened by joint consent.
- **Gameplay:** Potions and remedies, ingredient-gathering quests, poisoning mysteries, plague/Corruption response arcs. **World State:** stockpiles and shortages; black-market leakage grows as regions weaken ([`SECRET_ORGANIZATIONS.md`](SECRET_ORGANIZATIONS.md)).

## Explorers' Guild

- **Purpose:** Chart the continent's dangerous and forgotten places; license and outfit expeditions.
- **History:** Born in the early Restoration from the surveyors who remapped a continent the Sundering had rearranged.
- **Leadership:** A Guildmaster-Cartographer and a fellowship of senior expedition leaders; prestige counts more than coin.
- **Responsibilities:** Maps and surveys, expedition licensing, waystation registries, first-contact protocols for uncontacted communities.
- **Imperial Council:** Contracted for surveys and infrastructure routing; occasionally embarrassing to it, when an expedition finds something a realm preferred lost.
- **Great Library:** **Constant partnership** — the Library funds expeditions, the Guild delivers discoveries; the Library Expedition Corps ([`MILITARY_ORDERS.md`](MILITARY_ORDERS.md)) and the Guild share members, rivalries, and bar tabs.
- **Gameplay:** Exploration quests, ruin licenses, maps as items and unlocks, expedition patronage. **World State:** expedition range contracts as danger grows; lost-expedition rescues are a canonical quest seed.

## Builders' Guild

- **Purpose:** Build and keep the physical continent — roads, bridges, walls, aqueducts, settlements.
- **History:** The Restoration's workhorse; its founding charter was literally a reconstruction contract for the Capital's walls.
- **Leadership:** A Master of Works elected by the senior lodges; regional lodges hold real autonomy.
- **Responsibilities:** Public works, construction standards, disaster response, the road-and-bridge maintenance the Council funds and the Guild delivers.
- **Imperial Council:** Its favorite instrument — infrastructure is the Council's most visible success, and the Guild builds all of it. **Close cooperation with the Merchant Guild** is canonical: trade pays for roads, roads carry trade.
- **Great Library:** Deposits surveys and structural archives; borrows ancient engineering the Library recovers from ruins.
- **Gameplay:** Settlement rebuilding arcs, infrastructure quests (the visible repair of the world), siege and disaster response. **World State:** the most visible responder — repaired bridges and rebuilt villages are the player-facing proof that resilience is winning.

## Established Guilds (Existing Canon — summarized only)

- **Mages Guild** — the arcane institution; magical services, research politics, and Eleanor's faction. Includes the canonical contact channel with House Soleth's sphere via Senior Scholar Aldric Wenne ([`docs/world/vampire_houses.md`](../vampire_houses.md)). Reputation track exists.
- **Adventurers Guild** — the neutral trade organization for quest work, information, and contracts; the player's default institutional home. Reputation track exists.

Both predate this document; nothing here alters them.

## Design Rules

1. Guilds are continental institutions with regional lodges — they cross realm borders, which makes them natural Council allies and realm irritants.
2. Every guild answers the Long Decline through its trade: movement, craft, remedy, discovery, or repair. Guild quests should make the player *feel* civilization being held together by working people.
3. Inter-guild relationships in [`FACTION_RELATIONSHIPS.md`](FACTION_RELATIONSHIPS.md) are canon; keep them consistent.
