## Combat

### Combat Style
Swords as a human; transforms into werewolf form for combat as well. The werewolf form is significantly more powerful but carries inherent risk.

### Primary Weapon
Two short swords (human form)

### Special Ability / Mechanic
**Werewolf Form** — Transforms into a werewolf, dramatically increasing combat strength. Silver is his major weakness in this state and in general.

---

## Personality

### Core Personality
Depressed and withdrawn. He does not believe there is a place in the world for someone like him. Stays away from people to avoid the risk of losing control. Not unkind — simply convinced that isolation is the only responsible choice given his curse.

### Strengths
- Extremely powerful in werewolf form
- Intelligent

### Weaknesses
- Silver — his primary vulnerability
- Poor social skills; unintentionally embarrasses himself in social situations
- The curse itself — the constant fear of losing control

### Fears
That he will lose control and hurt innocent people. This fear shapes every decision he makes about where to go and who to be near.

### Goals
To rid himself of the werewolf curse entirely.

### Relationship Tendencies
Reserved. Will offer brief opinions when asked but defaults to keeping people at a distance. Not hostile — just convinced that connection puts others at risk.

## Appearance

### Visual Appearance
Same height as Eleanor. Blonde hair with a scruff on his face.

### Voice / Way of Speaking
More timid than one might expect. Soft-spoken.

## Background

### Background Summary
One night, while drinking with friends around a fire, a werewolf attacked the group. Ronan was the only survivor — or so he believed. He has been a drifter since, never staying in one place long enough to build anything.

He is smart but lacks social skills, often unintentionally saying or doing things that embarrass him in company. Loves gardening — it is what genuinely gives him peace of mind — but having no home means he no longer gets to do it.

### Potential Character Arc
Two possible resolutions: he removes the curse, or he keeps it and learns to control it. A significant revelation: he was not the only survivor of the werewolf attack. Someone else lived. A romance develops with Eleanor, who offers to help him find a cure.
