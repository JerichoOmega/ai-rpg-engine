## Combat

### Combat Style
Fast paired **twin short swords** as a human; transforms into **Werewolf Form** —
significantly more powerful, harder to control, and carrying real cost. Silver is
his major weakness in both states.

### Special Ability / Mechanic
**Werewolf Form** — controlled escalation. Mechanics: [`docs/heroes/RONAN.md`](../heroes/RONAN.md)
· [`docs/COMBAT_SYSTEM.md`](../COMBAT_SYSTEM.md).

## Personality

### Core Personality
**Surface:** quiet, observant, reserved, dry humour, patient, self-reliant. His
silence reads as **thoughtfulness, not distance.** **Underneath:** extremely
compassionate, fiercely loyal, protective, gentle with children and animals — and
**he doesn't recognise how much good he does for others.** A good man carrying a
heavy burden — **not** an edgy antihero. (Full: [`RONAN.md`](../heroes/RONAN.md).)

### Recurring Daily Behaviours *(canonical — see hero bible)*
Takes the **last watch**, **eats last**, **counts the party**, **walks the
outside** (between the group and danger), **never wastes food**, and quietly leaves
**anonymous kindness** for struggling villagers without taking credit.

### Nature & Animals
An **experienced woodsman first, a werewolf second** — reads wildlife, weather,
tracks, and edible plants fluently. Animals **trust** him: birds land nearby, dogs
settle, horses calm.

### Strengths
- Formidable in Werewolf Form; skilled duelist; expert survivalist/tracker.
- Never leaves anyone behind — his **greatest strength.**

### Weaknesses
- **Silver** — his primary vulnerability.
- The curse itself and the fear of losing control.

### Fears
- **Conscious fear:** losing control of the transformation with someone he loves
  too close to get clear.
- **Deeper fear (Canon v1.2):** waking up one day **no longer needing other
  people** — and becoming alone again.

### Goals
To find a way to live with (or lift) the curse — and, more quietly, to keep the
people he has found.

## Appearance

Lean, athletic, travel-worn; **5'6" (167 cm)**. **Deep reddish-brown hair with
copper undertones**, medium length with natural waves; short matching scruff;
**amber-yellow eyes.** Signature **oversized weathered hooded cloak** (repaired
countless times — a blanket, shelter, sling, and comfort item on the road). Carries
a symbolic, nonmagical **seed pouch**; later, a carved wooden **Pack Token** ties
beside it. Full visual canon: [`Characters.md#ronan`](../canon/Characters.md#ronan).

### Voice / Way of Speaking
Economical and precise; soft-spoken; dry, self-aimed humour. Even tone with a
tired-but-not-hopeless undercurrent.

## Party Dynamics

- **Eleanor** — his emotional counterpart; a romance can develop. She never says
  *"the wolf,"* only *"you."* (See [`The Lost Howl`](../quests/the_lost_howl.md) ·
  [`Ronan–Eleanor Banter`](../design/companions/ronan_eleanor_banter.md).)
- **Talos** — begins wary (protective concern, not personal); over time takes the
  watch himself, sensing Ronan needs someone else to carry the burden occasionally.
- **Torren** — keeps trying to make Ronan laugh, and eventually succeeds; a
  recurring source of lighthearted party banter.
- **Ragash** — judges Ronan **only by his actions**; mutual respect develops with
  little explanation needed.
- **Party Healer** — recognises that Ronan is not afraid of becoming a monster, but
  of **losing people**; one of the strongest emotional bonds in the party.
  *(Healer identity `_TBD_`.)*

## Legacy Development Notes

> Preserved for history. **Superseded by current canon** where they conflict; do
> not treat as authoritative.

- **Hair:** an early concept described Ronan with **blonde hair**. Superseded —
  canonical hair is **deep reddish-brown with copper undertones**
  ([`Characters.md#ronan`](../canon/Characters.md#ronan)).
- **Early personality framing:** he was originally written as *"depressed and
  withdrawn."* Current canon reframes this as **quiet, guarded, and burdened but
  warm underneath** — thoughtful, not hollow. His arc is belonging and chosen
  family, not despair.
- **Early background beat:** an early draft had Ronan turned when a werewolf
  attacked friends around a campfire, with a later revelation that *another
  survivor lived.* Current canon leaves his origin and the untold-scar an
  **intentional mystery** (`_TBD_`, [`RONAN.md`](../heroes/RONAN.md)); use that
  framing unless a future revision canonises the campfire origin.
- **Gardening:** the humanising detail that Ronan loves gardening dovetails with
  his canonical **seed pouch** (*"something beautiful can still grow, even in
  darkness"*).
- **Roster:** Steven was retired per `LEGACY_CHARACTER_RESERVE.md`; **Torren**
  fills the party role.

## Notes

- Twin-short-sword style (human) vs. the raw power of Werewolf Form mirrors who he
  tries to be versus what the curse makes him.
- The seed pouch and, later, the Hidden Pack's carved **Pack Token** are his paired
  symbols: **hope** and **belonging** (see [`The Lost Howl`](../quests/the_lost_howl.md)).
