## Combat

### Combat Style
One-handed weapon and shield. Built for sustained, defensive melee combat.

### Primary Weapon
Long sword with shield.

### Special Ability / Mechanic
**Battlecry** — Boosts stats for party members and unsettles enemies. Functions as a battlefield rallying tool.

**Shield Stance** — As a shield-bearing hero, Talos has access to the Shield Stance system. He may spend his Support Action to raise his shield and enter a persistent frontal defense stance, gaining increased block chance, reduced frontal damage, and access to shield-specific Reactions. Lowering the shield is free and restores the Support Action to normal use on that turn. See [`docs/COMBAT_SYSTEM.md`](../COMBAT_SYSTEM.md) for full rules.

## Personality

### Core Personality
Jaded from years of constant war that has taken friends and loved ones. On the surface: guarded, prideful, bitter at times. Underneath: a genuine sweetheart — especially with children, regardless of their race.

### Strengths
- Physically powerful, built from years of combat and training
- Deep loyalty once trust is earned
- Carries a strong moral compass independent of authority

### Weaknesses
- Extremely difficult to earn his trust — years of dealing with people who had their own agendas have made him suspicious
- His pride can make him difficult to work with before a bond is formed

### Fears
Has a deep fear of snakes.

### Goals
Do right by people — not governments. Actively protect innocent people while confronting his inner demons.

### Relationship Tendencies
Hard to reach at first. Once trust is established, he becomes a devoted and fiercely protective companion. Trust must be *earned*, not assumed.

---

## Appearance

### Visual Appearance
Tall and heavily built from constant training. Scars cover his body from years of active combat.

### Voice / Way of Speaking
Deep tone. Can be heartfelt or bitter depending on the conversation. Very prideful. Does not hide his feelings, but chooses his words with weight.

---

## Background

### Background Summary
Grew up on the border — his family had just enough to get by, and he watched neighbors go through far worse. This built an instinct to help his community. Grew up in a multi-race community; racial prejudice is a dealbreaker for him. Former military who has since walked away from institutional service.

Loves wine. Favorite food is roasted duck. Carries a bead bracelet — each bead gifted by a child he has met on his travels.

### Potential Character Arc
Fighting governments for how they treat their people. Confronting the personal cost of years of war.
