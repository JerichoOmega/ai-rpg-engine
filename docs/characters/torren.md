---

## Combat

### Combat Style
Deliberate and powerful. Uses his forging hammer with the weight and intent of someone who has been swinging it for decades. Does not chase targets — reshapes the battlefield so that enemies cannot easily exploit allies, and so that the party can hold positions they should not be able to hold.

### Primary Weapon
Forging Hammer (Warhammer). A craftsman's tool as much as a weapon. It has been repaired more than once.

### Special Ability / Mechanic
**Field Forge**
- Creates functional battlefield structures during combat
- Constructs are handcrafted (not magical); they persist across turns and affect positioning
- Available constructs: defensive barricades, spike barriers, forge beacons, supply caches, portable anvils, reinforced walls, defensive towers, ballistae
- Construct type and placement can reshape the geometry of an entire encounter

### Specialization Paths
| Path | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Forge Master** | Ally enhancement; equipment reinforcement; repairs; buffs |
| **Engineer** | Battlefield construction; defensive structures; terrain manipulation |
| **War Smith** | Hammer mastery; armor breaking; crowd control; frontline durability |

## Personality

### Core Personality
Calm, patient, reliable. Speaks after listening rather than instead of listening. Quietly humorous — his jokes arrive after a pause and are worth the wait. Not loud. Not boastful. The companion who was quietly indispensable and you only realize it after the fact.

### Strengths
- Equipment repair and enhancement
- Battlefield construction and terrain control
- Sustained combat endurance
- Reading what can and cannot be salvaged
- Party support through sustained presence

### Weaknesses
- Does not abandon things easily; occasionally holds on longer than is tactically wise
- Less effective at range
- Not built for speed or agility

### Philosophy
*"Broken things aren't worthless. They simply require someone willing to rebuild them."*

He applies this equally to weapons, armor, relics, buildings, and people.

### Goals
To leave things in better condition than he found them — not abstractly, but specifically, concretely, piece by piece.

### Relationship Tendencies
Becomes the party's dependable foundation. Companions tend to notice that their gear is in better shape than expected, that there is usually a defensible position when one is needed, and that Torren said something useful long after everyone else finished arguing.

---

## Appearance

### Visual Appearance
Broad-shouldered; the build of a lifelong smith. Hands that show decades of work — calloused, scarred, burn-marked. Practical clothing reinforced at the joints. Carries a portable forge kit that should be too heavy to travel with.

### Voice / Way of Speaking
Measured and unhurried. Shorter sentences after longer pauses. His humor is dry, late-arriving, and worth waiting for.

---

## Background

### Background Summary
Torren has worked with metal his entire life — building forges, repairing weapons under siege conditions, constructing fortifications in the middle of active fighting. What drove him to join the party is connected to something he has not discussed in detail: a past failure that built the philosophy he now carries. He believes broken things can be rebuilt because he has learned what it costs when you believe that and are wrong.

### Potential Character Arc
Torren's arc confronts his core belief under conditions that genuinely test whether it is true. The resolution will require him to decide what restoration actually means: fixing the original thing, accepting the loss, or building something new from what remains.

## Party Dynamics

- **Talos:** Mutual recognition between two people who have dedicated their lives to doing difficult work correctly. Neither announces this; both notice.
- **Eleanor:** A shared frame around restoration and harmony makes their conversations more productive than the surface difference between magic and craft would suggest.
- **Ragash:** She teaches him that imperfection is not simply damage. He teaches her that patience is a craft skill. Both change as a result.
- **Ronan:** *"Broken steel can be reforged."* Torren does not try to fix Ronan. He simply does not accept that being scarred means being beyond repair.

---

## Notes

- Torren's three specialization paths are **Forge Master**, **Engineer**, and **War Smith**. Players cannot unlock all three; every investment should matter.
- Field Forge constructs should feel handcrafted by a legendary smith — never magical summons, never advanced technology.
- His campfire presence (repairing equipment before anyone wakes, working quietly by firelight) is part of the game's atmospheric texture.
- His Core Wound involves a specific past failure — defined in spirit, not yet fully authored as a personal quest.
- Torren replaces Stephen, who has been retired from the canonical companion roster.
