## Combat

### Combat Style
Uses his blue pickaxe in surprisingly effective ways. Focuses on utility, resourcefulness, and exploiting terrain rather than formal combat techniques.

### Primary Weapon
Blue pickaxe.

### Special Ability / Mechanic
**Master Miner**
- Can discover hidden passages
- Can uncover buried treasure
- May reveal secret rooms and shortcuts
- Provides bonuses when gathering resources

---

## Personality

### Core Personality
Quiet, friendly, patient, and impossible to read. Despite never speaking, he somehow always seems to understand what is going on around him.

### Strengths
- Resource gathering
- Exploration
- Trap detection
- Finding hidden paths
- Survival skills
- Surprisingly resilient

### Weaknesses
- Cannot communicate verbally
- Poor at diplomacy
- Often underestimated
- Does not explain himself

### Fears
Being trapped underground with no way out.

### Goals
To find the perfect mining spot and uncover whatever lies beneath the world.

### Relationship Tendencies
Gets along with most companions simply because he is difficult to dislike. Often becomes an unexpected source of comfort within the party over time.

---

## Appearance

### Visual Appearance
Wears a worn blue t-shirt, faded jeans, and sturdy boots. Always carries his bright blue pickaxe. Often covered in dirt and stone dust.

### Voice / Way of Speaking
**Mute.** Communicates through gestures, expressions, drawings, and occasionally tapping patterns with his pickaxe.

---

## Background

### Background Summary
Almost nothing is known about Steven. He simply appears in certain regions of the world — mining tunnels, caves, and ruins. Travelers tell stories of a silent man with a blue pickaxe who always seems to be exactly where hidden treasures are found. No records of his birthplace, family, or affiliations exist anywhere.

### Potential Character Arc
Despite never speaking, Steven slowly becomes an important member of the party. Companions learn to understand his gestures over time, and he develops deep friendships without ever saying a word.
