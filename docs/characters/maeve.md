## Personality
**Surface:** gentle, patient, motherly, calm, warm.
**Hidden:** carries tremendous grief; rarely forgives herself; pushes herself too
hard; finds it difficult to accept help.

## Signature Items
- **Lantern of Ashes** — simple brass lantern (not magical); she lights it wherever
  hope must be rebuilt. Her defining symbol and a nighttime sign of safety.
- **Field Journal** — patient notes, remedies, pressed flowers, children's drawings,
  letters, and the names of those she lost. Records how people *lived*, not died.

## Signature Habits
Makes tea every evening; repairs clothing; replaces empty bandages; checks everyone
without being asked; leaves supplies anonymously; watches over the party as they
sleep; notices unmet needs before anyone speaks.

## Exploration & Combat
In settlements: checks the sick, helps children, visits local healers, gathers
herbs, assists elders, leaves medicine anonymously — NPCs recognise her reputation.
In combat: battlefield medicine, healing, protection, morale, civilian rescue,
preventing deaths.

## Core Wound
In the war she could save only one of two critically wounded patients. One lived,
one died; she remembers both names and keeps them in her journal. Her arc: letting
others carry her sometimes.

## Party Dynamics
- **Talos** — two who carry the cost for others; she tends what his duty leaves.
- **Eleanor** — radiant hope meets practised, hands-on hope.
- **Ronan** — names his real fear (losing people) and never flinches.
- **Torren** — the smith mends things, she mends people; warm jokes over tea.
- **Corwin** — he names the wound, she tends it.
- **Ragash** — quietly tends the hounds; Ragash never forgets it.

## Canonical Quote
> *"People rarely remember the medicine. They remember the person who stayed."*
