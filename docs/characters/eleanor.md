## Combat

### Combat Style
Spell-based. Ranged elemental casting. Eleanor's defining combat trait is her ability to cast multiple different elements — unusual even among experienced mages.

### Primary Weapon
Staff / Wand / Orb / Crystal (flexible; magic-focus items)

### Special Ability / Mechanic
**Rare Elemental Casting** — Can pull off powerful, rare spells that require an unusually strong connection to the elements. Only someone with her particular bond to the elements could use them.

---

## Personality

### Core Personality
Young, bubbly, and genuinely optimistic about the world. More positive than almost anyone else in the party. Deeply family-oriented.

### Strengths
- Unnaturally strong elemental bond — capable of wielding multiple elements simultaneously
- Sincerely good-hearted; easy to work with

### Weaknesses
- Very naive and too trusting of people
- Rare and powerful spells exhaust her quickly
- Combining multiple elements in a single spell makes it more unstable — more powerful but harder to control

### Fears
Letting her family down. She genuinely believes there are plenty of good people in the world — this faith makes betrayal hit harder.

### Goals
Advance through the ranks of the Mages Guild, as most of her family has done before her. Make her family proud.

### Relationship Tendencies
Trusts people too easily. This is both a warmth and a vulnerability.

---

## Appearance

### Visual Appearance
Young. Red hair. Mid-height.

### Voice / Way of Speaking
Has a slight Irish accent. Sounds hopeful — there is optimism in how she speaks, even in serious moments.

## Background

### Background Summary
Most of Eleanor's family are powerful mages. Her life's ambition is to reach the highest rank in the Mages Guild. She loves music quite a lot.

### Potential Character Arc
She is betrayed by someone she trusted — which could result in her being expelled from the Guild. She refuses to join a cult that a close relative becomes involved with. This could ultimately lead to a confrontation between them, with only one possible victor.
