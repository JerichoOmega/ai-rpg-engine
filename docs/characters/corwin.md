## Personality
**Surface:** curious, friendly, talkative, optimistic, observant.
**Hidden:** self-critical, constantly second-guessing, afraid of overlooking danger;
believes the smallest clue can save lives. A **terrible liar** with **childlike
curiosity.**

## Signature Habits
Always sketching; countless notebooks; collects unusual plants; studies footprints;
fascinated by wildlife; easily distracted by discoveries.

## Signature Items
- **Surveyor's Lens** — inherited brass field lens (not magical).
- **Green Journal** — lifelong field guide: sketches, maps, corruption notes,
  theories, unanswered questions. He hopes someone continues it after him.

## Exploration & Combat
Notices tracks, corruption, clues, traps, hidden passages, unusual plants, signs of
civilization — teaching the player to read the world. In combat: reveals hidden
enemies, exposes weaknesses, battlefield observation, tracking, trap detection.

## Core Wound
He once investigated early signs of corruption; his evidence was ignored or hidden,
and harm followed. He has blamed himself since. His arc: forgiveness, and learning
that truth spoken with care is enough even when unheeded.

## Party Dynamics
- **Talos** — respects his diligence and heeds his warnings.
- **Eleanor** — shared curiosity; she reframes his self-blame as care.
- **Ronan** — quiet mutual trust; Ronan steadies him wordlessly.
- **Torren** — *measure before you judge* rhymes with *truth is a kindness*.
- **Ragash** — tracker & naturalist swap sign; blunt respect.
- **Maeve** — he names the wound, she tends it.

## Canonical Quote
> *"The smallest clue can save the most lives."*
