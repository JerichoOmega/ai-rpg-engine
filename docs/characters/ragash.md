## Combat

### Combat Style
Primarily melee-focused but capable with a bow as well. Her hounds extend her presence on the battlefield.

### Primary Weapon
Longsword / Bow (dual capability)

### Special Ability / Mechanic
**Hound Summon** — Can summon one of her trained hounds to aid in combat. Her hounds are individually significant — she has raised them since they were pups.

---

## Personality

### Core Personality
Very prideful. Not afraid to speak her mind. She has spent her life being underestimated and has stopped caring what most people think of her because of it. Has more emotional investment in her hounds than in most people.

### Strengths
- Complete command over her hounds — a bond built from years of raising and training them personally
- Tactically flexible; capable at multiple ranges

### Weaknesses
- Physically weaker than most orcs — she was born as the runt of her clan
- Her directness drives people away; even other orcs often want nothing to do with her

### Fears
Losing one of her hounds. She raised them since they were pups — they are her world.

### Goals
To find a rare mythical breed of hounds long thought extinct, in order to raise and train some of her own.

### Relationship Tendencies
Will always speak her mind. Because of this, most people — especially other orcs — keep their distance. She has learned not to expect otherwise.

---

## Appearance

### Visual Appearance
Green skin. Shorter than most orcs — she is the runt of her clan. Has a scar across one eye from her first hound, which she raised herself.

### Voice / Way of Speaking
Deep but more reserved than her boldness might suggest. Has a sweet undertone beneath the directness.

---

## Background

### Background Summary
Ragash is the runt of her clan, which led to her being looked down upon from birth. Because no one was ever there for her, she stopped caring what others think. Her hounds are the constant — the only relationships she has fully invested in.

### Potential Character Arc
She finds the rare breed she has spent years searching for. Her first hound — the one who gave her the scar — dies.
