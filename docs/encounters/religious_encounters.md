# Religious Pilgrimage and Shrine Encounters

> **Canon Status:** [CONFIRMED — established July 2026]  
> **Authority:** This document defines canonical religious encounter content for travel across all six regions. All AI DM implementations of pilgrimage and shrine encounters must draw from these definitions.  
> **Cross-references:** [`docs/world/WORLD_BIBLE.md`](../world/WORLD_BIBLE.md) · [`docs/world/religions.md`](../world/religions.md) · [`docs/world/cults.md`](../world/cults.md) · [`docs/systems/reputation.md`](../systems/reputation.md)

## Overview

Two travel encounter categories — **Pilgrims** (Social Encounters) and **Hidden Shrines** (Discovery Encounters) — are the primary vectors through which religion enters exploration. This document defines the specific content behind those encounter entries.

Both types support the full range of player approaches: combat, diplomacy, investigation, stealth, and avoidance. Encounters marked **[QUEST HOOK]** can open into a fuller questline. Encounters marked **[REPUTATION]** affect standing with the associated religious order.

---

## Part I — Pilgrimage Encounters

Pilgrims are traveling for spiritual reasons. They are not threats, but they are not neutral either — they carry beliefs, affiliations, information, and sometimes secrets. Meeting pilgrims on the road is one of the most natural points of entry into religious politics.

### General Pilgrimage Rules

- Pilgrims are always affiliated with one of the four religions. Their affiliation should be visually and conversationally apparent.
- Pilgrims may be traveling alone, in small groups (3–8), or as part of larger organized processions (12–30+).
- Harming pilgrims causes significant reputation damage with the associated religious order and may trigger responses from that order in future encounters.
- Helping pilgrims — providing protection, information, or supplies — generates modest reputation gains and may open quest hooks.

---

### The Solari Covenant — Pilgrimage Encounters

The Covenant's most significant pilgrimage destination is the **Radiant Spire** in The Capital Province. Covenant pilgrims are the most organized and politically connected of all pilgrim groups.

---

#### SC-P1: The Sunward Procession
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** The Frontier, Sol Kareth, The Capital Province (arrival)  
**Scale:** Large procession (15–30 pilgrims)

A formal Covenant procession traveling to the Radiant Spire for the Festival of First Light. The procession is led by a **Senior Canon** in full ceremonial dress. A small honor guard of four Covenant soldiers accompanies them.

**What the players observe:**
- Travelers in matching cream-and-gold pilgrim robes walking in organized formation
- A gilded reliquary box carried by two attendants (contains a relic of debated authenticity)
- The procession moves slowly; the Canon stops at each roadside shrine to perform a brief blessing rite

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Observe and pass | Procession continues without incident |
| Speak with pilgrims | Pilgrims are friendly but slightly suspicious of non-Covenant travelers; willing to share road conditions ahead |
| Request to travel with them | Permitted if the party's reputation is neutral or positive; provides road safety and Covenant social access at destination |
| Offer service (escort, scouting) | [REPUTATION] Modest gain with The Covenant Clergy; Canon notes the party's names |
| Investigate the reliquary | Canon becomes defensive; insisting triggers reputation loss; a successful Deception or Stealth approach may reveal the relic is a reproduction |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The procession was larger yesterday; three pilgrims disappeared overnight near an abandoned waystation. The Canon is not yet certain whether they deserted or were taken. He will not delay the procession but would pay well for information — or the pilgrims' safe return.
- **[QUEST HOOK]** One of the pilgrim attendants passes the party a folded note when no one is looking. It reads: *"The Canon is not what he says. The reliquary carries more than faith. Don't let it reach the Spire."*

---

#### SC-P2: The Lone Penitent
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** The Frontier, The Capital Province, Sol Kareth  
**Scale:** Single individual

A Covenant penitent traveling alone, barefoot, wearing undyed linen rather than pilgrim robes. Penitents undertake pilgrimage as an act of penance — self-imposed hardship in response to a personal failing or a judgment from the Covenant hierarchy.

**What the players observe:**
- A person of any age walking without supplies, carrying only a small prayer disk
- Visibly tired, possibly injured feet from walking without shoes
- Will not initiate conversation but will respond if spoken to

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Offer food or water | Accepted with deep gratitude; penitent considers this a sign from the Radiant One |
| Offer shoes or healing | [REPUTATION] Accepted after hesitation; reputation gain with The Covenant Clergy |
| Ask what they did | Penitent may share, may refuse; the answer is always personal and sometimes significant |
| Offer to escort them | Welcomed; penitent will talk during travel |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The penitent confesses to witnessing something they should have reported and didn't — a Covenant Canon meeting privately with someone the penitent couldn't identify in a location that made no sense. The penance is for their silence. They're now not sure silence was the greater sin.
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The penitent is traveling to confess to a crime they committed under orders — orders from a Covenant superior they can name. They expect to be punished. They've made peace with it. They hand the party a sealed letter: "If I don't arrive, see that someone reads this."

---

#### SC-P3: The Blessing Merchants
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** Sol Kareth, The Capital Province  
**Scale:** Small group (4–6)

A group of Covenant clergy traveling between cities specifically to sell religious services: blessings of new businesses, naming rites for children whose parents couldn't travel to a temple, Solar Investiture for minor officials. They are friendly, commercially minded, and slightly uncomfortable being called merchants.

**What the players observe:**
- A small wagon with Covenant symbols; two clergy in formal but not ceremonial robes; two lay assistants
- Stopping at farmsteads and small settlements along the route
- Visibly prosperous; the blessing trade is good

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Purchase a blessing | Available; various costs; minor in-game mechanical benefit depending on type |
| Engage in conversation | Clergy are chatty and opinionated; excellent sources of regional gossip and Covenant internal politics |
| Question their methods | Triggers a polished theological defense; they have heard every objection |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** One of the lay assistants pulls the party aside. The clergy have been collecting something else at each stop: names. Lists of households that don't observe Covenant calendar events, that bury their dead without the Naming Rite, that murmur about the Old Ways. The assistant doesn't know what the lists are for. They're frightened. *"This doesn't feel like ministry anymore."*

### The Old Ways — Pilgrimage Encounters

Old Ways pilgrimage is not organized in the Covenant sense. Practitioners walk to sacred sites alone or in small groups, following routes passed down through oral tradition rather than institutional infrastructure. Old Ways pilgrims often look indistinguishable from ordinary travelers until they speak.

---

#### OW-P1: The Grove Walkers
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** The Great Forest, The Frontier  
**Scale:** Small group (3–7)

A group of practitioners making a seasonal journey to a sacred grove. They travel quietly, observing the landscape as they walk — this is part of the practice, not merely the journey.

**What the players observe:**
- Travelers carrying no obvious pilgrim markers; identified by the way they move through the landscape (stopping to observe, touching trees and rocks, noting animal behavior)
- One senior practitioner; the others are younger and learning the route
- Friendly toward travelers who treat the landscape respectfully

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Walk with them | Welcome if the party is not visibly destructive; practitioners will share plant lore and route knowledge |
| Ask about the grove | Described honestly; location shared if the party seems trustworthy |
| Ask about The Corruption | [REPUTATION] Genuine grief; they will share what they've observed; reputation gain with The Grove Keepers if the party engages seriously |
| Mention the Covenant | Cool reception; not hostile unless the party is aggressive |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The grove they're heading to is one the senior practitioner has walked to for thirty years. Last season, something changed — the grove felt wrong. She spent the winter trying to decide whether to return or not. She's returning. She'd welcome company she trusts at her back.
- **[QUEST HOOK]** A younger member of the group has been receiving what they describe as "wrong impressions" from the landscape — images and feelings that don't belong to any natural source they've been taught to recognize. The senior practitioner is alarmed but hasn't said so. She quietly asks the party if they've encountered anything similar elsewhere.

---

#### OW-P2: The Solitary Keeper
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** The Great Forest, The Frontier, The Frozen Highlands  
**Scale:** Single individual

A Grove Keeper traveling alone to maintain a remote sacred site. These individuals may be days or weeks from the nearest Old Ways community and are entirely self-sufficient.

**What the players observe:**
- A solitary figure, well-equipped for extended wilderness travel
- Carrying tools for practical work (clearing brush, repairing dry stone features, carrying specific plants)
- Watchful but not hostile

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Pass without interaction | Keeper watches but does not engage unless spoken to |
| Ask about the road ahead | Detailed and accurate; Keeper knows this landscape intimately |
| Ask about the sacred site | Described generally; full details require demonstrated trustworthiness |
| Offer help | [REPUTATION] Accepted if the party's conduct warrants it; reputation gain with The Grove Keepers |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The Keeper arrived at the site three days ago to find it had been used — not by practitioners, by someone else. Fire had been set in the wrong place. Symbols were carved into the oldest tree. The Keeper has been sitting with this for three days and cannot explain what was done here or why. *"Old Ways markings, but twisted. I don't know what I'm looking at."*
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The Keeper is not actually going to the site to maintain it. They're going to determine whether it should be closed — formally removed from the network of active sacred sites — because something is wrong with it and they don't know if it can be fixed. This is a decision they don't want to make alone.

---

#### OW-P3: The Seasonal Gathering
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** The Frontier, The Great Forest  
**Scale:** Large group (20–50)

Players encounter the road approaching a seasonal gathering point — an equinox or solstice site where Old Ways communities from across a region come together. The gathering itself is already underway; the road is busy with travelers heading to and from it.

**What the players observe:**
- Unusual foot traffic on otherwise quiet roads; families, small communities, individual travelers all moving in the same direction
- A faint smell of woodsmoke and cooking food from ahead
- Gathering site visible once they crest a hill: a natural clearing, a central fire, people camped around it

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Attend the gathering | Welcome as guests if the party is not hostile; significant social encounter; major information source |
| Observe from a distance | Possible; party gains some information about Old Ways practice |
| Avoid the area | Simple; no consequence |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** At the gathering, the party overhears (or is approached about) a significant dispute: a practitioner has shown up claiming that a sacred grove in a specific location has been destroyed — burned by parties unknown. The community is divided between those who want to investigate and those who believe it is too dangerous. They could use outside help.
- **[QUEST HOOK]** An elder at the gathering asks to speak with the party privately. She's identified something in the Corruption's effect on regional plant life that follows a geographic pattern — she can show them on a rough map. She needs someone to go to three locations and confirm what she suspects. *"I'm too old for that walk, and my people can't leave the gathering. But this matters."*

### The Ancestors' Path — Pilgrimage Encounters

Ancestors' Path pilgrimage is clan business more than individual spiritual practice. Groups travel to significant ancestral sites: burial halls, clan founding locations, sites of famous deeds. The journey itself is often ceremonially structured.

---

#### AP-P1: The Remembering Party
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** The Iron Peaks, The Frozen Highlands  
**Scale:** Medium group (8–15, typically a clan family)

A family group — dwarves or Highlands folk — making the journey to a clan ancestor site for The Remembering ceremony. They travel with a clan elder who leads the ritual recounting.

**What the players observe:**
- A multigenerational family group moving with clear purpose
- The elder is treated with deference; younger members carry the elder's pack as well as their own
- The group is in a state of emotional preparation — quieter than travelers usually are

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Greet respectfully and pass | Acknowledged with courtesy |
| Ask about their journey | Explained openly; the tradition is not secret |
| Ask about the ancestor being honored | Elder decides whether to share; success depends on the party's comportment |
| Offer escort | [REPUTATION] Considered; accepted if the party seems trustworthy; reputation gain with The Ancestor Speakers |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The elder is troubled. At the last three Remembering ceremonies, contact with the ancestors has been — wrong. Not absent, but distorted; the responses don't fit the ancestors being invoked. She hasn't told the family. She would very much like to speak with someone who might have experienced something similar elsewhere, or who might know what's happening to the connection between the living and the dead.
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The family's destination is a clan site in contested territory — a pass that was recently taken over by a Stonefang Tribe warband. They have a right to that site older than any goblin claim. They are going anyway. The elder has already said what she will do if blocked. The younger members are frightened. They would be grateful for company that might prevent bloodshed.

---

#### AP-P2: The Diaspora Pilgrim
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** The Capital Province, The Frontier, Sol Kareth  
**Scale:** Single individual or small family (1–3)

A dwarven pilgrim far from the Iron Peaks, making a private journey to a site connected to their family's history in this region — a battle where an ancestor fell, a forge their ancestor helped build, a waystone their clan erected.

**What the players observe:**
- A dwarf (or small dwarven family) who clearly doesn't belong to this region's demographic patterns
- Carrying a small ancestor shrine box — a portable version of the household shrine — treating it with visible care
- Consulting what appears to be a hand-drawn map and matching it against the landscape

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Offer directions | Gratefully received; good opportunity to learn the ancestor's history |
| Ask about the shrine box | Explained if the party is respectful; the box contains tokens of the specific ancestor |
| Accompany them to the site | [REPUTATION] Welcomed; reputation gain with The Ancestor Speakers if the site is reached safely |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The site on the map no longer exists — something was built over it, or it was destroyed. The pilgrim is sitting with this. They ask the party: if you had come this far to honor a dead person and found nothing, what would you do? They're not actually asking rhetorically.
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The pilgrim found the site — and found it occupied. Someone is using the location for purposes that are clearly not ancestral veneration. Symbols that don't belong to the Ancestors' Path. Evidence of recent activity. The pilgrim photographically remembers everything they saw but has no framework for understanding it. The party might.

---

#### AP-P3: The Trial Escort
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** The Iron Peaks, The Frozen Highlands  
**Scale:** Small group (5–8)

A group of adult clan members escorting a young person to complete their Trial of Blood — the coming-of-age ceremony that proves they carry their ancestors' qualities. The specific trial varies by clan. The escort is present to witness and record, not to help.

**What the players observe:**
- A mixed group of adults and one young person (12–16) who carries themselves with deliberate composure
- The adults maintain ceremonial distance from the young person during the journey
- The group declines help with travel logistics; they carry their own water and food even when offered

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Ask about the trial | Adults explain; the young person does not speak to outsiders until the trial is complete |
| Offer supplies or shelter | Politely declined; accepting would compromise the trial |
| Observe the trial site (if nearby) | Permitted as long as the party stays back |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The trial requires the young person to spend a night alone at a specific location. That location is not safe right now — the escorts know this but haven't found a solution that doesn't dishonor the trial. They would not accept direct help. But if the area happened to be safer by nightfall for unrelated reasons, that would be acceptable.
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The young person approaches the party privately during a rest stop. They're terrified. Not of the trial, but of something they saw at the trial site on a scouting pass: evidence that it's been used recently, not by their clan. They don't want to dishonor their family by failing, but they're not sure the location is what it was supposed to be.

### The Veiled Order — Pilgrimage Encounters

The Veiled Order does not conduct pilgrimage in the conventional sense. Its members travel to specific locations for archival, research, and vigil purposes. These journeys have a pilgrim-like quality — purposeful, solitary, spiritually motivated — but would not be recognized as such by practitioners of other faiths.

---

#### VO-P1: The Archival Expedition
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** All regions (near significant ruins, battlefields, or historical sites)  
**Scale:** Small group (2–4)

A Veiled Archivist traveling with one or two assistants to document a site of significant death or historical transition. They carry writing supplies, preservation materials, and a distinctive black-bound record book.

**What the players observe:**
- Quiet, focused travelers who notice more than most
- Wearing gray or black; Order members do not advertise their affiliation but don't conceal it if asked directly
- Examining the landscape, making notes, occasionally pausing for extended observation

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Speak with them | Remarkably well-informed; will share what they know if the conversation is substantive |
| Ask what they're doing | Explained honestly; *"We record what happened here, so that it isn't lost."* |
| Share information about the site | [REPUTATION] Welcomed; reputation gain with The Veiled Archivists if the information is genuine and new |
| Ask about The Corruption | Considered; the Order is cautious about sharing their conclusions; trusted parties receive more |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The Archivist's record book contains notes from a previous expedition to this area — by an Order member who disappeared three years ago. The notes are incomplete. They stop mid-sentence. The Archivist is here partly to finish the documentation and partly to determine what happened to their predecessor.
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The site they're documenting has records of a pattern the Archivist has now seen in five different locations. Something is visiting these sites — not randomly. She shows the party a map with the locations marked and asks if they've passed through any of the others. The pattern, if completed, points to a sixth location that doesn't appear in any current document.

---

#### VO-P2: The Vigil Keeper
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** All regions (near settlements, battlefields, sites of significant recent death)  
**Scale:** Single individual

An Order member who has heard that someone is dying nearby — or arrived at a site after a significant death — and has come to perform the Vigil. They sit with the dying, record their final words, and maintain the boundary between life and death with practiced attention.

**What the players observe:**
- A solitary figure sitting outside a home, a hospice, or near a site of recent violence
- Not praying in any visible sense; just present and attentive
- Will speak if spoken to, but will not leave before the Vigil is complete

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Ask who is dying | Answered honestly if the person is not under Order confidentiality |
| Ask what the Order does | Extended, thoughtful explanation; the Order member is not evangelical but not secretive |
| Ask what the dying say | *"Some things said at the threshold are shared. Most are not. The distinction matters."* |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The dying person spoke something in the Vigil Keeper's presence that the Keeper cannot explain and cannot simply archive and move on from. They ask the party if they can repeat it aloud: a specific phrase in a language the Keeper recognizes as very old — but the dying person had no way of knowing that language. *"This is not the first time I've heard this phrase at the threshold. I need to know where it's coming from."*
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The person who died is associated with something the players are already investigating. The Vigil Keeper documented their final words. Order confidentiality would normally prevent sharing. But the Keeper has concluded that in this specific case, confidentiality may be enabling a harm that the Order's principles don't permit. She will share — but needs the party to understand what they're agreeing to do with the information.

---

#### VO-P3: The Boundary Reader
**Encounter type:** Social  
**Regions:** The Frontier (battlefields), The Great Forest (corrupted areas), The Iron Peaks (ruins)  
**Scale:** Single individual

A Veiled Order practitioner traveling specifically to locations where the boundary between states of being is thin — to practice Boundary Reading and extract information from the site itself.

**What the players observe:**
- A person standing very still in a location that seems unremarkable — an abandoned crossroads, a ruined foundation, the site of an old battle
- Eyes open but unfocused; clearly in an internal state
- Not in danger; will return to ordinary awareness naturally

**Interaction options:**

| Approach | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Wait and speak after | Practitioner emerges slowly; sometimes disoriented; grateful for patient company |
| Interrupt | Practitioner is shaken back to full awareness; not angry, but the reading is lost |
| Ask what they experienced | Shared selectively; some of what they perceive at boundary sites is too fragmented to communicate directly |

**Quest hooks:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The reading showed the practitioner something they cannot account for: the site's historical record of deaths is being overwritten by something recent. Old deaths are being replaced by impressions of something that hasn't happened yet. The practitioner is not sure whether this is a property of the site, a property of the Corruption, or a property of their own deteriorating perception.
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The practitioner has been to nine boundary sites in this region and each one has shown the same anomaly: a directional pull. Something is drawing the thin-place energy in a specific direction. She has a rough bearing. She doesn't know what's at the end of it. She needs someone to go look.

## Part II — Hidden Shrine Encounters

Hidden shrines are sacred sites that are not maintained by institutional clergy or widely advertised. They exist because practitioners built them, because a location was sacred before institutions existed, or because the terrain made an official site impossible. Some are actively maintained; some are ancient and partly forgotten; some have been corrupted.

### General Shrine Rules

- Shrines are discovery encounters — the player must find them to trigger the encounter.
- Each shrine encounter includes what the player finds, what the shrine does (mechanical effect options), and what questlines or lore the shrine can open.
- Shrines can be in a clean state, a neglected state, or a corrupted state. The state affects all outcomes.
- Leaving a maintained shrine without acknowledgment has no effect. Defiling a shrine has reputation consequences.

---

### The Solari Covenant — Hidden Shrines

Covenant shrines outside major cities are typically roadside waypoints: carved stone plinths or niches in rock faces, marked with solar discs or the Radiant One's flame symbol. Maintained shrines often have fresh offerings — small coins, candle stubs, dried flowers.

---

#### SC-S1: The Roadside Sun-Niche
**Found in:** The Frontier, The Capital Province, Sol Kareth  
**State variants:** Maintained / Neglected / Defaced

A carved niche in a natural rock face or a worked stone plinth beside a well-traveled road. A stylized solar disc marks it as Covenant. Older shrines have layers of accumulated offerings.

**Maintained state:**
- Recent offerings present; candle flame if protected from wind
- Interacting with the shrine in good faith: minor blessing (next Covenant-affiliated encounter begins with +1 disposition)
- [REPUTATION] Leaving an offering: small reputation gain with The Covenant Clergy

**Neglected state:**
- No fresh offerings; shrine itself intact; road traffic has passed this by
- Restoration is possible; takes an hour of work; triggers reputation gain
- Represents the Covenant's weakening reach in this area — narrative information

**Defaced state:**
- Solar disc scratched out or covered in ash markings
- Evidence suggests deliberate desecration, not mere neglect
- Restoring a defaced shrine: [REPUTATION] significant gain with The Covenant Clergy
- **[QUEST HOOK]** Pattern: if the party has seen more than one defaced shrine, a Covenant cleric they encounter later will recognize the ash markings as specific — the symbol of the Ashen Tribunal, the radicalized Covenant cult. The cult is marking territory.

---

#### SC-S2: The Waystation Shrine
**Found in:** The Capital Province, Sol Kareth  
**State variants:** Maintained / Disputed

A larger Covenant shrine built into an existing waystation — a stone building with a sleeping room, a water source, and a small shrine chamber. Covenant pilgrims use these as rest points on major pilgrimage routes.

**Maintained state:**
- A lay Covenant custodian may be present (not clergy; a local farmer or trader maintaining the station as an act of faith)
- Full services available: water, basic food, shelter, and a Covenant blessing from the custodian
- Excellent source of road information and Covenant news from recent travelers

**Disputed state:**
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The custodian is in a standoff with a local landowner who claims the waystation land is theirs and wants the Covenant out. The Covenant's regional chapter hasn't responded to the custodian's requests for help. The custodian is not leaving. The landowner is escalating. Both parties are in the wrong to different degrees. The party can adjudicate, negotiate, investigate the land claim's legitimacy, or walk away.

---

#### SC-S3: The Buried Solar Temple
**Found in:** Sol Kareth  
**State variants:** Undiscovered / Active (Order use) / Corrupted

An ancient shrine predating the formal Covenant — one of the original Sol Kareth sun-reverence sites that the Covenant later claimed as its own theological ancestor. Partially buried by sand; entrance requires excavation or local knowledge.

**Undiscovered state:**
- Finding requires a successful Investigation or Perception check; local guides may know of it
- Interior contains pre-Covenant solar iconography that differs from modern Covenant art in notable ways — evidence that the Covenant's claimed heritage is a political construction

**Active state:**
- A Covenant Scholar is already here, documenting — and uncomfortable. The iconographic differences are not supposed to be public knowledge. She will try to discourage the party from exploring further. Whether she succeeds depends on the party's approach.

**[QUEST HOOK]** The temple contains a sealed inner chamber. The seal is not Covenant work — it predates the Covenant by centuries. Something is inside that neither the Covenant's founders nor their predecessors fully understood. What is inside, and why it was sealed, is content for a dungeon encounter.

---

### The Old Ways — Hidden Shrines

Old Ways sacred sites are natural locations that have been recognized as spiritually significant through long use: a spring, a tree of unusual age, a stone formation, a place where animals consistently gather. The "shrine" may not look like a shrine — it looks like the natural world, tended.

---

#### OW-S1: The Tended Spring
**Found in:** The Great Forest, The Frontier  
**State variants:** Maintained / Corrupted

A natural spring that has been maintained as a sacred site — the area around it cleared of dead wood, offerings of stones and leaves arranged at the water's edge, a specific pattern of small stones marking it as known to practitioners.

**Maintained state:**
- Water is clean and pure; drinking from it provides minor restoration
- Evidence of recent maintenance: fresh arrangements, no debris
- [REPUTATION] Maintaining the site (removing dead wood, refreshing arrangements): small gain with The Grove Keepers
- A Grove Keeper may be present or may appear shortly after the party arrives

**Corrupted state:**
- Water discolored; natural-looking but something is wrong
- Animal tracks approach but do not reach the water's edge
- The stone markers are present but the pattern has been disturbed — not violently, but systematically altered
- **[QUEST HOOK]** Something is wrong with this site. The corruption is subtle — it doesn't look like the Corruption's obvious manifestations elsewhere. An Old Ways practitioner who hears about this will recognize the specific pattern of marker-alteration as a method described in oral tradition: it's how practitioners were taught to mark a site that has been spiritually compromised. Someone knew this was happening and marked it using Old Ways notation. Who? When? Why didn't they do more?

---

#### OW-S2: The Ancient Grove
**Found in:** The Great Forest, The Frozen Highlands  
**State variants:** Active / Dormant / Twisted

One of the Old Ways' sacred groves — a specific location, usually marked by trees of unusual age or formation, where practitioners believe the Living World's presence is especially concentrated.

**Active state:**
- Visibly distinct from surrounding forest: older trees, unusual quiet, a sense of attention
- A Grove Keeper is likely present or returns during the party's visit
- [REPUTATION] Entering respectfully and leaving the grove as found: small gain with The Grove Keepers
- Extended meditation here (one hour) may yield a fragmented vision or piece of environmental lore

**Dormant state:**
- The grove is intact but the quality the practitioners describe is absent — like a fire that has gone out
- This is exactly what Old Ways practitioners are experiencing across their network; this grove is one of the affected ones
- **[QUEST HOOK]** A practitioner who realizes the party has been here will react with grief and then with questions: what did the grove look like? Did anything seem present at all? They're mapping the spread of the dormancy. This location fills in a gap.

**Twisted state:**
- The trees are physically changed: unusual growth patterns, bark with an unnatural texture, silence that feels wrong rather than peaceful
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The twisted grove is the endpoint of a pattern that began elsewhere. Investigating the site — what changed, when, what triggered it — leads back through the grove network to a source. Finding the source is a dungeon encounter.

---

#### OW-S3: The Frontier Cairn
**Found in:** The Frontier  
**State variants:** Maintained / Neglected / Unknown purpose

A stone cairn in a field or at a crossroads — simple enough to be dismissed as a wayfinding marker, but built in the specific proportions and with the specific stone selection that mark it as an Old Ways site. Frontier farming families have maintained this for generations, often without knowing the full theological significance.

**Maintained state:**
- Fresh wildflowers at the base; small food offerings
- A nearby farm family can explain: *"We've always left offerings here. Don't know exactly why. It's just what you do."* — a perfect illustration of how Old Ways practice survives institutionally in rural communities
- Talking to the family is an entry point into Old Ways practices in this community

**[QUEST HOOK]** The farm family is worried — their youngest child has been drawn to the cairn repeatedly, spending hours there, sometimes overnight. The child says the cairn is talking to them. The family is caught between fear (is this Corruption?) and something else (could this be genuine?). An Old Ways practitioner would know how to evaluate this. The family doesn't know any practitioners.

### The Ancestors' Path — Hidden Shrines

Ancestors' Path shrines are found where ancestors have been, not where priests have built. A cave where a clan founder sheltered; a battlefield where a great ancestor fell; a mountain pass they opened. The shrines are often marked by portable ancestor-tokens left by previous pilgrims.

---

#### AP-S1: The Clan Waymarker
**Found in:** The Iron Peaks, The Frozen Highlands  
**State variants:** Active / Forgotten / Disturbed

A stone waymarker carved with a clan symbol — identifying this location as significant in that clan's ancestral history. Multiple waymarkers in an area indicate a complete ancestral route.

**Active state:**
- Recent ancestor-tokens present (small carved figures, locks of hair, name-inscriptions)
- An Ancestor Speaker may be present or conducting a private ceremony
- [REPUTATION] Leaving an appropriate offering: small gain with The Ancestor Speakers if a practitioner is present

**Forgotten state:**
- The clan symbol is real but the clan itself may be gone — scattered, destroyed, or fully absorbed into another lineage
- The oldest tokens are very old; no recent ones
- **[QUEST HOOK]** A dwarven NPC the party knows is looking for any information about their clan's history. The symbol here matches what they've described. The location itself is a piece of that history.

**Disturbed state:**
- Ancestor-tokens removed; the carved clan symbol has been partially defaced — not destroyed but marked with an additional symbol that doesn't belong
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The additional symbol is recognizable to an Ancestor Speaker as something specific: not random desecration, but a ritual claim being made over this site. Someone is trying to appropriate this location's ancestral significance for their own purposes. Who would do this, and why, is the investigation.

---

#### AP-S2: The Battle Memorial
**Found in:** The Iron Peaks, The Frontier, The Frozen Highlands  
**State variants:** Maintained / Neglected

A site where a significant battle was fought — an ancestor-significant location for multiple clans. Marked by stone obelisks or carved memorial walls listing the names of the honored dead.

**Maintained state:**
- Annual maintenance by Ancestor Speaker delegations; the site is periodically crowded
- A powerful location for ancestral contact; [REPUTATION] participating in a memorial ceremony here provides significant gain with The Ancestor Speakers
- Multiple clan perspectives on the same historical event are recorded here; conflicting interpretations coexist

**Neglected state:**
- The site has been abandoned due to the difficulty of reaching it or a conflict between the clans who should maintain it
- The deterioration is visible and is considered a serious ancestral dishonor
- **[QUEST HOOK]** Restoring the site — clearing it, repairing the carvings, reestablishing the maintenance agreement between the relevant clans — is a significant undertaking with significant rewards. The clans themselves are in a dispute that has made joint maintenance impossible. Brokering a resolution comes first.

---

#### AP-S3: The Deep Shrine
**Found in:** The Iron Peaks (underground)  
**State variants:** Active / Sealed / Corrupted

An ancient ancestor shrine built deep inside the Iron Peaks — the kind that has been maintained in the same location for centuries without interruption. The oldest deep shrines are among the most sacred sites in Ancestors' Path practice.

**Active state:**
- A permanent attendant (a senior Ancestor Speaker) maintains the shrine and guards against unauthorized entry
- Entry is possible with appropriate clan credentials or significant reputation with The Ancestor Speakers
- The shrine is a direct interface for ancestral contact; more reliable than household shrines for significant consultations

**Sealed state:**
- The attendant sealed the shrine — recently — because something changed. Ancestral contact from this location has become distorted; the attendant cannot in good conscience allow practitioners to use a contact point that may be compromised
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The sealed shrine is at the center of a crisis. Multiple clans depend on this shrine's contact. Its closure is causing governance problems. The attendant will not unseal it without understanding what changed. Understanding what changed requires investigating the shrine — and the investigation has already sent two people into something they couldn't explain.

**Corrupted state:**
- The shrine still looks intact but what comes through it is not ancestral
- Contact attempts produce responses that are almost right — almost ancestral — but wrong in ways that are hard to articulate and easy to miss if you're hoping to hear from a specific ancestor
- **[QUEST HOOK]** This is the false-ancestor phenomenon — the Corruption has learned to impersonate ancestral contact well enough to be believed. This specific shrine is where the deception was first identified. The practitioner who identified it is inside the shrine complex and has not come out. They went in to understand what they were dealing with.

---

### The Veiled Order — Hidden Shrines

The Veiled Order does not have shrines in the conventional sense — it has houses (their small libraries and vigil rooms) and it has boundary sites (locations where the membrane between states of being is naturally thin). The latter function as shrines for practical purposes.

---

#### VO-S1: The Order House
**Found in:** Major cities and some significant ruins in all regions  
**State variants:** Active / Closed / Abandoned

A small Veiled Order house — typically an unremarkable building marked only by a small carved symbol (a circle with a vertical line through it) above the door. Contains a library, a vigil room, and space for a handful of residents.

**Active state:**
- 1–3 Order members present; welcoming to serious visitors
- Library contains records of local deaths, documented threshold experiences, and archival material unavailable elsewhere
- [REPUTATION] Contributing new archival material: reputation gain with The Veiled Archivists
- Excellent information source for local history, regional deaths, and Corruption manifestations

**Closed state:**
- The house is locked; the symbol is present but no one answers
- The house was operating recently; the closure is unexplained
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The Order member who ran this house has not reported to their Order contacts in three weeks. The Order sent a message asking the party to look into it discreetly. The house's records are inside. What happened to the resident matters both personally and because they were working on something significant when they disappeared.

---

#### VO-S2: The Thin Place
**Found in:** Near battlefields, sites of mass death, significant ruins in all regions  
**State variants:** Natural / Monitored / Destabilized

A location where the boundary between states of being is naturally thin — recognized by Order practitioners through training, but sometimes perceptible to anyone with relevant sensitivity. No physical marker exists; the location is known only through Order records and word of mouth.

**Natural state:**
- Players with relevant traits (arcane sensitivity, near-death experiences) may feel the location is unusual before anyone tells them what it is
- An Order practitioner doing Boundary Reading here may be present
- Extended time at this location: fragmentary perceptions — images, sounds, impressions — that serve as lore delivery

**Monitored state:**
- An Order member has established a temporary camp here and is documenting the site systematically
- They've been here long enough to have opinions and observations; willing to share with trustworthy parties
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The readings have been consistent for years. Three months ago, they changed. The change correlates with a specific event — but the practitioner isn't sure which event because several significant things happened around the same time. They need someone to help them narrow it down.

**Destabilized state:**
- Something has disrupted the natural thin-place quality; the boundary has become thinner than it should be
- This is dangerous: entities or impressions from the other side of the boundary are becoming more present in ways that affect the physical environment
- **[QUEST HOOK]** The destabilization is spreading. What started at this location is propagating outward. The Order member who was monitoring here has gone silent. The party needs to find the source of the destabilization before the affected radius grows large enough to affect the nearest settlement.

---

#### VO-S3: The Unmarked Archive
**Found in:** Significant ruins in the Capital Province, the Iron Peaks, Sol Kareth  
**State variants:** Intact / Damaged / Compromised

A concealed archive — books, records, and artifacts — left by previous Order members in a location too significant to abandon but too dangerous to maintain openly. The archive's existence is known to current Order members but its contents have not been fully catalogued.

**Intact state:**
- Finding the archive requires knowing it exists (Order contact) or significant Investigation success
- Contains records from a period of historical significance: previous Corruption manifestations, deaths of notable figures, documented theological conflicts
- [REPUTATION] Reporting the archive's condition to The Veiled Archivists: reputation gain
- At least one item in the archive is relevant to the current campaign's central conflict

**Compromised state:**
- The archive has been found by someone else first. They've read some of it; taken some of it; left the rest in disorder
- **[QUEST HOOK]** What was taken is more significant than what was left. Whoever found this archive knew what they were looking for. That means they knew the archive existed, which means they have Order-level information. But the Order didn't send them. Who knew, and how, and what did they want with specific records from several centuries ago?

## Part III — Corruption-Influenced Shrine Encounters

These encounters represent shrines that have been taken over by cults or directly affected by the Corruption. They are not legitimate religious sites in their current state — but they often look like legitimate sites until the player investigates.

---

### General Corruption-Shrine Rules

- Corruption-influenced shrines always have surface-level appearance of a legitimate shrine for the relevant religion
- Players who investigate carefully will find inconsistencies; players who interact without investigation may be deceived
- These shrines can be restored to legitimate use through investigation and specific actions — restoration is always a possible outcome
- Cult affiliation is traceable through consistent symbol usage; a party that has seen multiple corrupted shrines may recognize patterns

---

### CS-1: The Ashen Mark
**Associated cult:** The Ashen Tribunal (Solari Covenant radicalization)  
**Found in:** The Frontier, The Capital Province  
**Surface appearance:** A standard Covenant roadside shrine

At a glance: a Covenant shrine in reasonable condition. Closer inspection:

| Observation | What it indicates |
|---|---|
| Ash residue on the solar disc rather than candle wax | Fire was used here for purposes other than illumination |
| The offering dish contains ash mixed with bone fragments | Not standard Covenant practice |
| A secondary mark scratched beneath the solar disc | Ashen Tribunal symbol — difficult to notice unless the party has seen it before |
| The shrine's stone is slightly warm | Even when no candle has recently burned |

**What happened here:** An Ashen Tribunal operative used this shrine for a marking ritual — establishing this location as part of their network. The shrine has not been converted entirely; it can still function as a legitimate Covenant shrine. But it is now flagged in the Tribunal's system.

**Player options:**

| Action | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Report to a Covenant cleric | [REPUTATION] Significant gain with The Covenant Clergy; cleric is alarmed and asks follow-up questions |
| Remove the secondary mark | Disrupts the Tribunal's network; they will notice the break |
| Leave it but note the location | Information for later; the shrine continues serving dual purpose |
| Investigate the source | Tracks to a specific Tribunal operative in the region |

**[QUEST HOOK]** This is the third such marked shrine the party has encountered. The marks form a geographic pattern. What they mark — the territory? a route? a boundary? — is unclear. An Ancestor Speaker, an Old Ways practitioner, or a Veiled Archivist can help interpret the pattern if the party brings them observations.

---

### CS-2: The Severance Grove
**Associated cult:** The Severance (Old Ways radicalization)  
**Found in:** The Great Forest, The Frontier margins  
**Surface appearance:** A sacred grove with maintenance signs

At a glance: an Old Ways sacred grove, recently tended. Closer inspection:

| Observation | What it indicates |
|---|---|
| The maintenance removes rather than integrates — dead wood isn't scattered, it's piled | Fuel, not returned earth |
| Specific plants have been selectively removed from the grove | These are the calming and balancing plants in Old Ways lore; their removal is doctrinally significant |
| New carvings in the oldest trees — not traditional Old Ways markings | The Severance has developed its own notation |
| The animal quiet is different from the peaceful quiet of a maintained grove | Animals avoid this location |

**What happened here:** The Severance has been using this grove for meetings and rituals. They have not destroyed it — they still consider it sacred — but they have altered it in ways that an Old Ways practitioner would find deeply alarming.

**Player options:**

| Action | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Report to a Grove Keeper | [REPUTATION] Significant gain with The Grove Keepers; the Keeper is distressed and asks what specifically was altered |
| Attempt to restore the grove | Can be partially done; the carvings cannot be removed without damaging the trees |
| Track the Severance members | Possible; they were here recently; prints and other evidence |
| Leave a hidden observer | Information gathering opportunity |

**[QUEST HOOK]** The carvings include a location marker — Old Ways notation style, but using the Severance's additions. It indicates a secondary gathering site. This is where the Severance is meeting, and meeting soon.

---

### CS-3: The False Ancestor Site
**Associated phenomenon:** Corruption interference with Ancestors' Path contact  
**Found in:** The Iron Peaks, The Frozen Highlands  
**Surface appearance:** An Ancestors' Path memorial site

At a glance: a properly maintained ancestor memorial — waymarker, tokens, offerings. Closer inspection:

| Observation | What it indicates |
|---|---|
| The ancestor-tokens are positioned in the correct traditional arrangement — almost | Small deviations that a non-practitioner wouldn't notice but a practitioner would |
| Contact attempts at this site feel immediately responsive | Genuine ancestral contact takes time to establish; immediate response is a warning sign |
| The "ancestors" contacted know the right names and family details — but answer questions differently than the living remember them | Something has access to lineage records but not to the actual personalities |
| Extended contact leaves the practitioner disoriented in a way normal contact does not | A physical effect the tradition has no precedent for |

**What happened here:** The Corruption has identified this site as a contact point and is actively using it to intercept and replace ancestral communication. The site hasn't been physically altered — the problem is in what the site connects to.

**Player options:**

| Action | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Report to an Ancestor Speaker | [REPUTATION] Significant gain; the Speaker is alarmed; this confirms reports they've been collecting |
| Attempt to seal the site | Possible with Order or Speaker assistance; prevents future use for either purpose until the source is addressed |
| Trace the contact back | Dangerous; requires Order-level Boundary Reading skill or significant magical capability; possible quest route toward the Corruption's source |
| Document what the false ancestors say | Information — the Corruption reveals priorities in what it asks and answers |

**[QUEST HOOK]** A clan member who has been consulting this site for months has been receiving guidance that has been moving their clan in a consistent direction. The guidance has been wrong in subtle ways — not obviously, just slightly. The cumulative effect on clan governance has been significant. Undoing the harm requires first convincing the clan member that the contact was false — which requires evidence they will believe.

---

### CS-4: The Threshold Rift
**Associated cult:** The Threshold Circle (Veiled Order radicalization)  
**Found in:** Near Veiled Order houses or established thin-place sites in any region  
**Surface appearance:** A Veiled Order thin-place site

At a glance: a location consistent with Order boundary sites — minimal physical markers, a quality of atmospheric stillness. Closer inspection:

| Observation | What it indicates |
|---|---|
| The Order's circle-and-line symbol is present, but modified — the line extends past the circle on both ends | A Threshold Circle modification; the Order knows what it means |
| The thin-place quality is much stronger than natural sites produce | Artificially amplified; the Circle has been working to widen the boundary here |
| Documents left at the site are in Order notation but contain content the Order would never sanction | Explicit instructions for widening the boundary further |
| Contact with the boundary at this site produces a very specific communication — the same one, regardless of who attempts contact | Scripted by something or someone; not organic |

**What happened here:** The Threshold Circle has converted this thin-place site from a monitoring and documentation location to an active ritual site aimed at deliberately weakening the boundary between states of being. Their theology holds that this is beneficial; their methods are approaching territory where the Order's internal watchdogs should have intervened.

**Player options:**

| Action | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Report to a Veiled Archivist | [REPUTATION] Major gain; the Order needs this information immediately; the Archivist is deeply uncomfortable |
| Attempt to close the site | Possible; requires Order guidance or significant arcane capability |
| Read the documents left here | Full information about the Circle's current project and immediate goals |
| Follow the site to its supervisors | The Circle checks this site regularly; observation leads to Circle members |

**[QUEST HOOK]** The scripted communication at this site is not random. It's an invitation — specifically worded to appeal to people who are grieving a specific kind of loss. The Circle is using this site as a recruitment point: people who have recently lost someone, who come to thin-place sites hoping for contact, find instead a message that leads them toward the Circle. How many people have already been recruited through this specific site?
