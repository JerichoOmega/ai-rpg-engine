# HERO BIBLE PHILOSOPHY

Every companion in the game should feel handcrafted.

A companion should never simply be "a tank" or "a mage."

Their gameplay, abilities, dialogue, personality, equipment, animations, and story should all reinforce who they are as a person.

Mechanics should grow naturally from the character rather than being assigned arbitrarily.

The player's emotional connection to a companion should be just as important as their combat effectiveness.

---

# TALOS

Preserve Talos' existing lore and expand upon it.

The following should become his canonical interpretation.

---

## Core Identity

Talos is not a warrior because he enjoys violence.

He became a warrior because someone has to stand between innocent people and the horrors of the world.

Everything about his gameplay should reinforce this philosophy.

---

## Personality

Talos should remain:

- Battle-worn
- Distrustful
- Proud
- Compassionate
- Protective
- Honorable

Expand his personality to include:

### Public Personality

- Stoic
- Disciplined
- Blunt
- Slow to trust

### Private Personality

- Deeply kind
- Protective of weaker people
- Enjoys telling stories
- Dry sense of humor around trusted friends

---

## Moral Compass

Talos does not hate governments because he is rebellious.

He distrusts governments because he has spent years watching ordinary people suffer while those in power pursued their own agendas.

His loyalty should always be:

1. Innocent people
2. His companions
3. His community
4. Government (if deserved)

His morality should always prioritize protecting people over following authority.

---

## Visual Identity

Maintain his current visual design.

Special emphasis should be placed on:

- Heavy armor
- Numerous battle scars
- Towering build
- Sword and shield

Most importantly:

The bead bracelet gifted to him by children should remain visible on his character model whenever possible.

It represents exactly who he fights to protect.

---

## Gameplay Philosophy

Talos is a protector.

He is not designed around dealing the highest damage.

He is designed around enabling the rest of the party to survive.

---

## Hero Trait

Develop Talos' unique passive around protecting nearby allies.

Working direction:

Guardian's Resolve

When adjacent to allies:

- Allies gain defensive bonuses.
- Talos gains increased resistance to crowd control.

Exact values will be determined during balancing.

No other companion should possess this passive.

---

## Signature Ability

Preserve Talos' Battlecry concept.

Expand it into his defining signature ability.

Battlecry should:

- Inspire allies.
- Intimidate enemies.
- Represent Talos as a battlefield leader.

It should become the move players most associate with him.

---

## Shield Stance Integration

Talos should become the primary showcase for the Shield Stance system.

Future talents and abilities may expand upon:

- Shield Wall
- Hold the Line
- Bulwark
- Brace
- Guardian's Oath

Talos should feel like the definitive defensive hero.

---

## Companion Relationships

Expand the existing relationships.

### Eleanor

Talos trusts Eleanor surprisingly quickly.

He recognizes her innocence and optimism.

As the story progresses he naturally becomes protective of her.

---

### Ragash

Mutual respect.

Potential slow-burn romance depending on player choices.

Both admire each other's willingness to protect others.

---

### Ronan

Talos remains cautious.

He does not fear Ronan himself.

He fears what Ronan's curse could do to innocent people.

Over time this relationship can evolve into mutual respect.

---

## Personal Quest

Expand Talos' story.

His journey should challenge his belief that every government inevitably becomes corrupt.

Allow the player to influence whether Talos ultimately:

- Learns that some leaders deserve trust.
- Remains convinced institutions always fail.
- Finds that true change begins by protecting communities rather than governments.

The quest should be about healing emotional wounds rather than simply defeating villains.

---

## Character Details

Retain and expand:

- Love of wine.
- Favorite meal being roasted duck.
- Always visits orphanages.
- Loves telling children stories of great adventures.

These should appear naturally throughout gameplay.

This compassionate side should contrast with his intimidating appearance.

---

# HERO DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Every future companion should follow the same design principles.

Each companion should have:

- A distinct personality.
- A unique gameplay identity.
- A memorable visual design.
- A meaningful emotional journey.
- Distinct dialogue.
- Personal relationships.
- Personal quests.

No companion should ever feel interchangeable.

---

## Core Wound Philosophy

Every companion should possess one defining emotional wound.

This emotional wound should directly influence:

- Personality
- Dialogue
- Gameplay
- Personal quest
- Character growth

Examples:

Talos

Lost faith in authority after years of war.

Eleanor

Naive optimism that leaves her vulnerable to betrayal.

Ragash

Rejected by her own people and found belonging with her hounds.

Ronan

Believes he is a danger to everyone around him because of his curse.

Steven

His identity and mysterious past define his emotional journey.

Future companions should follow this same philosophy.
