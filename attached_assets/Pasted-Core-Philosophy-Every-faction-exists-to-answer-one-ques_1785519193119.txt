Core Philosophy

Every faction exists to answer one question:

How does this group believe civilization should survive the Long Decline?

No faction should exist simply to be “good” or “evil.”

Every organization should have understandable goals, strengths, flaws, and internal disagreements.

Every faction believes it is helping preserve civilization, even when those methods conflict.

⸻

Tier I — Continental Powers

Implement the following major factions.

⸻

The Imperial Council

Type:
Continental Government

Government:

Representative council.

No emperor.

No king.

Each major race elects representatives.

Primary Responsibilities:

* Maintain peace
* Coordinate diplomacy
* Build infrastructure
* Maintain trade
* Protect civilization

Strengths:

* Stable government
* Professional administration
* Infrastructure
* Diplomacy

Weaknesses:

* Political deadlock
* Regional disagreements
* Bureaucracy
* Corruption

View of the Divine Chorus:

Respected protectors of cosmic order.

Not expected to solve mortal politics.

View of the Long Decline:

The greatest threat facing civilization.

Many members underestimate its true cause.

Player Interaction:

Political quests.

Regional disputes.

Council decisions.

Diplomatic missions.

⸻

The Great Library

Type:

Academic Institution

Purpose:

Preserve all knowledge.

Canonical Motto:

“Knowledge belongs to tomorrow. What is forgotten belongs to oblivion.”

Responsibilities:

* History
* Maps
* Languages
* Ancient civilizations
* Magical research
* Preservation

View of the Long Decline:

Knowledge lost can never truly be recovered.

Player Interaction:

Historical investigations.

Lore.

Journal integration.

Artifact recovery.

Scholar quests.

⸻

The Covenant

Type:

Continental Religious Organization

Purpose:

Maintain temples.

Protect shrines.

Teach the traditions of the Divine Chorus.

Preserve sacred history.

Structure:

* High Council
* Regional Clergy
* Shrine Keepers
* Temple Wardens
* Pilgrim Orders

View of the Divine Chorus:

Cosmic caretakers rather than miracle workers.

View of the Long Decline:

An existential and spiritual crisis.

Player Interaction:

Shrines.

Pilgrimages.

Religious festivals.

Historical records.

Community support.

⸻

Imperial Army

Purpose:

Protect civilization.

Defend trade.

Respond to monster threats.

Assist frontier settlements.

Branches:

* Infantry
* Engineers
* Scouts
* Medical Corps
* Frontier Rangers

⸻

Reputation System

Every major faction should maintain its own reputation with the player.

Examples:

* Trust
* Political influence
* Access to services
* Discounts
* Special quests
* Unique dialogue
* Story branching

Do not implement a single universal reputation meter.

Different factions should react differently to the player’s choices.

⸻

World State Integration

Every faction must respond dynamically to changes in the World State.

Examples:

Imperial Council

* Redirects aid.
* Strengthens defenses.
* Debates emergency measures.

Great Library

* Sends archivists to endangered regions.
* Prioritizes preserving threatened knowledge.

Covenant

* Organizes relief efforts.
* Restores shrines.
* Supports local communities.

Merchant Guild

* Changes caravan routes.
* Responds to economic instability.

Builders’ Guild

* Repairs roads.
* Restores bridges.
* Rebuilds damaged settlements.

Frontier Rangers

* Increase patrols.
* Investigate new corruption zones.
* Warn settlements of emerging dangers.

NPC dialogue, quests, settlements, and environmental storytelling should visibly reflect these changes.

⸻

Canonical Design Rules

Every new faction added to the project must define:

* Purpose
* History
* Leadership
* Core Values
* Allies
* Rivals
* View of the Divine Chorus
* View of the Long Decline
* Gameplay Function
* Relationship to the World State

No faction should exist solely as exposition or decoration.

Every organization should contribute meaningfully to the player’s understanding of the world and reinforce the central themes of the project.

⸻

Developer Notes

This Faction Bible should become one of the project’s core reference documents.

Future quests, companions, settlements, political conflicts, dialogue, region design, and environmental storytelling should reference these factions consistently.

The guiding philosophy is that the world feels alive because its institutions continue pursuing their own goals whether or not the player is present. The player influences history, but the continent is sustained by countless ordinary people and organizations working to preserve civilization in the face of the Long Decline.
