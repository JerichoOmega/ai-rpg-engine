We have finalized the historical structure of the world and are simplifying the historical timeline.

This is now canonical and supersedes any previous versions of the historical ages.

Please update the Lore Bible and any supporting documentation to reflect the following four-age historical model.

⸻

Canonical Historical Ages

Age I — The Age of Awakening

The age in which the world itself began to take shape.

Major Events

* The First Song.
* The Divine Chorus awakens.
* Primordial Energy begins stabilizing.
* The first intelligent races emerge.
* The earliest civilizations form.
* The First Temple is established.
* The foundations of magic, culture, and civilization are laid.

This is an age of discovery, exploration, and beginnings.

⸻

Age II — The Age of Harmony

The greatest age of civilization.

This becomes the world’s Golden Age.

Major Events

* The First Empire rises.
* The Great Library is founded.
* Universities and centers of learning flourish.
* Trade routes connect civilizations.
* Magic reaches its greatest advancement.
* The Forgotten Eighth falls in love with an ordinary mortal.
* The mortal eventually sings the Final Verse.
* The Long Decline begins during the closing centuries of this age.

The Long Decline is not its own historical age.

It is considered the tragic final chapter of the Age of Harmony.

⸻

Age III — The Age of Sundering

The greatest catastrophe in recorded history.

Major Events

* The Eighth’s corruption spreads.
* Reality itself begins to fracture.
* Civilizations collapse.
* The Divine Chorus spends centuries attempting to save their sister.
* The Sundering permanently reshapes the continent.
* The Forgotten Eighth is imprisoned.
* Much of history is deliberately obscured or forgotten during the aftermath.

This is remembered as the darkest age in history.

⸻

Age IV — The Age of Restoration

The modern age.

Civilization rebuilds from the ashes of the Sundering.

Major Events

* New kingdoms emerge.
* Modern cultures develop.
* The Imperial Capital becomes the intellectual heart of civilization.
* The Great Library preserves what remains of the ancient world.
* Most people genuinely believe there have always been seven members of the Divine Chorus.
* The game begins near the end of this age.

⸻

Historical Philosophy

The four ages represent the natural life cycle of civilization:

Awakening
→ Birth

Harmony
→ Flourishing

Sundering
→ Collapse

Restoration
→ Recovery

This structure should be reflected consistently throughout the Lore Bible, codex entries, historical documents, educational materials, and future worldbuilding.

⸻

Documentation Updates

Update all relevant lore documentation to replace any previous multi-age historical framework with this canonical four-age model.

If references to the previous structure remain, adapt them naturally rather than simply deleting content.

The objective is to preserve existing lore while organizing it under this simplified historical framework.

⸻

Developer Note

This four-age structure is intended to make the world’s history easier for players to understand while still supporting thousands of years of lore.

Future historical events should always be categorized under one of these four ages unless the project owner explicitly establishes a new historical era.
