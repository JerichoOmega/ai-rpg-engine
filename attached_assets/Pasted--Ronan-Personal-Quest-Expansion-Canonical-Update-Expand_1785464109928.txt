# Ronan Personal Quest Expansion (Canonical Update)

Expand Ronan's personal quest with the following story element.

---

# The Hidden Pack

During Ronan's companion quest, the party eventually encounters a small traveling community of werewolves.

This is not a kingdom or large civilization.

Instead...

It is a secretive nomadic pack that has survived for generations by remaining hidden from the rest of the world.

The pack avoids unnecessary conflict and constantly moves throughout remote regions of the continent.

Very few outsiders even know they exist.

---

# Ronan's Discovery

For most of his life, Ronan believes he is alone.

Meeting the Hidden Pack completely changes his understanding of the world.

For the first time...

He meets people who understand exactly what he is experiencing.

Some members have mastered their curse.

Others still struggle.

Some embrace their dual nature.

Others continue fighting it every day.

The pack demonstrates that lycanthropy does not define a person's character.

Every individual chooses what kind of person they become.

---

# Quest Themes

This portion of Ronan's story explores:

- Identity
- Belonging
- Family
- Self-acceptance
- Responsibility
- Redemption

The Hidden Pack offers Ronan something he has never truly had...

A place where he belongs.

---

# Companion Reactions

## Eleanor

Eleanor is fascinated by the pack's history and traditions.

She eagerly learns everything she can about lycanthropy in hopes of better understanding Ronan.

---

## Ragash

Ragash immediately respects the pack's bond with nature and their strong sense of family.

She quickly forms friendships with many of them.

Because of her understanding of animals and pack behavior, she feels surprisingly comfortable among them.

---

## Talos

Talos remains cautious.

His years as a soldier have taught him to be skeptical of anything powerful and poorly understood.

However...

As he watches the pack protect one another and honor their traditions, he begins to realize that discipline and honor can exist even among those carrying terrible burdens.

His respect must be earned, but it can be earned.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Hidden Pack should never be portrayed as perfect.

They have disagreements.

Internal conflicts.

Different beliefs about living with the curse.

Some members advocate isolation.

Others believe coexistence with humanity is possible.

Like every intelligent society in Project Dungeon Keeper, they are complex people rather than a single stereotype.

---

# Narrative Goal

The Hidden Pack is not introduced simply to reveal more werewolves.

They exist to help Ronan understand that he is not alone.

His journey is no longer about escaping his curse.

It becomes about deciding the kind of man he wishes to be while living with it.
