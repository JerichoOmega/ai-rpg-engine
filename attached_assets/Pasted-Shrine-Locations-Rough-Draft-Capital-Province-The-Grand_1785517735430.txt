Shrine Locations (Rough Draft)

Capital Province

The Grand Temple of the Seven Voices

Type: Grand Cathedral

Dedicated To: The Divine Chorus

Age: Early Age of Unity

Condition: Immaculately Maintained

Caretakers: High Covenant Clergy

Lore
The largest temple on the continent and the spiritual heart of the Covenant. Pilgrims from every kingdom visit at least once in their lifetime.

Quest Hook
Political tensions among clergy threaten to divide the temple.

World State
The morale of the capital is influenced by the stability of this temple.

First Chorus Sanctuary

Type: Ancient Shrine

Age: First Empire

Condition: Restored

Lore
Believed to be one of the earliest shrines dedicated to the Divine Chorus.

Unknown to nearly everyone, hidden beneath its foundations lie sealed ruins connected to the Architects.

This remains undiscovered during most of the game.

General World State Examples

Shrines can exist in several conditions:

Maintained

* Active pilgrims
* Flowers
* Candles
* Music
* Healthy surroundings

Neglected

* Overgrown paths
* Faded banners
* Few visitors
* Aging caretaker

Abandoned

* Wildlife has reclaimed the grounds.
* Roof damage.
* Broken statues.
* Lost records.

Corrupted

* Strange growths.
* Distorted architecture.
* Unnatural silence.
* Local wildlife behaves aggressively.

Restored (Player Influence)

* Pilgrims return.
* Local festivals resume.
* New caretakers arrive.
* Historical records are preserved.
* The surrounding community begins to recover.

Design Rules

Every named shrine should include:

* Name
* Region
* Divine Chorus dedication (or Old Way tradition)
* Approximate age
* Current condition
* Caretaker (if applicable)
* Historical significance
* Local tradition
* Quest hook
* World State interaction
