- The world feels ancient.
- Every region has history.
- Every faction has depth.
- Companion relationships evolve naturally.
- Dynamic Campaign Story Arcs create unique emotional experiences.
- The AI Dungeon Master expands within established canon instead of rewriting it.

# DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Whenever new lore is created, follow these rules.

## Rule One

Never create lore that has no gameplay purpose.

Every historical event, faction, culture, religion, artifact, monster, or location should eventually influence:

- quests
- dialogue
- exploration
- combat
- politics
- companion interactions

---

## Rule Two

Companions should experience the world alongside the player.

Major world events should affect them emotionally.

Dynamic Campaign Story Arcs should unlock:

- unique conversations
- disagreements
- friendships
- emotional growth
- camp scenes

Different campaigns should naturally highlight different companion relationships.

---

## Rule Three

Every region should possess a distinct identity.

The player should immediately recognize where they are.

Current regional identities include:

The Frontier
- adventure
- danger
- exploration
- frontier settlements

The Great Forest
- ancient mystery
- elven history
- sacred groves
- forgotten ruins

Iron Peaks
- dwarven civilization
- mining
- craftsmanship
- mountain fortresses

Frozen Highlands
- survival
- resilience
- harsh beauty
- communities built around using every resource wisely

Sol Kareth
- trade
- archaeology
- ancient tombs
- desert kingdoms

Capital Province
- politics
- military
- religion
- education
- diplomacy

---

## Rule Four

Every intelligent faction should contain internal disagreements.

Avoid portraying any faction as completely unified.

Examples include:

- rival Vampire Houses
- Goblin tribal politics
- differing beliefs within the Mages Guild
- political disagreements among nobles
- religious divisions

The world should feel alive.

---

## Rule Five

The AI Dungeon Master should create stories inside the world's established canon.

It should not rewrite history.

It should build upon existing lore while respecting all canonical documentation.

# WORLD HISTORY

Create a new canonical History Bible.

The world's history should be divided into Four Ages.

Each age should possess its own identity, themes, historical events, and long-term consequences.

---

# FIRST AGE

## The Age of Creation

"When gods still walked among mortals."

This is the mythical beginning of recorded history.

During this era:

- the Ancient God shaped the world
- the first races emerged
- dragons were common
- magic flowed freely
- civilizations began forming

Eventually...

The Ancient God's growing corruption threatened the entire world.

A legendary alliance of heroes, powerful mages, divine champions, and ancient dragons united to imprison the Ancient God beneath the world.

The battle reshaped continents.

Magic itself was forever changed.

The gods withdrew from directly walking among mortals.

Legacy:

- the prison of the Ancient God
- legendary artifacts
- ancient ruins
- lost civilizations
- dragons becoming incredibly rare

Theme:

Sacrifice

Lesson:

Unity can overcome even impossible darkness.

---

# SECOND AGE

## The Age of Kingdoms

"An era of hope, discovery, and prosperity."

Following the imprisonment of the Ancient God...

Civilization flourished.

Major kingdoms were founded.

The Capital Province became the center of politics.

The Iron Peaks became the heart of dwarven civilization.

The Great Forest flourished.

Sol Kareth became a center of trade and learning.

The Mages Guild was founded.

Major religions expanded.

Most people believed darkness had been defeated forever.

Legacy:

- modern kingdoms
- trade routes
- universities
- temples
- magical education

Theme:

Hope

Lesson:

Peace and prosperity must be actively protected.

---

# THIRD AGE

## The Age of Wars

"When ambition replaced unity."

Old alliances collapsed.

Kingdoms fought for centuries.

Borders changed repeatedly.

Cities were destroyed.

Knowledge was lost.

The Vampire Houses quietly expanded.

Goblin tribes became more organized.

The Corruption slowly returned unnoticed.

Talos served during the latter years of this age.

His military service helped shape his worldview.

Legacy:

- modern politics
- ruined fortresses
- battlefields
- noble rivalries
- fractured alliances

Theme:

Pride

Lesson:

Division can destroy what previous generations built.

---

# FOURTH AGE

## The Age of Shadows

"The darkness returns unnoticed."

This is the current age.

Trade has resumed.

Kingdoms rebuild.

People believe the worst is behind them.

However...

The Corruption has begun spreading once again.

Ancient ruins awaken.

Monsters become more aggressive.

Dark magic resurfaces.

Only a handful of scholars recognize the pattern.

The player begins their journey during the earliest days of this age.

Legacy:

Still being written.

The player's choices will determine how history remembers this age.

Theme:

Choice

Lesson:

History shapes the future but does not determine it.

---

# HISTORY DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

History should explain the present.

Ancient ruins should exist for a reason.

Artifacts should possess history.

Kingdoms should remember old wars.

Different cultures should interpret historical events differently.

History should enrich exploration.

---

# VAMPIRE HOUSE EXPANSION

The Vampire Houses remain one of the world's primary evil factions.

They are intelligent civilizations.

They possess politics.

Rivalries.

Long-term goals.

The player may negotiate with them.

Temporarily ally with them.

Trade information.

Or oppose them.

This should never imply moral goodness.

Helping a Vampire House should always produce consequences.

Companions should react accordingly.

---

# COMPANION RELATIONSHIP EXPANSION

Continue expanding dynamic companion relationships.

Examples include:

• Ronan supporting Eleanor during The Fractured Circle.

• Ragash becoming increasingly protective of Eleanor.

• Ragash helping Ronan accept himself.

• Talos remaining cautious while gradually learning to trust certain individuals.

Relationships should evolve naturally throughout the game.

Companions should become a family.

---

# RONAN PERSONAL QUEST

Expand Ronan's companion quest.

Introduce a hidden nomadic werewolf community.

This community has survived for generations by remaining hidden.

They are not a kingdom.

They are not an empire.

They are a traveling pack.

Meeting them changes Ronan forever.

He realizes he is not alone.

Some members embrace their dual nature.

Others continue struggling.

The community demonstrates that lycanthropy does not define one's character.

Character is determined by choices.

This section of Ronan's story explores:

- identity
- belonging
- family
- redemption
- responsibility
