Canonical Companion Migration — Stephen → Torren

This is a canonical narrative and design update for Project Dungeon Keeper.

This is not a simple character rename.

Stephen has been retired as a companion because the project has evolved significantly, and his original concept no longer aligns with the game’s thematic direction.

The goal is to preserve the crafting gameplay niche while creating a companion that fully supports the game’s core philosophy.

⸻

Why This Change Was Made

When Stephen was originally designed, Project Dungeon Keeper had a much broader narrative focus.

Since then, the world, companions, religion, magic system, and Corruption have all evolved around a single central theme:

The greatest battle is not against darkness… but against forgetting who you are.

Every major companion now represents a different philosophical response to the Corruption.

Stephen’s original identity as an engineer/craftsman no longer meaningfully contributed to this theme.

Rather than forcing the character to fit, we have chosen to redesign the role from the ground up.

⸻

Stephen Has Been Replaced

Stephen is no longer considered a canonical companion.

Replace him with a completely new companion:

Torren

This is not a rename.

This is a full redesign.

Old personality, backstory, motivations, dialogue, combat style, and companion philosophy should all be retired unless they naturally fit the new concept.

⸻

What Was Preserved

The following ideas remain valuable and should be preserved:

* Crafting
* Equipment improvement
* Weapon maintenance
* Armor maintenance
* Battlefield support
* Construction

These mechanics are now represented through Torren instead of Stephen.

⸻

What Changed

Instead of an engineer or generic craftsman…

Torren is a master blacksmith.

His identity revolves around creation rather than invention.

Where the Corruption destroys…

Torren rebuilds.

Where others discard broken things…

Torren restores them.

His craftsmanship is an extension of his worldview.

⸻

New Core Philosophy

Torren represents:

* Hope
* Restoration
* Renewal

His defining belief is:

“Broken things aren’t worthless. They simply require someone willing to rebuild them.”

This philosophy applies equally to:

* Weapons
* Armor
* Relics
* Kingdoms
* Friendships
* People

He is not naïve.

He understands that some things cannot be saved.

But he refuses to stop trying before reaching that conclusion.

⸻

Combat Identity Changes

Stephen’s previous combat design should be retired.

Torren now fights using a forging hammer (warhammer).

His gameplay emphasizes:

* Battlefield control
* Defensive support
* Tactical positioning
* Heavy melee combat
* Construct creation

He creates handcrafted battlefield structures such as:

* Barricades
* Ballistae
* Forge beacons
* Portable anvils
* Defensive fortifications
* Supply caches

Avoid magical summons or advanced technology.

Everything should feel handcrafted by a legendary smith.

⸻

Progression Philosophy

Project Dungeon Keeper no longer uses Ultimate abilities.

Companions instead grow through meaningful specialization trees inspired by the philosophy of Path of Exile.

The goal is meaningful player choice, not overwhelming complexity.

Torren’s specialization paths are:

Forge Master

* Ally enhancement
* Equipment reinforcement
* Repairs
* Buffs

Engineer

* Battlefield construction
* Defensive structures
* Terrain manipulation
* Tactical support

War Smith

* Hammer mastery
* Armor breaking
* Crowd control
* Frontline durability

Players should specialize rather than unlock everything.

⸻

Story Role

Torren is no longer “the crafting companion.”

He is the companion who reminds the party that rebuilding is always worth attempting.

His worldview directly opposes the Ancient God’s philosophy.

The Corruption says:

“Everything eventually breaks.”

Torren answers:

“Then we rebuild.”

⸻

Companion Dynamics

Update companion interactions to reflect Torren’s new role.

Examples:

* Ronan finds encouragement in Torren’s belief that broken things can be reforged.
* Talos respects Torren’s discipline and dedication to his craft.
* Eleanor and Torren discuss the similarities between restoring relics and restoring people.
* Ragash helps Torren appreciate that imperfections often tell the story of survival.

⸻

Documentation Cleanup

Update all companion documentation, world references, dialogue plans, relationship notes, combat systems, progression documents, and future planning documents to remove Stephen as a canonical companion and replace him with Torren where appropriate.

Do not perform a blind find-and-replace. Carefully rewrite affected sections so they reflect Torren’s new personality, philosophy, gameplay role, and narrative purpose.

The end result should feel as though Torren was always intended to be one of the core companions from the beginning of Project Dungeon Keeper.
