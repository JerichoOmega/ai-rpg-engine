# WORLD HISTORY

Create a dedicated History Bible if one does not already exist.

Divide history into Four Ages.

Each Age should have its own identity, major events, philosophy, and lasting consequences.

---

## The First Age

### The Age of Creation

"When gods still walked among mortals."

The Ancient God created the world and Primordial Magic.

During this era:

- the first races emerged
- dragons were common
- magic flowed freely
- civilizations began forming

Eventually the Ancient God's influence became corrupted.

A legendary alliance of heroes, ancient dragons, powerful mages, and divine champions imprisoned the Ancient God beneath the world.

The battle reshaped continents.

Magic itself changed forever.

Afterward, the gods ceased walking openly among mortals.

Legacy:

- Ancient God imprisoned
- Primordial ruins
- Legendary relics
- Lost civilizations
- Dragons becoming incredibly rare

Theme:

Sacrifice

Lesson:

Unity can overcome impossible darkness.

---

## The Second Age

### The Age of Kingdoms

Civilization flourished.

Modern kingdoms were founded.

The Capital became the center of politics.

The Iron Peaks became the dwarven homeland.

The Great Forest flourished.

Sol Kareth became a center of commerce and scholarship.

The Mages Guild was founded.

The teachings of the Divine Chorus spread across the continent.

Theme:

Hope

Lesson:

Peace must be protected.

---

## The Third Age

### The Age of Wars

Old alliances failed.

Kingdoms fought for centuries.

Borders shifted.

Cities fell.

Knowledge was lost.

The Vampire Houses quietly expanded.

Goblin tribes became stronger.

The Corruption slowly returned unnoticed.

Talos served during the later years of this age.

Theme:

Pride

Lesson:

Division destroys what previous generations build.

---

## The Fourth Age

### The Age of Shadows

This is the current age.

Kingdoms rebuild.

Trade resumes.

Most believe peace has returned.

Meanwhile...

The Corruption quietly spreads.

Ancient ruins awaken.

Dark magic resurfaces.

Only a handful recognize the true threat.

The player's story begins during the earliest years of this age.

Theme:

Choice

Lesson:

History influences the future but never decides it.

# MAGIC

Create a dedicated Magic Bible if one does not already exist.

---

## Primordial Magic

Primordial Magic is the original magical force woven into creation by the Ancient God.

It is the foundation of every magical discipline.

Everything magical ultimately traces back to Primordial Energy.

---

## The Divine Chorus

The Divine Chorus did NOT create magic.

Instead...

Each member of the Divine Chorus became the Divine Steward of one aspect of Primordial Magic after the Ancient God was imprisoned.

Their purpose is maintaining harmony and teaching mortals how to safely wield Primordial Magic.

They are guardians.

Teachers.

Stewards.

Not creators.

---

# RELIGION

The Divine Chorus is the world's primary faith.

Nearly every civilized culture recognizes every member of the Divine Chorus.

This is not a collection of competing religions.

It is one shared faith.

People may personally revere one god more than another.

However...

They still believe in and honor the entire Divine Chorus.

No god is considered more important than another.

Each represents an essential part of creation.

---

## Personal Devotion

Individuals often develop a stronger relationship with one member of the Divine Chorus.

This is a personal devotion.

Examples:

- Blacksmiths often revere Solaryn.
- Healers commonly pray to Neressa.
- Builders and craftsmen often seek Tharok's blessing.
- Travelers frequently ask Zephyros for guidance.

This does NOT replace belief in the rest of the Chorus.

---

## Temples

Most temples are dedicated to the Divine Chorus as a whole.

Within each temple are shrines honoring every member.

People simply spend more time praying at the shrine of the god whose virtues they seek guidance from.

Ancient First Age sanctuaries dedicated exclusively to one god still exist.

These are rare sacred places with immense historical significance.

---

## Philosophy

The central teaching of the Divine Chorus is Balance.

Suggested ancient scripture:

"No flame burns without air.

No river flows without stone.

No mountain stands without time.

No life exists without balance."

Balance is the foundation of creation.

# THE DIVINE CHORUS

The gods should each possess:

- a true name
- a title
- an element
- virtues
- followers
- symbols
- ancient temples
- sacred relics

The title should reflect mythology rather than simply saying "God of Fire."

For example:

Solaryn, the First Flame

Rather than:

God of Fire.

Develop the remaining members of the Divine Chorus using this philosophy.

Each god should feel ancient, unique, and memorable.

---

# RELICS

Each member of the Divine Chorus possesses one legendary First Age relic.

These relics are symbols of their teachings rather than simply powerful magical artifacts.

Examples include:

- Solaryn's Ember
- Neressa's Tear
- Tharok's Anvil

Additional relics should follow this design philosophy.

These relics should become important historical artifacts and potential future quest objectives.

---

# MAGES GUILD

The Mages Guild approaches magic academically.

Religion approaches magic spiritually.

Neither philosophy is considered objectively correct.

Both are different ways of understanding the same Primordial Magic.

This philosophical difference should influence dialogue, quests, and worldbuilding.

---

# MAGIC LIMITATIONS

Most mages naturally possess an affinity for one elemental aspect of Primordial Magic.

Some exceptionally gifted mages may eventually master a second affinity after years of study.

Attempting to channel multiple elements without proper affinity is dangerous.

This limitation is accepted as a fundamental law of magic.

---

# ELEANOR

Expand Eleanor's companion quest.

From the beginning of the story...

Eleanor naturally combines multiple elemental affinities.

This should be considered impossible according to modern magical understanding.

Even the Mages Guild cannot explain it.

Some scholars admire her.

Others fear her.

Others wish to study her.

Eventually...

Her companion quest leads the party to an ancient First Age sanctuary connected to one member of the Divine Chorus.

There...

Eleanor encounters one of the gods.

She expects to learn how to control her gift.

Instead...

She learns why she possesses it.

The god reveals that Eleanor is a Harmonic Soul.

A Harmonic Soul is an extraordinarily rare individual naturally connected to Primordial Magic itself rather than a single elemental aspect.

The gods did not create this gift.

They simply recognize it.

The elements are not truly separate.

They are different expressions of one Primordial source.

Most mages learn one note.

Eleanor can hear the entire symphony.

This revelation explains her unique abilities.

It does NOT make her the chosen one.

It does NOT make her the most powerful mage.

It makes her uniquely capable of understanding the true nature of magic.

Her strength comes from understanding rather than raw power.
