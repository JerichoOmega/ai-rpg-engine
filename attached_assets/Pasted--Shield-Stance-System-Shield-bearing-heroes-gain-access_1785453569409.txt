# Shield Stance System

Shield-bearing heroes gain access to the Shield Stance mechanic.

Shield Stance provides an active defensive choice rather than a passive armor bonus.

## Entering Shield Stance

A shield-bearing hero may spend their **Support Action** to raise their shield and enter Shield Stance.

Entering Shield Stance does not consume any Action Points.

Once raised, the shield remains active until the player chooses to lower it.

---

## Benefits of Shield Stance

While Shield Stance is active, the hero gains defensive bonuses against attacks originating from the front.

Potential benefits include:

- Increased frontal defense
- Increased block chance
- Reduced ranged damage from the front
- Improved resistance against melee attacks from the front
- Access to shield-specific Reaction abilities

Exact numerical values will be determined during balancing.

---

## Maintaining Shield Stance

Shield Stance remains active between turns.

The player does not need to spend another Support Action every round to maintain the stance.

The hero continues benefiting from Shield Stance until it is voluntarily lowered or another game mechanic removes it.

---

## Lowering the Shield

A hero may lower their shield at the beginning of their turn without spending Action Points or a Support Action.

Lowering the shield removes all Shield Stance defensive bonuses.

Lowering the shield does **not** refund the Support Action previously spent to enter the stance.

Once lowered, the hero may use their current turn's Support Action normally for other abilities.

---

## Tactical Purpose

Shield Stance creates a meaningful tactical decision every battle.

Players must decide between:

- Maintaining a powerful defensive position
- Lowering their guard to use Support Actions for healing, reviving allies, buffs, consumables, or other utility abilities

This reinforces the identity of shield-bearing classes as defensive front-line protectors instead of simply high-armor damage dealers.

---

## Design Philosophy

Shield Stance is intended to make shields an active tactical choice rather than a passive equipment bonus.

The system rewards planning, positioning, and anticipation of enemy actions while remaining simple for players to understand.

Future skills, equipment, talents, and class abilities may expand upon the Shield Stance system.
