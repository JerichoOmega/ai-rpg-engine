The attached files are now the canonical source of truth for this project.

This includes:

* The Elyndor Universe Bible
* The Character Sheets
* Any future lore documents

Your job is to organize these documents into a scalable repository and preserve them as canon.

⸻

Canon Rules

Elyndor is a standalone fantasy universe.

It is not tied to any one game.

Future games, books, comics, or other media may all take place within Elyndor.

Do not redesign or rewrite the lore unless explicitly instructed.

⸻

Separate the Universe from the Game

Maintain two completely separate documentation systems.

1. Elyndor Universe Bible

This contains only reusable worldbuilding.

Examples include:

* World lore
* History
* Ancient civilizations
* Ancient Legends
* Creatures
* Monsters
* Magic
* Mythology
* Religions
* Organizations
* Artifacts
* Ruins
* Historical timelines

These documents exist independently of any game.

⸻

2. Tactical RPG Game Bible

This contains everything specific to the current game.

Examples include:

* Main Story
* Chapters
* Quests
* Playable Characters
* Enemy Factions
* Gameplay Systems
* Combat
* Equipment
* Progression
* Classes
* UI
* Design Documents

Nothing in this section should overwrite the Universe Bible.

⸻

Character Categories

Characters belong in one of two groups.

Ancient Legends

These are legendary historical figures from Elyndor’s distant past.

They are part of the world’s mythology.

Examples include:

* Aurelia Sunstrider
* Valen Ashfall

They are not members of the playable cast unless a future story specifically says so.

⸻

Present-Day Characters

These are the protagonists and companions of the Tactical RPG.

Examples include:

* Talos
* Beast Master
* Werewolf
* Every future companion

These belong exclusively inside the Game Bible.

⸻

Character Sheets

Every playable character should have their own markdown file using the attached Character Sheet template.

Do not modify the template unless instructed.

Populate each file using the information already established.

Leave unknown sections blank for future development.

⸻

Ancient Legend Sheets

Ancient Legends should use the same template, but with these values:

Era

* Age of Legends

Role

* Historical Figure

Their entries should focus on:

* Historical accounts
* Myths
* Folklore
* Legacy
* Influence on the modern world

⸻

Lore Writing Style

All lore should be written as though it were discovered by historians, explorers, librarians, or scholars living in Elyndor.

Avoid:

* Developer notes
* Gameplay terminology
* RPG mechanics
* Meta commentary

The world should feel believable and timeless.

⸻

Future Expansion

The repository should always be easy to expand.

Adding a new character should never require reorganizing the project.

Adding a new creature should automatically fit into the Bestiary.

Adding a new civilization should fit naturally into the World section.

Keep everything modular and organized.

⸻

Repository Goal

Treat this repository as a professional game studio would treat its internal lore database.

The Elyndor Universe Bible is the permanent source of truth for the setting.

The Tactical RPG Game Bible is the permanent source of truth for the current game.

Never mix the two unless explicitly instructed.
