# Campaign Design Philosophy

Project Dungeon Keeper is designed around a handcrafted narrative with dynamic world evolution.

The goal is **not** for every campaign to tell a completely unrelated story.

Instead, every campaign tells a different version of the same world crisis.

---

# Core Story

Every campaign begins with the same central premise.

An ancient god remains imprisoned beneath the world.

Its prison has begun to weaken.

As its influence spreads, the world slowly falls into chaos through a force known as **The Corruption**.

The player gradually uncovers the truth behind this ancient threat while exploring the continent.

This overarching narrative remains consistent across all campaigns.

---

# Dynamic Campaign Philosophy

While the main story remains consistent, each campaign should feel unique because the world evolves differently.

Examples of systems that may vary include:

- Which companions become recruitable.
- Recruitment order.
- Companion availability.
- Regional events.
- Political conflicts.
- Faction relationships.
- World events.
- Optional bosses.
- Hidden dungeons.
- Secret merchants.
- Travel encounters.
- Side quests.
- Companion quests.
- Environmental storytelling.
- Town outcomes.
- Player consequences.

The player should never know exactly how the world will develop during a new campaign.

---

# The Corruption

The Corruption does not control people's minds.

Instead, it amplifies existing flaws within individuals, societies, and nature.

Possible manifestations include:

- Political instability.
- Religious extremism.
- Environmental corruption.
- Arcane instability.
- Ancient monsters awakening.
- Increasing hostility between factions.

Each campaign emphasizes different manifestations of the Corruption, creating a unique experience while remaining true to the world's lore.

---

# Handcrafted First

Project Dungeon Keeper prioritizes handcrafted storytelling over procedural generation.

Major story beats, companion arcs, world lore, and important quests are intentionally authored.

Randomization exists to create variation, replayability, and player-driven stories—not to replace quality writing.

---

# Replayability Philosophy

Replayability comes from experiencing a familiar world that changes in meaningful ways.

Players should ask:

- Which companions will I meet this time?
- Which regions will be in crisis?
- Which factions will rise or fall?
- What secrets will I discover?
- How will my choices shape this version of the world?

Rather than:

"I wonder if I'll get an entirely different game."

Every campaign should feel like a new telling of the same epic legend.

---

# Dungeon Master Philosophy

The AI Dungeon Master is not responsible for creating completely different stories.

Its role is to guide, adapt, and personalize each campaign by responding to player actions and orchestrating dynamic world events.

The AI should ensure every playthrough feels natural, coherent, and unique while respecting the established lore and handcrafted narrative.

---

# Design Goal

Project Dungeon Keeper combines:

- A handcrafted central narrative.
- Memorable companion stories.
- Tactical turn-based combat.
- Dynamic world evolution.
- Meaningful player choices.
- High replayability through campaign variation.

The objective is for players to finish one campaign and immediately wonder:

"What happened in someone else's world?"

rather than simply replaying the exact same adventure.
