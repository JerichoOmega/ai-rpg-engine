Describe the purpose of the Lore Bible.

This folder contains the canonical lore for the RPG. Any future content must remain consistent with these documents. If future ideas contradict these files, these files take precedence until intentionally revised.

⸻

CANON_RULES.md

Document these immutable design principles:

World Philosophy

* There is no chosen one.
* There are no chosen races.
* There are no divine bloodlines.
* Heroes are created through their choices.
* History belongs to mortals.
* Every civilization rises or falls through its own decisions.

The Divine Chorus

The Divine Chorus preserves existence—not history.

They do not:

* choose rulers
* manipulate wars
* decide political outcomes
* create prophecies selecting heroes

They intervene only when reality itself is threatened.

The Forgotten Eighth is the defining historical example.

DIVINE_CHORUS.md

Document the philosophy of the Divine Chorus.

Key principles:

* Preserve reality.
* Preserve cosmic balance.
* Preserve Primordial Energy.
* Preserve the Final Verse.
* Respect mortal free will.
* Do not shape history.
* Care deeply for Creation while intentionally allowing mortals to determine their own future.

ARCHITECTS.md

Document the Architects.

Rules:

* They predate the Divine Chorus.
* Even the Chorus does not understand them.
* They never appear.
* They never speak.
* They never become enemies.
* Their existence is intentionally mysterious.
* Their origin is never confirmed.
* Only subtle archaeological clues should exist throughout the world.
* The greatest known mystery lies beneath the First Temple.

Developers should never over-explain the Architects.

Order of Archivists

Mission:

Preserve truth for future generations regardless of politics.

Loyalty:

* truth
* knowledge
* history
* civilization

Never to governments.

⸻

Official Oath

Include exactly:

“Knowledge is not ours to possess, but ours to protect. We preserve today so that tomorrow may understand. Let no truth be hidden through fear, no history be forgotten through neglect, and no voice be silenced by our own hands. We are not the owners of knowledge—we are its stewards.”

After the oath, the Chief Archivist always replies:

“Then carry tomorrow well.”

⸻

Scholar Traditions

Document the sayings:

“The future remembers only what we choose to preserve.”

“Tomorrow is built upon what we refuse to forget.”

These are traditional sayings used by professors and historians rather than official institutional mottos.

⸻

Archivist Tradition

Every archivist receives a blank journal upon taking the oath.

The first page contains the oath.

The remaining pages are filled over a lifetime with discoveries, translations, observations, and reflections.

Upon the archivist’s death, the journal is returned to the Great Library and becomes part of the permanent collection.

Developer Notes

Future quests, dialogue, books, codex entries, historians, scholars, and environmental storytelling should consistently reinforce these themes:

* Knowledge is humanity’s inheritance.
* Truth should outlive politics.
* History belongs to those who preserve it.
