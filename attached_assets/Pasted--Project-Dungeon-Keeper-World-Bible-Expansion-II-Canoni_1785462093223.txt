Analyze the current repository and expand the World Bible and Game Bible using the following information.

## IMPORTANT

- Preserve all existing lore.
- Expand documentation only.
- Do NOT implement gameplay systems or code.
- Cross-reference existing documentation whenever appropriate.

---

# PLAYABLE RACES

The playable races at launch are intentionally limited to establish a focused and believable world.

## Playable Races

- Humans
- Elves
- Dwarves
- Orcs
- Halflings
- Gnomes

Each race should possess its own history, traditions, architecture, values, and culture.

A race should never define an individual's personality.

Not every dwarf enjoys mining.

Not every elf embraces ancient traditions.

Not every orc seeks battle.

Individuals should be shaped by their experiences rather than stereotypes.

---

# NON-PLAYABLE INTELLIGENT RACES

Some intelligent peoples exist within the world but are not playable.

Current example:

- Goblins

Additional intelligent races may be introduced in future worldbuilding if they naturally fit the setting.

---

# THE CAPITAL

The Capital is the political, military, educational, and cultural heart of the continent.

It is known for:

- Royal Government
- Military High Command
- Mages Guild Headquarters
- Major Temples
- Universities
- Great Libraries
- International Trade
- Noble Districts

Talos once served within the Capital's military before losing faith in the government.

This connection should continue influencing his personal story.

The majority of the continent's military is stationed within the Capital.

Smaller garrisons exist throughout the continent to protect towns, forts, roads, and trade routes.

---

# ADVENTURERS GUILD

The primary headquarters of the Adventurers Guild is located within The Frontier.

Reasons include:

- Constant monster activity
- Ancient ruins
- Exploration opportunities
- New discoveries
- Untapped riches
- Dangerous frontiers

The guild should also maintain smaller branch offices throughout larger cities.

The Adventurers Guild serves as one of the player's primary sources of:

- Contracts
- Exploration requests
- Rumors
- Bounties
- Optional quests

---

# MAGES GUILD

The Mages Guild headquarters is located in the Capital.

Responsibilities include:

- Training mages
- Magical research
- Preserving magical knowledge
- Studying magical anomalies
- Investigating the Corruption
- Securing dangerous magical artifacts

---

# VAMPIRE HOUSES

The world contains three major Vampire Houses.

Their names and detailed histories will be developed later.

Current canonical design philosophy:

- Each house occupies a different region of the continent.
- Each possesses its own leadership.
- Each follows different philosophies.
- Each maintains different long-term goals.
- They should compete with one another as often as they cooperate.

One house is politically influential within the Capital.

The remaining houses should naturally fit other regions of the world.

Vampires are a major supernatural power rather than generic monsters.

# PLAYER CHOICE PHILOSOPHY

Project Dungeon Keeper embraces tabletop-inspired roleplaying.

Combat should never be the only solution.

Whenever appropriate, encounters should support multiple approaches.

Examples include:

- Combat
- Diplomacy
- Negotiation
- Intimidation
- Stealth
- Deception
- Avoidance
- Bribery
- Investigation

Players should be encouraged to solve problems in the manner that best fits their character.

---

# GOBLIN DIPLOMACY

Goblins are intelligent people rather than mindless enemies.

Depending on reputation, circumstances, and tribal leadership, players may be able to:

- Negotiate
- Form temporary alliances
- Trade
- Accept quests
- Resolve territorial disputes
- Broker peace
- Recruit information
- Avoid unnecessary bloodshed

Combat should remain a valid solution but never the only solution.

Player decisions involving goblins should have meaningful consequences that may influence future encounters and regional politics.

---

# REPUTATION PHILOSOPHY

Faction reputation is heavily inspired by Fallout: New Vegas.

Each major faction tracks the player's reputation independently.

Reputation should significantly influence:

- Dialogue
- Quest availability
- Prices
- Safe passage
- Political relationships
- Alliances
- Hostility
- World events

The world should remember the player's actions.

There should never be a universal "Good" or "Evil" reputation meter.

Instead, every faction forms its own opinion of the player based on the player's decisions.

---

# DESIGN GOAL

The ultimate objective is to create a living fantasy world that feels like a tabletop RPG campaign.

The player should feel free to approach situations however they choose.

The AI Dungeon Master should support creativity, meaningful choices, and emergent storytelling while remaining faithful to the established lore.

These additions should become canonical and be integrated into the World Bible, Game Bible, and all future design documentation.
