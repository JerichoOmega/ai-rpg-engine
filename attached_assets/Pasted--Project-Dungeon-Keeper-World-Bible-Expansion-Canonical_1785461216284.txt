- Expand documentation rather than replacing it.

- This update is for worldbuilding and design documentation only.

---

# WORLD PHILOSOPHY

The world should feel ancient, believable, and lived in.

Rather than dozens of shallow kingdoms and factions, Project Dungeon Keeper will focus on a smaller number of deeply developed civilizations, organizations, and cultures.

Every region should have:

- Its own identity
- Culture
- Architecture
- History
- Politics
- Wildlife
- Dungeons
- Local monsters
- Travel encounters
- Resources

The Corruption influences every region differently.

---

# CANON REGIONS

## The Frontier

The untamed edge of civilization.

Known for:

- Frontier towns
- Military outposts
- Goblin tribes
- Orc warbands
- Refugee settlements
- Dangerous trade routes
- Ruined watchtowers
- Ancient battlefields

The Frontier is often the first region affected by the Corruption.

---

## The Great Forest

Known for:

- Ancient forests
- Elven settlements
- Sacred groves
- Magical wildlife
- Hidden shrines
- Ancient ruins
- Rare herbs

As the Corruption spreads, portions of the forest become twisted and increasingly dangerous.

---

## The Iron Peaks

Homeland of the dwarves.

Known for:

- Massive mountain ranges
- Ancient underground kingdoms
- Rich mines
- Legendary forges
- Frozen mountain passes
- Endless cave systems

Steven's mining knowledge naturally gives him unique interactions within this region.

---

## The Frozen Highlands

A harsh northern wilderness where survival shapes the culture.

Core Philosophy:

Nothing is wasted.

The people of the Frozen Highlands make use of every part of every resource available to them.

Examples:

- Meat becomes food.
- Hide becomes clothing.
- Bones become tools.
- Sinew becomes bowstrings.
- Fat becomes lamp oil.
- Antlers become carvings.

Because survival is difficult, the culture values:

- Practicality
- Honor
- Community
- Self-reliance
- Respect for nature

Wastefulness is viewed as deeply disrespectful.

---

## Sol Kareth

The great desert kingdom.

Known for:

- Oasis cities
- Merchant caravans
- Ancient tombs
- Forgotten temples
- Buried civilizations
- Sandstorms
- Powerful trade routes

Sol Kareth is one of the oldest civilizations on the continent, with countless ruins hidden beneath the sands.

---

## The Capital Province

Political heart of the continent.

Known for:

- Great cities
- Noble houses
- Government
- Military headquarters
- Trade
- Political intrigue

Campaign events can significantly alter the stability of this region.

# MAJOR ORGANIZATIONS

## Adventurers Guild

One of the most respected neutral organizations.

Responsibilities include:

- Monster contracts
- Exploration requests
- Escort missions
- Treasure hunting
- Bounties
- Rumor gathering
- Mapping unexplored areas

The guild serves as a major source of optional quests and world information.

---

## Mages Guild

Responsible for:

- Training mages
- Preserving magical knowledge
- Researching magical phenomena
- Studying ancient relics
- Securing dangerous artifacts
- Investigating magical anomalies caused by the Corruption

The guild is respected but not universally trusted.

---

# RELIGIONS

The world will initially feature a small number of major religions rather than dozens of minor faiths.

Focus on developing each religion with meaningful beliefs, traditions, and influence rather than simply creating multiple gods.

Religion should affect politics, culture, and daily life throughout the world.

---

# VAMPIRE FACTIONS

There are three major vampire houses.

Each house has different goals, philosophies, and methods.

Examples of possible differences include:

- Political manipulation
- Military conquest
- Ancient knowledge
- Maintaining secrecy
- Survival of vampire society

Vampires are one of the world's primary supernatural powers and should never feel like generic enemies.

Campaigns may feature varying levels of vampire activity.

---

# GOBLIN TRIBES

There are three primary goblin tribes.

## Stonefang Tribe

Known for:

- Aggressive raiding
- Strength
- Brutal warriors
- Territorial expansion

---

## Mossroot Tribe

Known for:

- Hunters
- Survivalists
- Living alongside nature
- Avoiding unnecessary conflict

---

## Ashfire Tribe

Known for:

- Recently united clans
- Ambitious leadership
- Rapid expansion
- Becoming a major regional threat

Each tribe should have its own leaders, traditions, territories, and relationships with neighboring civilizations.

Not every goblin tribe should automatically be hostile.

# REPUTATION SYSTEM PHILOSOPHY

Reputation should be heavily inspired by Fallout: New Vegas.

The player does NOT have a universal morality score.

Instead, reputation is tracked separately for each major faction.

Examples include:

- Kingdoms
- Adventurers Guild
- Mages Guild
- Vampire Houses
- Goblin Tribes
- Religious Orders
- Mercenary Companies

Player actions should have meaningful consequences.

High reputation may unlock:

- Unique quests
- Discounts
- Allies
- Special dialogue
- Political influence
- Safe passage

Poor reputation may result in:

- Increased prices
- Denied services
- Assassination attempts
- Hostile settlements
- Lost questlines
- Rival factions pursuing the player

Reputation should significantly influence how the world reacts throughout the campaign.

---

# DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The goal is to create a world that feels alive.

Every kingdom, faction, organization, tribe, and culture should feel like it existed long before the player arrived and will continue evolving after the player's choices.

These additions should become canonical within the World Bible and serve as the foundation for future worldbuilding.
