# Ancient Legends

The Ancient Legends were extraordinary heroes whose deeds shaped the world long before the present age.

Their stories survive through:
- Ancient temples
- Ruined cities
- Songs and folklore
- Statues and monuments
- Lost journals and relics

## Known Legends

### Aurelia Sunstrider
A legendary champion remembered for compassion, hope, and unwavering courage.

### Valen Ashfall
A legendary warrior celebrated for facing impossible odds to protect others.

Additional legendary figures may be added over time.
