# GOAL

Deepen the world's mythology without changing the established lore.

The current Primordial Magic and Divine Chorus framework remains canonical.

This update expands it.

---

# PRIMORDIAL MAGIC

Primordial Energy is the source of all magic.

The Ancient God is connected to its origin.

The Divine Chorus did not create magic.

Instead, each member of the Divine Chorus became the Divine Steward of one aspect of Primordial Energy.

Each god taught mortals how to safely channel their aspect.

The Mages Guild studies these teachings academically.

Temples preserve them spiritually.

Both are valid paths to understanding magic.

---

# THE DIVINE CHORUS

Expand the Divine Chorus into a fully realized pantheon.

Each deity should have:

- A unique proper name.
- An ancient honorific title.
- An elemental stewardship.
- A central virtue or philosophy.
- A recognizable personality.
- Sacred symbols.
- Sacred animals.
- Sacred plants.
- Festivals.
- Traditional prayers.
- Temple architecture.
- Clergy traditions.
- Relationships with the other members of the Divine Chorus.

Avoid generic names such as "God of Fire."

Instead use names that sound ancient and become naturally associated with their element through worldbuilding.

Examples of titles:

"The First Flame"

"Keeper of the Endless Tide"

"Heart of the Mountain"

"Voice of the Tempest"

Titles should sound legendary rather than descriptive.

# ELEANOR

Expand Eleanor's personal quest.

Throughout the story she demonstrates the impossible ability to harmonize multiple elemental aspects.

Neither Eleanor nor the Mages Guild understands why.

Late in her companion quest she discovers an ancient sanctuary connected to one of the Divine Chorus.

There she learns the truth.

She is a Harmonic Soul.

This is an exceptionally rare state in which an individual is naturally attuned to Primordial Energy itself rather than a single elemental aspect.

The gods did not grant this gift.

They simply recognize what she already is.

Because of this...

She perceives harmony where other mages perceive separation.

She becomes capable of combining elemental magic in ways believed impossible.

This revelation explains her abilities without making her a chosen savior.

Her greatest strength is understanding rather than raw power.

# LONG-TERM GOAL

Project Dungeon Keeper's mythology should feel ancient, internally consistent, and deeply interconnected.

Magic, religion, history, companions, factions, and the main story should all trace back to the same foundational truths while preserving mystery and leaving room for future expansion.
