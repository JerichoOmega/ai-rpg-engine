# Main Story Philosophy

## The Ancient God

The central conflict of Project Dungeon Keeper revolves around an ancient god imprisoned long ago by forces now forgotten.

The god remains sealed at the beginning of every campaign.

However, its prison has begun to weaken.

As the seal deteriorates, the god's presence slowly leaks into the world.

This presence is known as **The Corruption**.

---

## The Corruption

The Corruption is not mind control.

It does not force people to become evil.

Instead, it amplifies existing flaws, fears, hatred, greed, ambition, despair, and violence.

Examples include:

- Corrupt rulers becoming tyrants.
- Fearful villages becoming hostile to outsiders.
- Cults hearing whispers from the imprisoned god.
- Forests becoming twisted.
- Wildlife becoming unnaturally aggressive.
- Ancient monsters awakening.
- Magic becoming unstable.
- Ancient ruins stirring once again.

The Corruption accelerates darkness that already exists.

Individuals remain responsible for their choices.

---

## Campaign Variations

Every campaign begins with the same overarching threat:

**The imprisoned god is awakening.**

However, the way The Corruption spreads differs between campaigns.

Examples include:

### Political Corruption

- Kingdoms descend into civil war.
- Nobles become increasingly oppressive.
- Assassinations and betrayals become common.

### Natural Corruption

- Forests become cursed.
- Crops fail.
- Wildlife mutates.
- Entire ecosystems collapse.

### Religious Corruption

- Cults spread rapidly.
- Holy orders fracture.
- Ancient temples awaken.
- Religious conflict erupts.

### Arcane Corruption

- Magic becomes unstable.
- Ancient relics activate.
- Mages lose control of powerful spells.
- Magical disasters become common.

Multiple corruption types may appear during a single campaign, but each playthrough emphasizes different aspects of the world's decline.

---

## Story Philosophy

The ancient god is not simply the final boss.

Its awakening serves as the catalyst for the entire campaign.

The player experiences the consequences of its influence long before discovering its existence.

The mystery surrounding the Corruption should unfold gradually through exploration, quests, companion stories, ancient records, and environmental storytelling.

Most people in the world do not know the true cause of the world's decline.

Each faction has its own explanation, creating political conflict and misinformation throughout the campaign.

---

## Design Philosophy

The main story remains consistent across campaigns:

- The imprisoned god is awakening.
- The world is slowly falling into chaos.
- The player must uncover the truth.

What changes between campaigns is how the Corruption manifests, which factions rise or fall, which companions join the party, regional events, quests, world states, and the consequences of player choices.

This creates a game with a handcrafted central narrative while allowing the AI Dungeon Master to generate meaningful variation across multiple playthroughs.

The goal is for every campaign to feel like a different telling of the same great legend rather than an entirely unrelated story.
