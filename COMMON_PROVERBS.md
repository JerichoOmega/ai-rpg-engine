# COMMON_PROVERBS.md — Sayings of Elyndor

> **Canon Status:** ✅ CANONICAL — established February 2026.
> **Purpose:** A reusable reference of proverbs, sayings, and folk wisdom for NPC dialogue, cultural texture, and companion voice. Each saying is short enough to be spoken by an ordinary person and philosophically consistent with the world's established pillars.
> **Design principle:** proverbs distill philosophy into speakable wisdom. A good proverb sounds older than the person saying it.

---

## 1. Universal Sayings

Spoken across every civilization in some form. These are the world's shared wisdom.

- *"No single voice is the Chorus."*
- *"Leave the world better than you found it."*
- *"What is remembered is never entirely lost."*
- *"Strength exists to protect."*
- *"Every dawn is another chance."*
- *"Every choice carries weight."*
- *"No one thrives alone."*
- *"Life survives because it changes."*
- *"Nothing lasts forever, and that is part of life."*
- *"The Chorus watches. It does not steer."*
- *"Bread broken is bread doubled."*
- *"A closed hand holds nothing. An open hand holds everything worth keeping."*
- *"Balance is not perfection. Balance is refusing any one voice as the whole."*
- *"The Corruption does not create. It amplifies what was already there."*
- *"The line you refuse to cross is what you are."*
- *"Wisdom is knowing which voice to listen to now."*

---

## 2. Human Sayings

Human proverbs tend to be pragmatic, occasionally cynical, with an undercurrent of hope. Frequent Voices: Tyras, Aurelion, Kaelos.

- *"A promise is a small stone. A hundred promises kept build a wall."*
- *"The law that bends is the law that stands. The law that will not bend breaks the people it was written to serve."*
- *"Kings die. Kingdoms turn. The road still needs walking."*
- *"Judge the deed. The person is complicated."*
- *"Better a hard truth than a soft lie."*
- *"The lantern shows the road. The traveler still has to walk it."*
- *"A city is a promise its people keep."*
- *"When the powerful say it is simple, ask who benefits from you not looking."*
- *"Winter reveals what summer hid."*
- *"Trust slowly. Doubt kindly."*
- **Solari Covenant:** *"Solaryn kindled the flame. It is ours to tend."* (Reinforces Herald-vs-Voice confusion; a theologically debated saying.)
- **Frontier folk:** *"Out here, your word is your fortress."*

---

## 3. Goblin Sayings

Goblin proverbs emphasize contribution, community, and unglamorous truth. Frequent Voices: Lyssara, Veridan, Elyra.

- *"Leave the tribe stronger than you found it."*
- *"The young carry our future. The elders carry our memory. A tribe without either cannot endure."*
- *"No contribution is ever truly lost."*
- *"We do not give up on our own. But neither do we sacrifice the many for the one."*
- *"We do not hate them for what they are. We oppose those who choose to prey upon others."*
- *"Debts are how we remember."* (Stonefang.)
- *"Listen twice; speak once."* (Mossroot.)
- *"I am fuel and forge both. What I burn for is what I am."* (Ashfire — Ash Oath closing.)
- *"A tool has three lives: the maker's, the user's, and the mender's. Honor all three."*
- *"Guests eat first. Even enemies under truce."*
- *"A hand that teaches feeds twice."*
- *"The First Hearth is not the fire. It is what we do to keep it burning."*
- **Stonefang:** *"Strength is not isolation. Strength is knowing what is worth protecting and what is worth building."* (New per V-6.)
- **Mossroot:** *"The forest remembers who walked kindly."*
- **Ashfire:** *"What was broken can be rebuilt. What is being rebuilt is still ours."*

---

## 4. Dwarven Sayings

Dwarven proverbs emphasize craft, permanence, and remembered oaths. Frequent Voices: Kaelos, Lyssara, Solenne.

- *"The stone that shapes you is worth its weight in the shaping."*
- *"An oath spoken is an oath owed."*
- *"A hammer knows only the truth of the strike."*
- *"Better to build slowly than to rebuild often."*
- *"The mountain does not forgive haste. Neither should you."*
- *"A tool that outlives its maker is a tool made well."*
- *"Silence in a hall means the work is going well. Do not interrupt it."*
- *"Beard length is not wisdom. Beard length is only time."*
- *"Kin above coin. Craft above kin. Truth above craft."*
- *"The forge does not lie. Neither should we."*
- *"A debt to a dwarf is a debt to the mountain. It waits."*
- *"What we make outlasts us. Make well."*

---

## 5. Elven Sayings

Elven proverbs emphasize time, cycles, and quiet certainty. Frequent Voices: Veridan, Solenne, Noctis.

- *"The tree does not hurry. It arrives."*
- *"What blooms will fall. What falls will feed. What feeds will bloom."*
- *"A century is a season. A season is a breath."*
- *"The wound remembers longer than the sword."*
- *"To hurry the world is to break the world."*
- *"The moon has seen this before."*
- *"Silence is not absence. It is presence you have not yet learned to hear."*
- *"We do not forgive what should not be forgiven. But we let time do its work."*
- *"The young cry that nothing lasts. The old know: everything lasts, but not in the shape you knew it in."*
- *"Grief is love that outlasts the object of love."*

---

## 6. Gnome Sayings

Gnomish proverbs emphasize invention, curiosity, and precise attention. Frequent Voices: Solenne, Lyssara.

- *"The question is the tool. The answer is only the shaving."*
- *"An untested idea is a rumor. A tested idea is a foundation."*
- *"Every mechanism has a purpose. Even the ones that failed."*
- *"Small changes accumulate. So does small carelessness."*
- *"A record is a favor to your future self."*
- *"The elegant solution costs less to maintain."*
- *"Understanding a thing is not the same as owning it."*
- *"A library is a slow conversation across generations."*
- *"Nothing is beneath notice. Only beneath your current attention."*

---

## 7. Halfling Sayings

Halfling proverbs emphasize home, hospitality, and the everyday over the grand. Frequent Voices: Elyra, Aurelion, Lyssara.

- *"A good chair, a full pot, and someone to share both with — anything else is decoration."*
- *"The road always leads home eventually. The trick is enjoying the road."*
- *"An empty pantry is a problem. An empty table is a tragedy."*
- *"There are no small kindnesses."*
- *"Feed strangers well. You may need them to feed you someday."*
- *"A house is a wall. A home is a habit."*
- *"The greatest tale is the one you get to sit down after."*
- *"Big adventures are usually just small errands that got out of hand."*

---

## 8. Beastfolk Sayings

Beastfolk proverbs emphasize instinct, honesty, and the truths the wild teaches. Frequent Voices: Veridan, Noctis.

- *"The wind tells if you are willing to be quiet."*
- *"Every pack is only as strong as the one who watches the edges."*
- *"Blood remembers what the mind forgets."*
- *"The predator that never rests becomes prey."*
- *"To walk quietly is to hear more."*
- *"A wound honestly won heals faster than a wound dishonestly avoided."*
- *"The seasons do not ask permission."*
- *"Trust what your senses say before what your fear says."*

---

## 9. Vampire Sayings

Vampire proverbs are shaped by immortality — burdened by time, philosophically deep, morally ambiguous. Frequent Voices: Kaelos, Solenne, Noctis (interpreted through V-1 through V-4).

- *"To create another immortal is the greatest responsibility a vampire can accept."*
- *"A sire's authority ends when their progeny finds their own Measure. Their responsibility never does."*
- *"Every vampire has a hunger. What defines us is the line we refuse to cross to satisfy it."*
- *"The first thing the Corruption takes is not your strength. It is the line you once refused to cross."*
- *"Centuries teach patience. They do not teach wisdom."*
- *"The past does not release us. It only becomes lighter to carry, or heavier."*
- *"We survived our civilization. Some days that is the only truth worth speaking."*
- *"To forget is a mercy. To remember is a duty. Choose."*
- *"An immortal without a Measure is not an elder. Only an old child."*
- **Vetharis:** *"Power held openly is power with a target on it."*
- **Vetharis:** *"The mask that wears you is the mask you have worn too long."*
- **Drakmor:** *"The world respects strength. Everything else is storytelling."*
- **Drakmor:** *"A death honestly given is not lost."*
- **Soleth:** *"Everything that was written down still exists, if you know where to look."*
- **Soleth:** *"To share a memory is to double it. To hoard one is to end it with you."*

## 10. Regional Sayings

Sayings that emerge from specific geography and history.

### The Frontier

- *"Out here, your name is what you did last week."*
- *"The frontier does not care what you were. It cares what you can do tomorrow."*
- *"Every road ends in a hearth. Find one before dark."*

### Sol Kareth

- *"The sand keeps everything and returns nothing."*
- *"The wise traveler carries less and remembers more."*
- *"What the desert buries, the desert reveals — eventually."*

### The Iron Peaks

- *"The mountain teaches patience by refusing to negotiate."*
- *"Down together. Up together."* (Mining crews.)
- *"There is no shortcut through stone."*

### The Great Forest

- *"The forest is older than the language you speak. Listen accordingly."*
- *"A path is a suggestion the forest makes. It may unmake it."*
- *"The Deep Root remembers every step."* (Old Ways / Mossroot.)

### The Capital Province

- *"In the Capital, everyone knows someone. Learn who."*
- *"The city is a hundred agreements. Break one and hear the rest crack."*
- *"Gold speaks. Silence speaks louder."*

### The Frozen Highlands

- *"Cold kills the careless first. The careful only sometimes."*
- *"A shared fire is a shared life."*
- *"The Highlands owe nothing. Bring what you need."*
