## Canonical facts to keep intact while brainstorming

These are established canon per the Lore Bible and Canon Rules. New brainstorm ideas should not contradict them.

**Goblins are:**
- An intelligent, self-governing race — not chaotic vermin.
- Non-playable but fully realized culturally.
- Native to The Frontier and surrounding regions.

**Three tribes exist:**

- **Stonefang** — raiders and expansionists. Warchief Grakkor. Territorial and confident; the "loud" tribe.
- **Mossroot** — hunters, nature-attuned, conflict-averse. Elder Vess. Notice things the other tribes miss; the "quiet" tribe; early warning system for the Corruption in the natural world.
- **Ashfire** — newly unified, rapidly expanding. Warchief Skarra. The most dangerous of the three because their goals are large and rationally held; the "rising" tribe.

**Cross-tribe canon:**
- The three tribes have distinct philosophies about survival, land, and outsiders.
- No tribe is inherently "the good tribe" or "the villain tribe." Player relationships evolve through choices.
- Reputation is per-tribe (see `docs/systems/reputation.md`) — Fallout: New Vegas-style, no universal goblin score.
- The Corruption affects each tribe differently. The Mossroot notice it first (in wildlife). The Stonefang feel it as increasing tensions with neighbors. The Ashfire ride it — Skarra's expansion is partially opportunistic to the surrounding chaos.

## Suggested brainstorming angles

Feel free to explore any of these — or ignore them and go your own way:

1. **Individual named NPCs per tribe.** The World Bible names leaders but only sketches supporting characters. Who are the mid-tier voices — the hunter who first noticed the hollow game, the young raider who questions Grakkor, the Ashfire diplomat sent to negotiate with the Capital?
2. **Second-quest possibilities.** Each tribe has one authored quest today. What's the *second* quest for each tribe once the player has established reputation?
3. **Tribe-vs-tribe events.** How does the player's alliance with one tribe change the relationships between the other two?
4. **Goblin settlements as playable hubs.** Are any tribe camps large enough to function as safe settlements (per D-16 Settlement Dispersal ruling)? If so, how do companions disperse there? Which companions are welcomed and which are watched?
5. **Corwin ↔ Mossroot.** Corwin is now canonical (Gnome Ranger, Corruption investigator). The Mossroot are the Corruption's early warning in the natural world. What is their pre-game relationship? Has Corwin exchanged information with Elder Vess before the game begins?
6. **The Community Healer ↔ tribal medicine.** She is tied to a refugee settlement. Do refugees include displaced goblins? If yes, how does that shape her worldview?
7. **Ashfire's rise as a Dynamic Story Arc.** Ashfire's expansion could become its own campaign-exclusive arc (like *The Fractured Circle*). What would that look like — political, natural, arcane, or religious in flavor?
8. **What the Corruption whispers to goblins.** Corruption is *amplified virtue*. What virtues do goblins carry that would be dangerous if amplified? (Loyalty → fanaticism? Survival → predation? Belonging → xenophobia?)
9. **The tribe not yet imagined.** Is there a fourth tribe hidden somewhere — nomadic, isolated, or in decline — that the current three don't acknowledge?

## Not-yet-decided (open design questions)

These are places where canon is intentionally light — brainstorm freely.

- **Goblin lifespan, physiology, and reproduction.** Not established.
- **Goblin religion.** Do they share the Divine Chorus faith? Have their own oral traditions? Reject organized religion?
- **Goblin language.** Do they speak a shared tongue across tribes? Do the three tribes have dialects?
- **Trade with non-goblins.** Are there any established trade routes between tribes and the Capital / Frontier merchants?
- **Interaction with the Vampire Houses.** Any documented relationship? None currently.
- **Interaction with the Adventurers Guild.** Do adventurers hunt goblins? Coexist? Recruit from them?
