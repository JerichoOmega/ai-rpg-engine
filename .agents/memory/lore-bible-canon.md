## Immutable Rules (from CANON_RULES.md)

- No chosen one
- No chosen races
- No divine bloodlines
- Heroes are made through choices, not destiny
- History belongs to mortals
- The Chorus does not govern kingdoms, choose rulers, manipulate politics, create heroes, or issue prophecies
- The Chorus intervenes only when reality itself is threatened (the Sundering is the single historical example)
- The Architects never appear, never speak, never become enemies — never over-explain them
- The First Temple's sub-structure is never resolved
- The Final Verse (death's aftermath) is never answered

## Narrative Principles

- World feels ancient, lived-in, historically layered
- History is discovered, not explained
- Mysteries remain mysteries
- Show history through architecture, artifacts, culture — not exposition dumps
- Different cultures interpret history differently
- The world itself is the main character

**Why:** Established by the project owner as the design foundation. Violations require explicit written instruction to override.

**How to apply:** Before creating any new lore, faction, NPC, quest, location, or system — check CANON_RULES.md. If the new content would require the Chorus to act politically, the Architects to be explained, or the First Temple mystery to be resolved, it violates canon.
