# Faction canon reconciliation decisions (user-approved, July 2026)

- **Federal government (canon):** Queen Merveth Aldenmoor stays sovereign of the Capital Province and is a voting member — with no absolute authority — of the **Imperial Council**, the continental representative body (every recognized kingdom, province, race, territory; handles continental law/diplomacy/trade/infrastructure/defense; realms keep internal governance). Framed as revival of the First Council. Canonical doc: `docs/world/factions/GOVERNMENT.md`.
- **Naming rule:** Mortal noble houses and Vampire Houses are strictly separate institutions; name collisions prohibited unless later approved as deliberate story revelations. Draft houses "Soleth" and "Merveth" were renamed to **Corvath** and **Wrenmoor**; Aldenmoor stays royal among the eight founding Capital houses.
- **Vampire taxonomy (canon):** ten houses — three Great Houses (Vetharis, Drakmor, Soleth; operational doc `docs/world/vampire_houses.md` authoritative) + seven Founding Houses (Noctis, Valenor, Draven, Seraphe, Morcant, Kaelor, Vesper) who founded the Crimson Conclave and the Veil. Founding Houses have narrative standing only, no reputation tables.
- **General rule (user's repeated pattern):** existing canon takes precedence; uploaded drafts supplement, never overwrite; conflicts resolved by remapping draft concepts onto canon (e.g., draft "example houses" became Founding Houses, draft's council became the federal layer above the Queen).
- **Why:** the user chose these options explicitly and asked to preserve all existing canon wherever possible.
- **How to apply:** any future uploaded lore draft that names factions or rulers must be reconciled against existing faction/capital canon before writing; prefer renames over retcons.
