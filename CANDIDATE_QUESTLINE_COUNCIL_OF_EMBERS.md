## Concept

Not a main-story quest. A **Legacy Civilization Questline** for the goblins — one that, if completed, permanently changes the world.

The player does not unite the goblin tribes. The player creates the conditions under which the tribes choose to cooperate. Stonefang, Mossroot, and Ashfire remain fully independent civilizations. What they form together is a permanent inter-tribal council — the **Council of Embers** — for matters that transcend any single tribe: the Corruption, shared defense, refugees, trade, ancient sites, diplomacy with other civilizations.

Full concept, per-tribe obstacles, foreshadowing plan, ceremony structure, and environmental storytelling are preserved from the prior analysis and not repeated here — see the conversation log leading into this file for the full design.

## Failure Condition — REVISED (owner directive)

**The Council of Embers cannot fail because of a single dialogue choice, persuasion check, or isolated mistake.** No arbitrary fail state exists.

The questline fails only through **sustained neglect**. If the player never earns the tribes' trust and never engages with goblin content, the Corruption's spread eventually reaches the point where cooperation is no longer possible. Key frontier regions are overrun. Enough Corrupted Goblins now exist that the surviving tribes cannot afford the risk of a shared meeting. The moment passes.

This is not a punishment for a wrong choice. It is a Living World consequence — history moves forward whether the player participates or not.

### Trigger conditions for the failure state

The questline becomes permanently unavailable when **all** of the following are true:

1. The player has not completed any of the three authored tribal questlines by a defined story milestone (recommended: end of Act 2).
2. Corruption pressure in the Frontier region has reached a threshold value where at least one tribe has been forced to relocate or has lost a settlement.
3. Elder Vess (or equivalent Council leadership in another tribe) has died or been Corrupted.

If all three conditions hold, the questline is closed. NPCs across the world begin referencing "what could have been." The abandoned First Convocation site gains a new sapling. Life continues — just with the Council of Embers never formed.

### Consequences of failure

- The Corruption overruns key frontier regions.
- Tribal cooperation is now impossible; the tribes retreat inward.
- The Council of Embers is never formed.
- Goblins receive no representation in the Capital.
- Mixed tribal patrols never appear.
- Old goblin trade routes never reopen.
- The Fourth Flame is never lit.
- Companions and NPCs reference the missed opportunity for the rest of the campaign, but without accusation. History simply took a different path.

## Design Principle Behind the Revision

The player is never punished for choosing "the wrong dialogue." The player is trusted to shape the world through **sustained engagement** — earning trust across tribes, showing up when it matters, treating goblin civilization as worthy of long-term investment.

Failure by inaction feels like real consequence. Failure by dialogue would feel like a gotcha. This project chooses the former.

---

## Success Condition

Success requires **sustained investment**, not any single perfect scene:

- Positive reputation with all three tribes.
- Completion of all three authored tribal questlines: *The Debt Comes Due* (Stonefang), *What the Forest Carries* (Mossroot), *Smoke and Terms* (Ashfire).
- One resolved historical debt from the First Convocation era.
- One preserved tradition from each tribe respected in the player's approach.

When these conditions are met, the questline unlocks. The Fourth Flame Ceremony can be held. Goblin history changes because the player showed up over time.
